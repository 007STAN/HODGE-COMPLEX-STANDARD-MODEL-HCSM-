## Abstract

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) identifies the fourteen modes at the self-paired eigenvalue λ = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the periodic 8×8 torus with one Standard Model generation. The electric charge in units of e/3 is q₃ = F(n₁, σ), where n₁ is the mode's first momentum index and σ = sign(s) is the sign of the zero-state amplitude (HCSM-22).

The central new result of this revision is that the **charge table is derived**, not disclosed. In the previous revision (HCSM-24 Rev. 1), the charge table was a disclosed anchor, reduced to one binary choice. In this revision, the charge table is derived from the **sorting machine**, which uses the N/4 labels of HCSM-23 Rev. 8. Since the N/4 labels are now derived from framework constants (HCSM-23 Rev. 8, Theorem 4.1), the charge table is a theorem of the substrate.

We prove: (i) the bijection property; (ii) the σ-line charge sums 0, +5, −8, summing to the vacuum invariant −1e; (iii) the processor-charge bookkeeping (im T sum −2, ker T sum +1); (iv) the identification with the Standard Model content of one generation; (v) three structural properties of the charge on the 8×8 torus; (vi) the non-existence of a coordinate closed form in nine candidate families; (vii) the sorting-machine mechanism; (viii) the rigidity-class theorem, now derived; (ix) the total-charge rigidity theorem, now derived with zero binary residue.

The electron/neutrino binary choice is resolved by the within-pair ordering rule of HCSM-23 Rev. 8, Corollary 4.5, which is itself derived from the TF operator. The framework has **zero empirical anchors** in the charge sector.

All numerical claims are verified in reproducible Python.

**Keywords:** Hodge Complex Standard Model; HCSM; charge theorem; charge table; sorting machine; N/4 labels; rigidity class; zero empirical anchors.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The charge problem

HCSM-04 identifies the fourteen modes at the self-paired eigenvalue λ = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on Λ = ℤ₈ × ℤ₈ with one Standard Model generation. HCSM-23 Rev. 8 states the identification thesis and derives the N/4 mass labels from framework constants. HCSM-22 constructs the zero state s, the unique nullvector of a 14×19 measurement matrix of vacuum-native observables, and establishes its rs-odd structure.

The present paper addresses the charge. We write

    q₃ = F(n₁, σ),   σ = sign(s),

where n₁ is the mode's first momentum index, s is the zero state of HCSM-22, and q₃ ≡ 3Q is the electric charge in units of e/3.

The questions this paper answers are:
1. Does a closed-form expression for F(n₁, σ) exist?
2. Is the charge assignment structurally unique, or does the framework allow multiple consistent assignments?

The answers are: no closed form exists in nine candidate algebraic families, and the charge assignment is **fully determined** by the sorting machine.

### 1.2 What changed in this revision

In the previous revision (HCSM-24 Rev. 1), the charge table was a disclosed anchor. The rigidity theorem reduced 5¹⁴ assignments to two, and the electron/neutrino binary choice was resolved by the Standard Model content.

In this revision, the charge table is derived from the sorting machine, which uses the N/4 labels of HCSM-23 Rev. 8. Since the N/4 labels are now derived from framework constants, the charge table is a theorem of the substrate. The binary residue is zero.

### 1.3 Main results

1. **(Bijection, Theorem 4.1).** The 14 modes have 14 pairwise distinct (n₁, σ) tuples. The map F is well-defined on the realized pairs and is a bijection onto the five charge eigenvalues {−3, −1, 0, +2, +3}.
2. **(σ-line sums, Theorem 5.1).** The σ-line sums are 0, +5, −8; total Σᵢ q₃ᵢ = −3 = −1e.
3. **(Torus-structural properties, Propositions 6.1–6.3).** Every mode has n₁+n₂ even; the line n₁+n₂ = 4 contains all five charge values exactly once; the line n₁+n₂ ≡ 2 (mod 4) carries the entire vacuum charge −3.
4. **(Processor bookkeeping, Theorem 7.1).** Σ_{j∈im T} Qⱼ = −2, Σ_{j∈ker T} Qⱼ = +1, total −1.
5. **(Non-existence of a coordinate closed form, Theorem 9.1).** No closed-form expression for F(n₁, σ) exists in any of nine candidate algebraic families.
6. **(Sorting-machine mechanism, Theorem 10.1).** The charge table is the residue of a five-step sorting operation on the 14-mode multiplet whose sort key is the N/4 labels.
7. **(Framework-constant σ-line sums, Theorem 10.4).** The σ-line sums are 0, d+1, −2d (not arbitrary integers).
8. **(F-pair sum rules by shell, Theorem 10.5).** Within each |Δn| shell, the F-pair charge sum is a framework-constant linear function of the parent mass rank.
9. **(Rigidity class, Theorem 11.1).** Exactly one assignment of charge values to the 14 modes satisfies all five structural constraints.
10. **(Total charge rigidity, Theorem 11.2).** The unique solution has total charge −1e.
11. **(No binary residue, Corollary 11.3).** The electron/neutrino binary choice is resolved by the within-pair ordering rule of HCSM-23 Rev. 8, Corollary 4.5.

## 5. The σ-line sums

**Theorem 5.1 (σ-line sums).**

    Σ_{σ=−1} q₃ = 0,   Σ_{σ=0} q₃ = +5,   Σ_{σ=+1} q₃ = −8.

*Proof.* For σ = −1 (modes ν, d, s, dark, c, Z): 0 − 1 − 1 + 0 + 2 + 0 = 0. For σ = 0 (modes W, t): 3 + 2 = 5. For σ = +1 (modes e, μ, τ, b, H, u): −3 − 3 − 3 − 1 + 0 + 2 = −8. ∎

**Corollary 5.2 (Vacuum invariant).** Σᵢ q₃ᵢ = 0 + 5 − 8 = −3 = −1e.

**Theorem 5.3 (σ-line sums in framework-constant form).**

with total Σᵢ q₃ᵢ = −(d−1) = −3.

## 10. The sorting-machine interpretation

**Theorem 10.1 (Sorting-machine interpretation).** The charge table q₃ is the residue of the following operation on the 14-mode multiplet:

1. Enumerate modes by descending |s|.
2. Pair modes by the diagonal flip F: (n₁, n₂) ↦ (n₂, n₁).
3. At each amplitude level, assign σ = sign(s).
4. Sort F-pairs by the shell |Δn| = |n₁ − n₂|, then by descending parent N/4.
5. Charge each F-pair so that its charge sum follows the sort rank within the shell.

The operation reproduces the entire charge table exactly.

**Theorem 10.2 (|Δn| = 4 charge-sum sequence).** The four F-pairs with |Δn| = 4 have charge sums −3, −2, −1, 0.

**Theorem 10.3 (|Δn| = 2 charge-sum sequence).** The two F-pairs with |Δn| = 2 have charge sums +1, −3.

**Theorem 10.4 (σ-line sums in framework-constant form).** See Theorem 5.3.

**Theorem 10.5 (F-pair sum rules by shell).** Within each |Δn| shell, the pair charge sum is a framework-constant linear function of the parent's mass rank.

**Theorem 10.6 (Consistency identity).** Σ_pairs (q₃ₘ + q₃F(m)) = −8 = Σ_{σ=+1} q₃.
