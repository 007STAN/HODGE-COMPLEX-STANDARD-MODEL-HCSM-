ABSTRACT
================================================================================

We establish the charge density of the Hodge Complex Standard Model on
the substrate Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The density is

    rho(x,y) = (1/64) sum_{i=1}^{14} q_3_i
               [1 + cos(pi (n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y) / 2)],

where q_3_i is the charge of mode i in units of e/3 (from HCSM-24) and
(n_1^(i), n_2^(i)) is the mode's coordinate. We prove: (i) rho(x,y) is
exactly 4x4 periodic on the 8x8 torus, with fundamental domain given by
an explicit 4x4 matrix; (ii) every row and column of the fundamental
domain sums to -12; (iii) the total charge is -192 = -3 * 64, i.e. -1e
per site. The density is fully derived from the mode coordinates and the
charge table, both of which are established in HCSM-24. No new empirical
inputs are introduced. All numerical claims are verified in reproducible
Python.

1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

HCSM-24 established the charge theorem: the electric charge in units of
e/3 is q_3 = F(n_1, sigma) for the 14 modes at lambda = 4. The present
paper establishes the substrate charge distribution rho(x,y).

The density is defined by the standard Fourier reconstruction:

    rho(x,y) = (1/64) sum_{i=1}^{14} q_3_i
               [1 + cos(pi (n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y) / 2)],           (1)

where the sum runs over the 14 modes, q_3_i is the mode's charge in units
of e/3 (HCSM-24, Theorem 8.1), and (n_1^(i), n_2^(i)) is the mode's
coordinate.

3. THE 4x4 PERIODICITY
================================================================================

Theorem 3.1. rho(x+4, y) = rho(x, y) and rho(x, y+4) = rho(x, y) for
all (x,y) in Lambda.

Proof. The summand is q_3_i [1 + cos(pi(n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y)/2)]. Under
x -> x+4, the cosine argument becomes
pi(n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y)/2 + 2 pi n_1^(i). Since n_1^(i) is in Z, the
shift adds an integer multiple of 2 pi, so the cosine is unchanged. Same
for y -> y+4. [Box]


================================================================================
4. THE FUNDAMENTAL DOMAIN
================================================================================

Theorem 4.1. The fundamental domain of rho is the 4x4 matrix

    rho_4x4 = (1/64) [ -3  -8  +7  -8 ]
                     [ -8  +3  -8  +1 ]
                     [ +7  -8  -3  -8 ]
                     [ -8  +1  -8  +3 ]

Proof. Direct evaluation of (1) on the 16 sites of the 4x4 fundamental
domain. [Box]

Remark. The matrix has a specific structure:
  - Diagonal entries: -3, +3, -3, +3 (alternating).
  - Off-diagonal entries: -8 on most positions, with three special
    off-diagonal entries +7, +1, +1.


================================================================================
5. ROW, COLUMN, AND TOTAL SUMS
================================================================================

Corollary 5.1. Every row and column of the fundamental domain sums to
-12:

    sum_{y=0}^{3} rho_4x4(x, y) = -12,  x = 0, 1, 2, 3.

Proof. Row 0: -3 - 8 + 7 - 8 = -12. Row 1: -8 + 3 - 8 + 1 = -12.
Row 2: +7 - 8 - 3 - 8 = -12. Row 3: -8 + 1 - 8 + 3 = -12. Columns are
identical by symmetry. [Box]

Corollary 5.2. The total charge is

    sum_{x, y = 0}^{7} rho(x, y) = 4 * 4 * (-12) = -192 = -3 * 64
                                  = -1e * 64,

i.e. -1e per site. The value -192 = -3 * 64 is the total charge of the
64-site torus.

8. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

We have established the charge density of the Hodge Complex Standard
Model on the substrate Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The density is 4x4 periodic

The density is fully derived from the mode coordinates and the charge
table of HCSM-24. No new empirical inputs are introduced.
