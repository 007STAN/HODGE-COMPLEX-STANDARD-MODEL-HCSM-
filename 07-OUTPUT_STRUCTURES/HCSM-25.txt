ABSTRACT
--------
We establish the charge density of the Hodge Complex Standard Model
(HCSM) on the substrate Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The density is

    rho(x,y) = (1/64) * sum_{i=1}^{14} q_{3i}
               * cos( pi * (n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y) / 2 ),

where q_{3i} is the electric charge of mode i in units of e/3 (from
HCSM-24) and (n_1^(i), n_2^(i)) is the mode's coordinate (from
HCSM-04). We prove:

  (i)   rho(x,y) is exactly 4x4 periodic on the 8x8 torus, with
        fundamental domain given by an explicit 4x4 matrix;
  (ii)  every row and column of the fundamental domain sums to -12;
  (iii) the total charge is -192 = -3*64, i.e. -1e per site.

The density is fully derived from the mode coordinates (HCSM-04),
the charge table (HCSM-24, itself a theorem), and the substrate
Laplacian eigenfunctions. No new empirical inputs are introduced.
All numerical claims are verified in reproducible Python.

1. INTRODUCTION
=====================================================================

HCSM-24 established the charge theorem: the electric charge in units
of e/3 is q_3 = F(n_1, sigma) for the 14 modes at the self-paired
eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the periodic 8x8
torus. The charge table is a theorem of the substrate: it is the
unique assignment satisfying the five structural constraints (C1)-(C5)
of HCSM-24, with the electron/neutrino binary residue resolved by the
Midpoint Sorting Theorem of HCSM-23 Rev. 8.

The present paper establishes the substrate charge distribution
rho(x,y) associated with this charge table. The density is the
standard Fourier reconstruction of the charge on the 64 sites of the
torus, using the substrate Laplacian eigenfunctions at the self-paired
eigenvalue as the reconstruction basis.

MAIN RESULTS
------------
(i)   4x4 periodicity (Theorem 3.1).
      rho(x+4,y) = rho(x,y) and rho(x,y+4) = rho(x,y)
      for all (x,y) in Lambda.

(ii)  Fundamental domain (Theorem 4.1).
      rho_{4x4} = (1/64) * [[ -3, -8, +7, -8],
                              [ -8, +3, -8, +1],
                              [ +7, -8, -3, -8],
                              [ -8, +1, -8, +3]]

(iii) Row/column sums (Corollary 5.1).
      Every row and column of rho_{4x4} sums to -12.

(iv)  Total charge (Corollary 5.2).
      sum_{x,y=0}^{7} rho(x,y) = -192 = -3*64 = -1e * 64,
      i.e. -1e per site.

2.1 The substrate and the 14-mode multiplet
-------------------------------------------
The substrate is the periodic square lattice Lambda = Z_L x Z_L with
N = L^2 sites, selected at L = 8, N = 64 by the unified substrate
selection theorem of HCSM-00 Section 2.6. Site (x_1,x_2) is identified
with (x_1+8,x_2) and with (x_1,x_2+8).

The discrete 5-point Laplacian

    (Delta f)(x) = 4 f(x) - sum_{|e|=1} f(x+e)

has eigenvalues

    lambda(n_1,n_2) = 4 - 2 cos(pi n_1 / 4) - 2 cos(pi n_2 / 4)

for (n_1,n_2) in {0,...,7}^2. The spectrum is mirror-symmetric under
lambda <-> 8 - lambda, and the unique self-paired eigenvalue is
lambda = 4 with multiplicity 2(L-1) = 14 (HCSM-04, Theorem 3.2).

2.2 The 14 modes and their coordinates
--------------------------------------
In standard mode order (nu, e, u, d, s, mu, dark, c, tau, b, W, Z, H, t),
the mode coordinates are (HCSM-04, Theorem 4.1 and Remark 4.2):

    nu     : (0,4)      e     : (4,0)
    u      : (1,3)      d     : (1,5)
    s      : (3,1)      mu    : (3,7)
    dark   : (5,7)      c     : (7,3)
    tau    : (7,5)      b     : (5,1)
    W      : (2,2)      Z     : (2,6)
    H      : (6,2)      t     : (6,6)

These decompose under the D_4 point group into three orbits
O_0, O_1, O_2 of sizes 2, 8, 4 (HCSM-04, Theorem 5.1).

2.3 The charge table
--------------------
The charge table in units of e/3 is established in HCSM-24 as the
unique solution of the five structural constraints (C1)-(C5):

    q_3(nu)   = 0      q_3(e)    = -3
    q_3(u)    = +2     q_3(d)    = -1
    q_3(s)    = -1     q_3(mu)   = -3
    q_3(dark) = 0      q_3(c)    = +2
    q_3(tau)  = -3     q_3(b)    = -1
    q_3(W)    = +3     q_3(Z)    = 0
    q_3(H)    = 0      q_3(t)    = +2

The total charge is sum_i q_{3i} = -3 = -1e (HCSM-24, Theorem 11.2).

3. THE SUBSTRATE CHARGE DENSITY
=====================================================================

DEFINITION 3.0 (Charge density). The substrate charge density on
Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 is

    rho(x,y) = (1/64) * sum_{i=1}^{14} q_{3i}
               * cos( pi * (n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y) / 2 )

where the sum runs over the 14 modes at lambda = 4, q_{3i} is the
mode's charge in units of e/3, and (n_1^(i), n_2^(i)) is the mode's
coordinate.

REMARK 3.0 (HCSM-native reconstruction). The reconstruction basis in
the definition is the set of real Laplacian eigenfunctions at
lambda = 4 on Lambda,

    phi_{n_1,n_2}(x,y)  proportional to
    cos( pi (n_1 x + n_2 y) / 2 ),

which are HCSM-native objects. The factor of 2 in the denominator
(rather than 8) is fixed by the half-period structure of the lambda = 4
eigenspace: the lambda = 4 modes satisfy n_1 +/- n_2 = 4 (mod 8),
which makes the phase pi(n_1 x + n_2 y)/2 exactly 4x4 periodic. The
normalization 1/64 is the inverse of the number of sites.

=====================================================================
4. THE 4x4 PERIODICITY AND THE FUNDAMENTAL DOMAIN
=====================================================================

THEOREM 3.1 (4x4 periodicity). For all (x,y) in Lambda,

    rho(x+4,y) = rho(x,y)   and   rho(x,y+4) = rho(x,y).

Proof. The summand is q_{3i} cos( pi (n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y) / 2 ).
Under x -> x+4, the cosine argument becomes

    pi (n_1^(i)(x+4) + n_2^(i) y) / 2
        = pi (n_1^(i) x + n_2^(i) y) / 2 + 2 pi n_1^(i).

Since n_1^(i) is an integer, the shift is an integer multiple of 2 pi,
and the cosine is unchanged. The same argument applies to y -> y+4.
                                                              QED

COROLLARY 3.2 (Reduction to a 4x4 cell). The density rho is
completely determined by its values on the 4x4 fundamental domain
{0,1,2,3}^2.
