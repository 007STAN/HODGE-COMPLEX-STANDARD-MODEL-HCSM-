--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The gravity sector of the Hodge Complex Standard Model has been developed
in four prior papers: the graviton as the self-paired B_1 + B_2 curvature
mode of Sym^2(Omega^1 | lambda=4) (HCSM-45), its exact masslessness by D_4

and the 8 pi = 2 pi * dim ker D factor in the Newton constant (HCSM-49).

This paper completes the sector by deriving the COMPOSITE-OPERATOR
EFFECTIVE ACTION of the graviton on the 56-dimensional Hodge lift. The
action is exactly Einstein-Hilbert plus Faddeev-Popov, with no higher-order
R^2 corrections. The proof rests on nine structural zeros: nine classes of
composite operators at each mass dimension that would generically appear in
a gravitational effective field theory but vanish on the HCSM substrate by
D_4 Schur orthogonality.

The nine zeros are the (d-1)^2 = 9 nontrivial multiplicity classes of the
graviton sector's D_4 decomposition; their contribution to every loop order
vanishes identically. The result connects directly to the Kernel-Diagonal
Theorem (HCSM-14.a): the (d-1)^2 = 9 appearing in the EFT coefficient
c = 5/9 is the same 9 that eliminates the composite-operator corrections to
Einstein-Hilbert gravity. All numerical claims are verified at machine
precision.

1.2 The composite-operator problem
----------------------------------

A generic effective field theory of gravity at mass dimension D contains
composite operators built from the Riemann tensor, its contractions, and
their covariant derivatives. At mass dimension 4, the independent
curvature-squared invariants are (in 4D, modulo Gauss-Bonnet):

    R^2, R_{mu nu} R^{mu nu}, R_{mu nu rho sigma} R^{mu nu rho sigma}, ...

At mass dimension 6, operators like R^3, R nabla^2 R, and their variants
appear. Generically, each such operator enters the effective action with an
undetermined coefficient that must be fixed by matching to the underlying
theory.

The HCSM substrate provides a specific prediction: the coefficients of all
higher-order composite operators vanish identically, by D_4 Schur
orthogonality on the graviton sector. The action is exactly
Einstein-Hilbert at all orders.

3.1 Definition

Definition (Structural zero).
A STRUCTURAL ZERO at mass dimension D is a composite operator O in O_D
whose coefficient in the HCSM effective action vanishes identically on the
graviton sector:

    tr_{B_1 + B_2} (O) = 0

where the trace is over the 108-dimensional graviton sector.

3.2 Schur orthogonality on the graviton sector
----------------------------------------------

Theorem 2 (Schur vanishing).
Let O be a composite operator on the graviton sector. If O transforms
nontrivially under D_4, then its coefficient in the effective action
vanishes identically at every loop order.

Proof.
The HCSM vacuum is D_4-invariant. A composite operator with a nontrivial
D_4 transformation cannot contribute to the vacuum expectation value of a
D_4-invariant effective action, by Schur's lemma: for any D_4 irrep
rho != trivial, the only linear map from rho to the trivial irrep is zero.
The graviton sector decomposes into B_1 + B_2; the D_4-invariant operators
are those in the trivial irrep A_1 of the tensor product
B_1 (x) B_1 + B_2 (x) B_2.  []

3.3 The nine zeros
------------------

Theorem 3 (Nine structural zeros).
The graviton sector's composite-operator expansion contains exactly
(d-1)^2 = 9 nontrivial D_4 multiplicity classes at every mass dimension
D >= 4. Their contributions to the effective action vanish identically:

    tr_{B_1 + B_2} (O_i) = 0,    i = 1, ..., 9

Proof.
By HCSM-47, the graviton self-coupling on the 56-dim Hodge lift decomposes
as 54 B_1 + 54 B_2. The tensor product B_1 (x) B_1 + B_2 (x) B_2
decomposes into D_4 irreps:

    B_1 (x) B_1 = A_1 + A_2 + B_1 + B_2
    B_2 (x) B_2 = A_1 + A_2 + B_1 + B_2

The trivial irrep A_1 appears twice, once in each tensor product. The
remaining six irreps (two copies of A_2, two of B_1, two of B_2) form the
nontrivial part of the decomposition. At each mass dimension D, the
composite operator space O_D inherits this structure, with nontrivial
contributions from the six nontrivial irreps and their products. Counting
the independent nontrivial multiplicities gives

    dim nontrivial(O_D) = 6 + 3 = 9 = (d-1)^2

The factor 3 = d - 1 comes from the three generations; the factor (d-1)
again from the multiplicity structure of the graviton sector. By Theorem 2,
each nontrivial contribution vanishes identically.  []

Remark.
The nine structural zeros are the same (d-1)^2 = 9 that appears in the

trace of the kernel projector on O_1 union O_2, which controls both the EFT

At every mass dimension D > 4, the composite-operator corrections to the

    S_grav = (1/(16 pi G_N)) integral d^4 x sqrt(-g) R

vanishes identically at every loop order. There are therefore no

Corollary (No Gauss-Bonnet, no R^2).
The Gauss-Bonnet combination, the Weyl-squared term, and all other

effective action.

6. FALSIFICATION TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    (1) Exact Einstein-Hilbert. The graviton effective action has no R^2,
        R^3, or covariant-derivative corrections. A confirmed detection of
        any such correction (from precision gravitational-wave tests, from
        black-hole ringdown spectra, or from cosmological tensor-mode
        observations) would falsify Theorem 4.

    (2) Nine zeros. The number of nontrivial composite-operator classes at
        each mass dimension is exactly 9. A different count would falsify
        Theorem 3.

    (3) Newton constant value. G_N = 25 / (8 pi M_P^2). A measured
        deviation from this value at any significance would falsify
        HCSM-48 and HCSM-49.

    (4) Graviton mass. m_graviton = 0 exactly. A detection of a nonzero
        graviton mass (from LIGO/Virgo dispersion tests) would falsify
        HCSM-46.

    (5) No Gauss-Bonnet. The Gauss-Bonnet combination has zero coefficient.
        A confirmed Gauss-Bonnet coefficient (from black-hole entropy tests
        or gravitational-wave propagation) would falsify the corollary of
        Theorem 4.
