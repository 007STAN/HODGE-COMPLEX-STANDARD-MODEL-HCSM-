ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge Complex Standard Model identifies the graviton as the self-paired
B_1 + B_2 curvature mode of the symmetric tensor square Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4)
on the periodic 8 x 8 torus (HCSM-45). This paper proves that the graviton is
exactly massless at all orders, with no finite-N, one-loop, or non-perturbative
correction. The proof is a Schur-orthogonality argument: any mass operator on
the lambda = 4 sector that couples through the trace epsilon T_mu^mu (with
epsilon = D - 4 a Lorentz scalar) transforms in the A_1 isotypic
representation of D_4, while the graviton transforms in B_1 + B_2; the two
irreps are distinct, so the mass matrix element vanishes identically. This is
the discrete analogue of the continuum statement that diffeomorphism
invariance forbids a graviton mass. The paper also retracts two results from
the earlier WIN Gravity Theorem: the finite-N graviton mass estimate (an
imported effective-field-theory ansatz, whose own arithmetic also exceeds the
LIGO bound by 13 orders of magnitude) and the Kaluza-Klein interpretation of
the first excited graviton level at 1.52 TeV (a category error, since the
lattice is not a compactified spatial dimension). All numerical claims are
verified at machine precision.

1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 The graviton mass question

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) identifies the graviton as the
self-paired B_1 + B_2 curvature mode of the symmetric tensor square
Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4) on the periodic 8 x 8 torus (HCSM-45). The graviton
sector has dimension 108 and decomposes as 54 B_1 + 54 B_2; its polarization
space is V = B_1 + B_2, of dimension 2.

The question addressed by this paper is whether the graviton is exactly
massless, or whether it acquires a mass from the substrate's dynamics. In
continuum general relativity, the graviton mass is forbidden by
diffeomorphism invariance. In the HCSM substrate, the analogue of
diffeomorphism invariance is the D_4 point-group symmetry of the lattice;
the question is whether this symmetry forbids a graviton mass exactly.

The answer is yes. The proof is a Schur-orthogonality argument on the D_4
isotypic decomposition: any mass operator on the lambda = 4 sector that
couples through the trace epsilon T_mu^mu transforms as A_1, while the
graviton transforms as B_1 + B_2; the mass matrix element vanishes
identically.


1.2 The one-line summary

The graviton is exactly massless at all orders because its D_4 isotypic sector
B_1 + B_2 is orthogonal to the A_1 sector that hosts any trace-coupled mass
operator. No perturbative or non-perturbative correction generates a graviton
mass.

  - Structure of the mass operator (Section 3). Any mass operator on the

  - Falsifiable predictions (Section 6). Exact masslessness, no fifth force,

2. SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 The graviton sector

By HCSM-45, the graviton is the symmetrized self-paired bilinear
h = omega tensor omega in the substrate 1-form omega in Omega^1, restricted to
the lambda = 4 sector and projected onto the B_1 + B_2 isotypic piece of the
symmetric tensor square Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4). The symmetric square
decomposes as

    Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4) = 60 A_1 + 46 A_2 + 54 B_1 + 54 B_2 + 96 E,

of total dimension 406. The graviton sector is

    V_grav = 54 B_1 + 54 B_2,    dim V_grav = 108.

Its polarization space is V = B_1 + B_2, of dimension 2, with D_4 character
(2, 2, -2, 0, 0) on the five conjugacy classes.


2.2 The D_4 character table and irreps

The D_4 point group has five irreps: two 1-dimensional real irreps A_1, A_2;
two 1-dimensional real irreps B_1, B_2; and one 2-dimensional real irrep E.
The character table on conjugacy classes {e}, {r^2}, {r,r^3}, {s,r^2 s},
{rs,r^3 s} with class sizes (1, 1, 2, 2, 2):

    irrep | e   r^2   r    s    rs
    ------+----------------------
    A_1   | 1    1    1    1     1
    A_2   | 1    1    1   -1    -1
    B_1   | 1    1   -1    1    -1
    B_2   | 1    1   -1   -1     1
    E     | 2   -2    0    0     0

The five irreps are pairwise non-isomorphic. In particular, A_1 and
B_1 + B_2 are distinct.


2.3 The isotypic decomposition of the lambda = 4 sector

The full 56-dimensional Hodge sector at lambda = 4 carries the D_4 content

    Omega^0|lambda=4 + Omega^1|lambda=4 + Omega^2|lambda=4
        = 9 A_1 + 5 A_2 + 7 B_1 + 7 B_2 + 14 E

(HCSM-02, HCSM-05). Any D_4-invariant operator on this space decomposes
block-diagonally with respect to the isotypic decomposition; by Schur's
lemma, it cannot mix distinct isotypic sectors.

3. STRUCTURE OF THE MASS OPERATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 The trace coupling

Proposition 3.1 (Mass operator structure). Any mass operator on the
lambda = 4 sector that couples through the trace epsilon T_mu^mu (with
epsilon = D - 4 a Lorentz scalar and T_mu^mu the trace of the stress-energy
tensor) transforms in the A_1 isotypic representation of D_4.

Proof. The Lorentz scalar epsilon = D - 4 is invariant under D_4; it
transforms in the trivial representation A_1. The trace T_mu^mu of the
stress-energy tensor is also a Lorentz scalar and therefore also invariant
under D_4; it transforms in A_1. Their product epsilon T_mu^mu transforms as
the tensor product A_1 tensor A_1 = A_1.

For a metric perturbation h_mu nu around the flat torus, the trace h_mu^mu
is the Lorentz-scalar part of h_mu nu; it transforms in A_1 (see, e.g., the
standard decomposition of a symmetric 2-tensor into trace,
traceless-symmetric, and antisymmetric parts). Any mass term that couples the
metric perturbation to the trace epsilon T_mu^mu is therefore of the form

    m^2 (h_mu^mu)^2 + ...,

where all terms in the ellipsis transform in A_1 by the same argument.

Hence the mass operator M lies in the A_1 isotypic sector of
Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4). []

Remark 3.2. The statement is structural: it does not depend on which specific
microscopic mechanism generates the coupling epsilon T_mu^mu. As long as the
coupling is a Lorentz scalar times the trace of the stress-energy tensor, the
mass operator transforms in A_1.


3.2 The graviton sector

Proposition 3.3 (Graviton sector). The graviton sector is
V_grav = 54 B_1 + 54 B_2, which lies entirely in the B_1 + B_2 isotypic
sector of Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4).

Proof. Direct from HCSM-45 Theorem 3.4 and Corollary 4.2: the B_1 and B_2
multiplicities in Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4) are both 54. []

4. EXACT MASSLESSNESS OF THE GRAVITON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Schur orthogonality

Theorem 4.1 (Graviton Exact Masslessness). The graviton is exactly massless
at all orders in perturbation theory and at all orders in 1/N:

    m_graviton = 0.

Proof. By Proposition 3.1, the mass operator M lies in the A_1 isotypic
sector of D_4. By Proposition 3.3, the graviton lies in the B_1 + B_2
isotypic sector. The irreps A_1 and B_1, B_2 are distinct irreducible
representations of D_4 (Section 2.2).

By Schur's lemma, any D_4-invariant operator is block-diagonal with respect to
the isotypic decomposition. Hence

    <A_1, B_1 + B_2>_{D_4} = 0.

The mass operator therefore annihilates every mode in B_1 + B_2, and the
graviton mass vanishes.

The argument is exact: it does not rely on smallness of any coupling, on a
perturbative expansion, or on a finite-N suppression. Since the D_4 symmetry
is an exact symmetry of the discrete torus, the Schur orthogonality is exact
at all orders. []

Remark 4.2 (Discrete analogue of diffeomorphism invariance). In continuum
general relativity, the graviton mass is forbidden by diffeomorphism
invariance, in the same way the photon mass is forbidden by gauge invariance.
On the discrete torus, the analogue of diffeomorphism invariance is the D_4
symmetry, which is an exact symmetry of the lattice. Schur orthogonality of
the A_1 and B_1 + B_2 sectors is the discrete counterpart of the statement
that a mass term m^2 h_mu nu h^mu nu is forbidden by diffeomorphism
invariance.


4.2 Absence of finite-N corrections

Proposition 4.3 (No finite-N correction). No finite-N correction to the
graviton mass exists in the current HCSM architecture.

Proof. The earlier WIN-era Gravity Theorem proposed a finite-N correction
based on the SYK kernel. The SYK/QIN layer was removed from the HCSM
architecture in HCSM-00 Section 9: direct computation of the infrared
structure of the Omega^1 fermionic sector establishes that site-local
D_4-invariant quartic interactions do not flow to SYK, because momentum
conservation at each vertex prevents melonic routing. The correct infrared
description of Omega^1 is a (2+1)-dimensional local fermionic theory, not SYK.

There is no SYK kernel in the HCSM architecture. Any correction based on the
SYK kernel is therefore not defined within HCSM. The remaining interactions
(the D_4-invariant quartic phi^4 / omega^4 terms of HCSM-18 and the
Omega^1 / Omega^0 / Omega^2 interactions) are D_4-invariant; by the same
Schur orthogonality argument as Theorem 4.1, none of them can generate a mass
term in A_1 tensor (B_1 + B_2) = (A_1 tensor B_1) + (A_1 tensor B_2)
= B_1 + B_2, which is not equal to A_1. []

Remark 4.4 (No generic quantum correction). In a generic effective field
theory, quantum corrections generate masses of order the cutoff, suppressed by
powers of the cutoff scale. The HCSM framework is not a generic EFT: it is a
finite theory on a finite lattice with an exact D_4 symmetry. There is no
continuum limit to take, no UV divergences to cancel, and no anomalous
dimensions to run. The symmetry is exact, and it protects the graviton mass
exactly.

5. RETRACTIONS FROM THE WIN GRAVITY THEOREM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Retraction of the finite-N graviton mass estimate

The WIN-era Gravity Theorem proposed a finite-N correction of the form

    m_graviton^2 <~ delta_edge^2 M_P^2 exp(-2 kL) ~ 10^{-74} M_P^2.

This estimate is retracted for three independent reasons:

(1) Arithmetic error. With delta_edge = 0.012143 and exp(-2 kL) = 4.067e-34,
the formula gives

    delta_edge^2 * exp(-2 kL) = 6.0e-38,

not 1e-74. The paper's stated value disagrees with its own formula by a factor
of 10^36.

(2) Observational exclusion. Even at face value, the claimed 1e-74 M_P^2
corresponds to m_graviton ~ 1e-9 eV, which exceeds the LIGO/Virgo bound
m_graviton < 1.2e-22 eV by 13 orders of magnitude.

(3) Structural error. The formula is a scalar ansatz: it would give mass to
every D_4 irrep equally, including the A_1 trace mode, which is the mass
sector itself. This contradicts the block-diagonal structure of D_4-invariant
operators (Schur's lemma).

The estimate is replaced by Theorem 4.1.


5.2 Retraction of the Kaluza-Klein resonance interpretation

The WIN-era Gravity Theorem interpreted the first excited graviton level
E_sum = 1.1716 (in lattice units) as a Kaluza-Klein resonance at 1.52 TeV.
This interpretation is retracted.

Proposition 5.1 (No Kaluza-Klein interpretation). The Kaluza-Klein
interpretation of the WIN Gravity Theorem is a category error: it imports the
assumption that the lattice is a compactified spatial dimension, which
contradicts the framework's central result dim ker D = 4.

Proof. The Kaluza-Klein interpretation assumes a compactified spatial
dimension of size R and identifies the excited levels with momenta n/R. In
HCSM, the lattice Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 is not a compactified spatial dimension:
it is the pre-geometric substrate from which spacetime emerges (HCSM-03). The
four-dimensional structure of spacetime is ker D, of dimension 4 (HCSM-02
Proposition 2.1). If the lattice were itself a spatial dimension, spacetime
would be five-dimensional rather than emergent, contradicting HCSM-02. []

Remark 5.2 (The 1.52 TeV number). Independent of the category error, the
arithmetic of the WIN-era paper does not close. The naive conversion of
E_sum = 1.1716 from lattice to physical units gives

    E_phys = 1.1716 * (M_P_phys / M_P_lattice) ~ 5.5e13 TeV,

not 1.52 TeV. Reproducing 1.52 TeV requires a warp suppression exp(-alpha kL)
with alpha = 0.812, which is not a natural exponent. The 1.52 TeV number does
not emerge from any HCSM-native conversion.

6. CONSEQUENCES FOR GRAVITATIONAL PHENOMENOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The HCSM framework's gravitational predictions are now:

  - Exactly massless graviton: m_graviton = 0 (Theorem 4.1).
  - Standard GR at sub-Planckian scales.
  - No fifth force, no scalar graviton.
  - No Kaluza-Klein tower.

The absence of a KK tower is a sharp prediction: any detection of a discrete
graviton excitation spectrum at accessible energies would falsify the
framework.

9. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This paper has established the exact masslessness of the graviton in the
Hodge Complex Standard Model.

The graviton is the self-paired B_1 + B_2 curvature mode of the symmetric
tensor square Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4), with 54 B_1 + 54 B_2 = 108 modes. Any
trace-coupled mass operator on the lambda = 4 sector transforms in the A_1
isotypic representation of D_4. Since A_1 and B_1 + B_2 are distinct irreps
of D_4, Schur orthogonality gives m_graviton = 0 at all orders. The
masslessness is exact, not approximate: it holds at all orders in perturbation
theory and at all orders in 1/N, with no finite-N, one-loop, or
non-perturbative correction.

The masslessness is the discrete analogue of the continuum statement that
diffeomorphism invariance forbids a graviton mass. The exactness of the D_4
symmetry (unlike the approximate symmetry of an effective field theory) makes
the masslessness exact.

Two results of the earlier WIN-era Gravity Theorem are retracted. The
finite-N graviton mass estimate was an imported effective-field-theory
ansatz, and its own arithmetic also disagrees with the LIGO bound by 13 orders
of magnitude. The Kaluza-Klein resonance interpretation of the first excited
graviton level imported the assumption that the lattice is a compactified
spatial dimension, which contradicts the framework's central result
dim ker D = 4.

The corrected framework predicts an exactly massless graviton, no fifth force,
no scalar graviton, and no Kaluza-Klein tower, all consistent with
observation.
