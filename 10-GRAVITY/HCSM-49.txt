5.1 Retraction of §VI.E (finite-N graviton mass correction)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The WIN Gravity Theorem [WIN-Gravity] proposed

    m_graviton^2 <= delta_edge^2 M_P^2 e^{-2 kL} ~ 10^{-74} M_P^2.   (16)

This estimate is retracted for three independent reasons:

1. Arithmetic error. With delta_edge = 0.012143 and
   e^{-2 kL} = 4.067 x 10^{-34}, the formula gives

       delta_edge^2 * e^{-2 kL} = 6.0 x 10^{-38},                   (17)

   not 10^{-74}. The paper's stated value disagrees with its own formula
   by a factor of 10^{36}.

2. Observational exclusion. Even at face value, the claimed 10^{-74} M_P^2
   corresponds to m_graviton ~ 10^{-9} eV, which exceeds the LIGO/Virgo
   bound m_graviton < 1.2 x 10^{-22} eV by 13 orders of magnitude.

   D4-invariant operators (Schur's lemma).

The estimate is replaced by the Graviton Exact Masslessness Theorem
(Theorem 4.1).
