Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The framework's central claim is that the Standard

  "Physics is what the machine cannot cancel. The Hodge complex is the
   machine."

HCSM does not ask what fields exist or what symmetry groups are broken.
It asks a single question: what substrate could produce exactly the
Standard Model, and nothing else? The answer is a discrete lattice

2.2 The 5-point Laplacian is forced
------------------------------------

THEOREM 2.2.1 (Laplacian uniqueness). Let Delta : C^N -> C^N be a linear
operator on the substrate Lambda_L = Z_L x Z_L satisfying:

  (1) D_4-invariance: [R, Delta] = 0 for every R in D_4.
  (2) Nearest-neighbour locality: (Delta f)(x) depends only on f(x) and
      f at the four nearest neighbours of x.
  (3) Positivity and kernel: Delta is positive-semidefinite and
      ker Delta is exactly the set of constant functions.

Then Delta is uniquely determined up to an overall positive scale, and
equals the 5-point Laplacian

    (Delta f)(x_1, x_2)
      = 4 f(x_1, x_2)
      - f(x_1+1, x_2) - f(x_1-1, x_2)
      - f(x_1, x_2+1) - f(x_1, x_2-1).                            (2.2)

Proof. Condition (2) writes Delta f(x) = a_0 f(x) + sum_mu a_mu
f(x + mu). The four nearest-neighbour directions +-e_1, +-e_2 lie in a
single D_4 orbit (the reflection s maps e_1 -> -e_1; the rotation r maps
e_1 -> e_2). Hence condition (1) forces a_mu = a for all four directions,
giving Delta f(x) = a_0 f(x) + a sum_mu f(x + mu). Condition (3) forces
Delta 1 = 0, hence a_0 + 4a = 0, so a_0 = -4a. Normalizing a = 1 gives
(2.2). QED.

REMARK 2.2.2. The 5-point Laplacian annihilates the constants by

3.1 The three form degrees
--------------------------

On the 2-torus Lambda_L, the Hodge complex of differential forms has
three graded pieces:

    Omega^0 = R^N       (0-forms on the N sites),          dim = N,
    Omega^1 = R^{2N}    (1-forms on the 2N directed edges), dim = 2N,
    Omega^2 = R^N       (2-forms on the N plaquettes),     dim = N.

The total dimension is

    dim(Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2) = N + 2N + N = 4N.

At N = 64 this gives 4 . 64 = 256.

3.2 The exterior derivative and the codifferential
--------------------------------------------------

Let e_1 = (1, 0) and e_2 = (0, 1) be the two oriented lattice
directions. The exterior derivative acts on 0-forms by

    (d_0 f)(x, mu) = f(x + e_mu) - f(x),    mu in {1, 2},          (3.1)

and on 1-forms by

    (d_1 omega)(x) = omega_1(x) + omega_2(x + e_1)
                   - omega_1(x + e_2) - omega_2(x).                (3.2)

These satisfy d_1 d_0 = 0 exactly.

The codifferential d* = d^dagger is defined as the adjoint with respect
to the standard Euclidean inner product. Taking d_0* = d_0^T and
d_1* = d_1^T as matrices gives

    (d_0* omega)(x) = sum_{mu=1}^{2} [omega_mu(x - e_mu)
                                     - omega_mu(x)],               (3.3)

    (d_1* chi)(x, 1) = chi(x) - chi(x - e_2),
    (d_1* chi)(x, 2) = chi(x - e_1) - chi(x).                      (3.4)

The sign convention is fixed by the requirement that d_0* and d_1* are
the transposes of d_0 and d_1. With this convention d_0* d_1* = 0
follows from d_1 d_0 = 0.

REMARK 3.2.1 (Sign convention). The formula (3.3) differs by an overall
minus sign from sum_mu [omega_mu(x) - omega_mu(x - e_mu)]. The choice
here is fixed by requiring d* to be the matrix transpose of d; with the
opposite sign, D^2 on Omega^0 equals -Delta rather than +Delta.

3.3 The Hodge-Dirac operator
-----------------------------

DEFINITION 3.3.1 (Hodge-Dirac operator). The Hodge-Dirac operator is

    D = d + d*,                                                    (3.5)

acting on the full Hodge complex Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2. In block
form,

         (  0     d_0*    0   )
    D =  (  d_0    0     d_1* )                                   (3.6)
         (  0     d_1     0   )

a symmetric 256 x 256 real operator.

THEOREM 3.3.2 (Hodge-Dirac square). On the discrete torus Lambda, the
identity

    D^2|_Omega^k = Delta . Id_{Omega^k}                            (3.7)

holds exactly on each form degree k = 0, 1, 2.

Proof. We give the computation on Omega^0 explicitly. The computations
on Omega^1 and Omega^2 follow the same structure, with cross terms
removed by nilpotency d_1 d_0 = 0.

Case k = 0. On Omega^0, D^2 = d_0* d_0. Using (3.1) and (3.3), for any
function f,

    (d_0* d_0 f)(x)
      = sum_{mu=1}^{2} [ (d_0 f)_mu(x - e_mu) - (d_0 f)_mu(x) ]
      = sum_{mu=1}^{2} [ (f(x) - f(x - e_mu))
                       - (f(x + e_mu) - f(x)) ]
      = sum_{mu=1}^{2} [ 2 f(x) - f(x - e_mu) - f(x + e_mu) ]
      = 4 f(x) - sum_{|e|=1} f(x + e),

which is Delta f exactly.

Case k = 1. On Omega^1, D^2 = d_0 d_0* + d_1* d_1. The cross-terms
d_1 d_0 = 0 ensure no mixed-degree contributions appear. Direct
expansion of both terms in components gives
(d_0 d_0* + d_1* d_1) omega = Delta . omega, acting component-wise on
(omega_1, omega_2).

Case k = 2. On Omega^2, D^2 = d_1 d_1*. Using the plaquette-boundary
structure of d_1 and the adjoint (3.4), a direct expansion gives
(d_1 d_1* chi)(x) = 4 chi(x) - sum_{|e|=1} chi(x + e) = Delta chi on
each plaquette.

All three identities are verified at machine precision. QED.

REMARK 3.3.3 (No species doubling). The Hodge-Dirac operator on the
discrete torus has no species doubling: its square equals the scalar
Laplacian on each form degree, and therefore its spectrum contains one
mode per eigenvalue of Delta (up to the Omega^1 doubling of
multiplicity). The low-energy spectrum is bounded away from zero by

from the spinorial Dirac operator on the same lattice, whose square is

    mu_min(L) = 2 sin(pi / L).                                     (3.12)

12. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This paper has established the foundation of the Hodge Complex Standard
Model. The substrate is the periodic 8x8 torus Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8,
selected by two independent derivations: equal entropy spacing on the
torus Laplacian, and maximal cancellation of the winding generator at
L = 8. The substrate carries the Hodge complex
Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2 with D^2 = Delta exactly on each form
degree. The 14 modes at the self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 are
identified with one Standard Model generation. The processor T and its
five-layer loop are the machine's operation. The output structures
(charge, mass, flavor, gravity, cosmology) are the residue after mirror
cancellation.

The framework's most consequential structural results are three.
First, the Kernel-Diagonal Theorem unifies the framework's three
leading coefficients under a single projector: the EFT coefficient
c = 5/9, the mass-map coefficient (d-1)/2 = 3/2, and the kernel
dimension 10 = (d-1)^2 + 1 are three traces of the diagonal of the
projector onto ker(H_wind). Second, the Lambda-promotion mechanism is
closed: the residual vacuum energy is promoted by pure projection onto
the timelike direction of ker D, and the hidden u(1) VEVs vanish.
Third, the framework's coverage of the Standard Model gauge content
(absence of a light dark photon, spin-chirality Z_2 grading,
strong-CP solution, Proca sector, topological protection of phi_gen)
is complete.

The framework has a single dimensionful anchor (the Planck mass M_P)
and derives every other quantity from the substrate at d = 4 and
L = 8.

The framework's claim is that the Standard Model is what the substrate
looks like when you observe it. The Hodge complex is the machine.
Physics is what the machine cannot cancel.
