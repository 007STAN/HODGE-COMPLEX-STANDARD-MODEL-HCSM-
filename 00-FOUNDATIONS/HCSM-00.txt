1.1 What HCSM is
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge Complex Standard Model is a structural derivation of the Standard
Model of particle physics from a single discrete substrate: the periodic
8x8 torus Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The framework's central claim is that the
Standard Model is not a fundamental description of nature, but the residue
left behind when a small, discrete, information-bearing lattice cancels
itself against its own mirror.

    Physics is what the machine cannot cancel. The Hodge complex is the
    machine.

HCSM does not ask what fields exist or what symmetry groups are broken. It
asks a single question: what substrate could produce exactly the Standard
Model, and nothing else? The framework's answer is a discrete lattice whose
mirror cancellation leaves exactly the observed particle content.

1.2 The single input d = 4
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The whole structure of HCSM traces back to a single input: the spacetime
dimension d = 4. This input is itself derived from the photon's two helicity
states, and the substrate size L = 8 is selected independently by the
equal-entropy-spacing theorem on the torus Laplacian. The two derivations
agree, and together they force the substrate and the emergent dimension.

Level 0. Substrate. The periodic 8x8 torus Lambda, the discrete 5-point

Level 3. Processor and dynamics. The winding generator H_wind, the three

Level 5. Emergent physics. Flavor (CKM, PMNS), the Higgs sector, gravity,

Level 6. Cosmology. The kL crossover, dark energy, the DME entropy-well
