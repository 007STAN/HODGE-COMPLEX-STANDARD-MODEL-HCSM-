Standard Model of particle physics from a single discrete substrate:
the periodic 8x8 torus Lambda = Z8 x Z8. The framework's central claim

HCSM does not ask what fields exist or what symmetry groups are broken.

lattice whose mirror cancellation leaves exactly the observed particle
content.

2.2 The discrete 5-point Laplacian
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each site (x, y) has four nearest neighbors: (x +/- 1, y) and
(x, y +/- 1), with all coordinates modulo 8. The natural differential
operator on the lattice is the discrete 5-point Laplacian

    (Delta f)(x, y) = 4 f(x, y) - f(x+1, y) - f(x-1, y)
                                - f(x, y+1) - f(x, y-1).

The normalization 4 in front of f(x, y) is chosen so that the constant
function f(x, y) = 1 is annihilated by the Laplacian: Delta 1 = 0.

The eigenvalues of Delta on plane waves
phi_{n1,n2}(x, y) = e^{2 pi i (n1 x + n2 y) / 8} are

    lambda(n1, n2) = 4 - 2 cos(pi n1 / 4) - 2 cos(pi n2 / 4),
    n1, n2 in {0, 1, ..., 7}.

The spectrum is mirror-symmetric under lambda <-> 8 - lambda, with 13
distinct eigenvalues.

3.2 Exterior derivative and codifferential
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For a 0-form f and a 1-form omega = (omega_1, omega_2), the exterior
derivatives are

    (d0 f)(x, mu) = f(x + e_mu) - f(x),
    (d1 omega)(x) = omega_2(x + e_1) - omega_2(x)
                    - omega_1(x + e_2) + omega_1(x).

The codifferential d* = d^dagger is the adjoint with respect to the
standard inner products. They satisfy exact nilpotency:
d1 d0 = 0.
