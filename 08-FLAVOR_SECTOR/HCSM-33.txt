ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) identifies the 14 modes at the
self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the
periodic 8x8 torus with one Standard Model generation. The assignment of
each mode to a specific Standard Model particle, together with the N/4
mass label attached to each, is the framework's EMPIRICAL THESIS
(HCSM-23), structurally parallel to the Standard Model's Yukawa-coupling
sector. The substrate derives the 14 modes, their coordinates, and the
D4 orbit structure; the N/4 labels are EMPIRICAL ANCHORS.

Given the anchor, the mass map

    m_i = A_mass * exp(-B_mass * (N/4)_i)

relates the N/4 anchors to the observed particle masses. Both
coefficients are now derived:

    A_mass = (2d-1)N/2 = 224,
    B_mass = (d-1)kL^2/(2N^2) = 0.5411967342...

The coefficient (d-1)/2 = 3/2 in B_mass is derived in HCSM-19 by two
independent methods:

  (i)  Processor F/B split: (|F|-|B|)/|B| = (d-1)/2.
  (ii) DNLS Hartree orbit trace:
       Tr K_O1 / Tr K_O0 = 3/2 exactly in rational arithmetic.

Both derivations agree.

Applied to the twelve charged particles (electron through top quark,
plus W, Z, and Higgs), the map reproduces the observed masses at
RMS 0.0449 dex (~11% accuracy), spanning six orders of magnitude. The
residual does not correlate with N/4 (Spearman rho = +0.414, p = 0.18),
consistent with noise. The map predicts a neutrino mass m_nu = 1.775 meV
from the anchor (N/4)_nu = 60, and relates the neutrino and Planck masses
via the geometric-mean identity
m_nu M_P = A_mass v_EW exp(2(d-1)), used in HCSM-51.
