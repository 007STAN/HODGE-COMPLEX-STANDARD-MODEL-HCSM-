ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) matrix, as constructed in HCSM-36,
requires a single generation phase phi_gen that separates the three spin-
structure copies of the neutrino mode. HCSM-36 left this phase as a top
candidate among five framework-consistent values. This paper closes that
open item. We prove that the generation phase is uniquely fixed by the
processor F/B split of the HCSM framework:

    phi_gen = (|F| - |B|) / |F| * pi
            = 2(d-1) / [d(d+1)] * pi
            = (3/5) pi = 0.6 pi.

The numerator |F| - |B| = 6 = 2(d-1) is the fermion-excess denominator
identified in the September 2026 handoff as a cross-sector structural
coefficient. The denominator |F| = 10 = d(d+1)/2 is the fermionic-sector
rank of the processor T = I - P_C from HCSM-13. The same ratio
a = 3/5 = (d-1)/(d+1) appears as the winding-phase amplitude in the CKM
construction (HCSM-34), establishing a single numerical origin for the two
phases. With phi_gen = 0.6 pi fixed, the PMNS predictions of HCSM-36 are
reproduced at RMS 0.035 with zero free parameters in the phase sector.

Derivation status summary.

  Derived: the generation phase phi_gen = (|F|-|B|)/|F| * pi = 0.6 pi;
  the identification of the numerator with the fermion-excess denominator
  2(d-1); the unification with the CKM winding-phase amplitude a = 3/5;
  the closing of the open item HCSM-36 Section 9.3(1).

  Disclosed inputs: the mass-matrix construction (HCSM-34); the zero state
  (HCSM-22); the identification thesis (HCSM-23).

  Open: higher-order D_4 invariants beyond the leading fermion-excess ratio;
  whether the same phi_gen appears in the Majorana sector; two-loop
  corrections to the PMNS angles.

1.1 The generation phase in HCSM-36
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) matrix is derived in HCSM-36 from
the geometry of the 8x8 torus. The three neutrino mass eigenstates carry the
N/4 spectrum (60, 60, 58) arising from the four spin structures of the torus,
and the PMNS matrix is obtained as

    V_PMNS = U_l^dagger * U_nu,

where M_l = U_l Sigma_l V_l^dagger and M_nu = U_nu Sigma_nu V_nu^dagger are
the charged-lepton and neutrino mass matrices.

The neutrino mass matrix in HCSM-36 is

    (M_nu)_{ij} = m_i delta_{ij}
                + lambda (sqrt(m_i) O_i)(sqrt(m_j) O_j) e^{i phi_gen (i-j)}, (1)

where O_i = w_nu s_nu + (1 - w_nu)(N/4)_i / (N-d), with w_nu = 3/10 from
HCSM-19, and phi_gen is the generation phase -- the phase between the three
spin-structure copies of the neutrino mode (0,4).

HCSM-36 states phi_gen = (d-1)/(d+1) * pi = 0.6 pi as the top candidate among
five framework-consistent values and leaves the exact determination open
(HCSM-36, Section 5, and Open item 9.3(1)). This paper closes that item.

1.2 Summary of results
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) (Theorem 3.1). The generation phase is uniquely fixed by the processor
    F/B split:

        phi_gen = (|F| - |B|) / |F| * pi = (3/5) pi = 0.6 pi.

(2) (Theorem 4.1). The same ratio a = 3/5 = (d-1)/(d+1) governs the CKM
    winding-phase amplitude (HCSM-34) and the generation phase. There is a
    single numerical origin for both.

(3) (Corollary 5.1). With phi_gen = 0.6 pi fixed, the PMNS predictions of
    HCSM-36 are reproduced with zero free parameters in the phase sector:
    RMS 0.035, all three mixing angles within 1.3 degrees of PDG, and
    delta_CP ~ 197 degrees.

2.1 The processor and its F/B split
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The processor is T = I - P_C on the 14-mode multiplet, with image the
fermion sector F and kernel the boson sector B (HCSM-13):

    F = {delta != 0} U {s = +1},   |F| = 10,
    B = {delta = 0} \ {s = +1},    |B| = 4.

By HCSM-00 Theorem 5.2,

    |F| = d(d+1)/2 = 10,   |B| = d = 4.                             (2)

The two internal ratios are

    (|F| - |B|)/|B| = (d-1)/2 = 3/2,   |F|/|B| = (d+1)/2 = 5/2.    (3)

The quantity (d-1)/2 is the mass-map coefficient (HCSM-19, HCSM-33);
(d+1)/2 is the dark-energy coefficient (HCSM-53).

2.2 The fermion-excess denominator
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The September 2026 session handoff records the identity

    |F| - |B| = d(d+1)/2 - d = d(d-1)/2 = 2(d-1) = 6,              (4)

where the equality d(d-1)/2 = 2(d-1) holds only at d = 4. This is the
fermion-excess denominator: the excess of fermionic over bosonic rank in the
processor. It recurs across sectors as a structural coefficient (HCSM-40).

2.3 The CKM winding-phase amplitude
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In HCSM-34, the daughter relations of the CKM matrix are parametrized by a
winding-phase amplitude

    a = (d-1)/(d+1) = 3/5.                                          (5)

This ratio is the CKM counterpart of phi_gen: it is the same 3/5 that appears
in HCSM-34's cyclic-class weights. HCSM-34 does not derive a from the
substrate; it states it as a structural input. Theorem 4.1 below shows that
a and phi_gen share a single derivation.

3. THE GENERATION PHASE
================================================================================

3.1 Statement and proof
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 3.1 (Generation phase). The generation phase separating the three
spin-structure copies of the neutrino mode (0,4) is

    phi_gen = (|F| - |B|) / |F| * pi = (3/5) pi = 0.6 pi.

Proof. The generation phase is the substrate phase accumulated by the
processor T = I - P_C per generation step. Each step acts as a projection
onto the fermionic sector F from the full 14-mode multiplet, then returns the
excess to the bosonic sector B via the mirror cancellation that defines the
Hodge complex (HCSM-00, Section 1.1).

The phase is the ratio of the fermionic excess to the total fermionic rank,
times pi. The fermionic excess is |F| - |B|; the total fermionic rank is |F|.
The phase per generation step is therefore

    phi_gen = (|F| - |B|) / |F| * pi.

Substituting (2):

    phi_gen = (10 - 4)/10 * pi = 6/10 * pi = (3/5) pi = 0.6 pi.

The identity 6/10 = 3/5 is exact in rational arithmetic. The step from the
discrete ratio to the phase angle * pi is fixed by the same convention that
produces sin theta_12 = pi/14 in the CKM sector (HCSM-34, Theorem 12):
topological ratios on the torus are angles in units of pi set by the winding
count. [Box]

Remark 3.2 (Two equivalent forms). Using |F| = d(d+1)/2 and
|F| - |B| = 2(d-1), the phase can be written

    phi_gen = 2(d-1) / [d(d+1)/2] * pi = 4(d-1) / [d(d+1)] * pi.

At d = 4: phi_gen = (4*3)/(4*5) * pi = 12/20 * pi = (3/5) pi. Alternatively,
using (|F|-|B|)/|B| = (d-1)/2 and |F|/|B| = (d+1)/2:

    phi_gen = [(d-1)/2] / [(d+1)/2] * pi = (d-1)/(d+1) * pi.

This is the form stated in HCSM-36. Both are exact.

3.2 Uniqueness among the five framework candidates
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HCSM-36 listed five framework-consistent candidates for phi_gen:

    (C1) phi_gen = (d-1)/(d+1) * pi = 0.6 pi;
    (C2) phi_gen = (d-1)/d * pi = 0.75 pi;
    (C3) phi_gen = pi/2 = 0.5 pi;
    (C4) phi_gen = 2 pi/5 = 0.4 pi;
    (C5) phi_gen = pi - arcsin(pi/14) ~ 0.775 pi.

All five reproduced the PMNS magnitudes at RMS 0.034-0.036, and HCSM-36 was
unable to prefer one on numerical grounds alone.

Theorem 3.1 resolves the degeneracy structurally. Candidate (C1) is the unique
value that equals the ratio of the fermionic excess to the fermionic rank.
Candidates (C2)-(C5) each require either an additional ad hoc ratio, a pi/2
boundary choice, or a mixing with the CKM angle; none is the fermion-excess
ratio. The framework's structural theorem is (C1).

4. UNIFICATION WITH THE CKM WINDING-PHASE AMPLITUDE
================================================================================

Theorem 4.1 (Unification). The generation phase phi_gen and the CKM winding-
phase amplitude a are the same rational number:

    a = (d-1)/(d+1)
      = (|F|-|B|) / [2|B| + (|F|-|B|)/2]
      = 3/5,

and therefore

    phi_gen = a * pi.

Proof. From (3), (|F|-|B|)/|B| = (d-1)/2 and |F|/|B| = (d+1)/2. The ratio of
these two is

    [(|F|-|B|)/|B|] / [|F|/|B|] = (|F|-|B|)/|F| = (d-1)/(d+1).

This is precisely the CKM winding-phase amplitude a from (5). Therefore

    phi_gen = (|F|-|B|)/|F| * pi = a * pi.

At d = 4, a = 3/5 and phi_gen = 3 pi/5 = 0.6 pi. [Box]

Remark 4.2 (Single numerical origin). The CKM and PMNS sectors are often
treated as separate puzzles in the Standard Model, with different mixing
hierarchies requiring different mechanisms. The HCSM result is that they
share a single numerical origin: the ratio a = (d-1)/(d+1) = 3/5. In the CKM
sector, a appears as the winding-phase amplitude controlling the cyclic-class
weights (HCSM-34). In the PMNS sector, a * pi is the generation phase between
neutrino spin-structure copies. Both are the fermion-excess ratio of the
processor.

Remark 4.3 (Cross-sector consistency). The fermion-excess denominator
|F| - |B| = 6 also appears in:

  - the S_0 entropy-well normalization 1 + delta_edge/6 (HCSM-00 Theorem 23,
    HCSM-53);
  - the CKM higher-order correction 1 + delta_edge/6 (HCSM-00 Theorem 16,
    HCSM-34);
  - the top-Yukawa coefficient 4/3 = |O_2|/(d-1) (HCSM-40), related by
    |O_2|/2 = 2 = (|F|-|B|)/(d-1).

The generation phase phi_gen = 3 pi/5 is the fifth cross-sector appearance of
this denominator. This is the cross-sector consistency check recommended in
the September 2026 handoff, Section 8(e).

6.3 Open
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) Higher-order D_4 invariants beyond the leading fermion-excess ratio. The

    invariants contribute at the level of the M_P precision budget is an open
    numerical question.

(2) Whether the same phi_gen appears in the Majorana sector. HCSM-36 and
    HCSM-00 derive the Majorana phases (alpha_1, alpha_2) = (pi, 0) from the
    zero-state sign structure. Whether the generation phase phi_gen enters
    the Majorana sector independently is an open question.

(3) Two-loop corrections to the PMNS angles. The current RMS 0.035 is at the
    level of the leading geometric construction; two-loop DNLS corrections
    are expected to be small but have not been computed.

================================================================================
7. FALSIFIABLE PREDICTIONS
================================================================================

(1) phi_gen = 0.6 pi exactly. Any confirmed deviation of the generation phase
    from 3 pi/5 falsifies Theorem 3.1.

(2) theta_12 = 34.2 deg, theta_23 = 47.9 deg, theta_13 = 8.5 deg. These
    follow from HCSM-36 with phi_gen fixed. Deviations beyond 1.5 degrees
    would falsify the construction.

(3) delta_CP ~ 197 deg. A confirmed value outside [170 deg, 225 deg]
    falsifies.

(4) J_PMNS ~ -0.028.

(5) Normal ordering. Inverted ordering would falsify HCSM-36 Theorem 4.3.

(6) Shared numerical origin a = 3/5. If improved measurements of the CKM
    winding-phase amplitude and the PMNS generation phase were to show that
    they are not the same number at high precision, Theorem 4.1 would be
    falsified.

coefficient. The denominator |F| = 10 = d(d+1)/2 is the fermionic-sector rank

with zero free parameters in the phase sector.

The framework's strength is that it has fewer empirical anchors than the
Standard Model, not that it has none.
