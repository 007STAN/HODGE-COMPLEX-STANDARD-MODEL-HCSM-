1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 The flavor problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Standard Model does not derive its own flavor structure: fermion masses,
the CKM matrix, and the PMNS matrix are inputs. The Jarlskog invariant
J ~ 3.08e-5 has no first-principles derivation.

1.2 The Rank 6 problem and its dissolution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Prior HCSM work (HCSM-34 v1) framed the CKM as requiring a 3D irreducible
representation of D_4. Since D_4 has only 1- and 2-dimensional irreps, the
framework seemed to require enlargement: lattice extension Z_8 x Z_8 x Z_3,
spin-structure construction, or coset D_4 x A_4.

This paper shows that the enlargement is not needed. The CKM is a
hierarchical series in the Cabibbo angle lambda = pi/14, where
pi/14 = pi/(2(L-1)) is the half-angle between adjacent modes on the 14-mode
circle. The three generations are the three levels of the hierarchy, not
three states of a representation. Rank 6 closes by reframing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(2) (Theorem 4.1). lambda = pi/14; A = [d/(d+1)]*(1+delta_edge);
    sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) = [(d+1)/(d(d-1))]*(1+delta_edge/6).

(3) (Theorem 5.1). |V_us| = lambda; |V_cb| = A lambda^2;
    |V_ub| = A lambda^3 sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2).

(4) (Theorem 6.1). Rank 6 closed: no 3D irrep of D_4 required.

(5) (Theorem 7.1). The higher-order correction to
    sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) is the fermion-excess factor
    1 + delta_edge/(|F|-|B|), matching observation at better than 0.07%.

================================================================================

framework-native:

    sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2)

Numerically:

Structural derivation of the third parameter. The leading factor
(d+1)/(d(d-1)) = 5/12 is a pure D_4-orbit identity. The correction factor

    |F| - |B| = 10 - 4 = 6 = 2(d-1),                                   (3)

which is the same denominator that appears in the bridge identity (HCSM-40
eq. 48) and the DME entropy-well normalization (HCSM-53 Theorem 5.1):

    S_0 = v_EW^2 * (7/40)(1 + delta_edge/6).                           (4)

The fermion-excess denominator is therefore a universal HCSM coefficient
that appears across the Higgs, cosmological, and flavor sectors. The sign is
+ for sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) because it probes the CP-violating corner
of the unitarity triangle, while the bridge identity probes the Yukawa
saturation boundary and carries a - sign. [Box]

Remark 4.2 (Higher-order structure). The correction factor
