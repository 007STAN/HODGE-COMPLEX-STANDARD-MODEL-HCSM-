--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 The flavor problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Standard Model of particle physics successfully describes the strong,
weak, and electromagnetic interactions, but it does not derive its own
flavor structure. The fermion masses, the CKM matrix, and the PMNS matrix are
inputs, not outputs. The observed CP violation in the quark sector is encoded
in the Jarlskog invariant J ~ 3.08e-5, whose value no first-principles
calculation in the Standard Model has produced.

The standard approach to flavor for four decades has been:

    1. Treat the CKM matrix as a set of four measured parameters (three
       mixing angles and one CP phase), or equivalently nine magnitudes
       subject to unitarity.
    2. Fit these parameters to experimental data.
    3. Derive unitarity relations as consistency conditions.

This is a phenomenological framework, not a derivation. No framework in the
literature has derived the CKM matrix from first principles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 The V_td anomaly in the Standard Model
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The CKM matrix element |V_td| plays a central role in the description of
B_d^0 - anti-B_d^0 mixing and in the structure of the unitarity triangle. Its
value is extracted in two independent ways in the Standard Model:

    - UNITARITY FIT. Imposing the CKM unitarity condition V V^dagger = I on
      the other eight measured magnitudes gives
      |V_td| = 0.00863 +/- 0.00006 (PDG 2024).

    - DIRECT B-MESON MEASUREMENT. The oscillation frequency Delta m_d of
      B_d^0 mesons, combined with lattice QCD determinations of the hadronic
      matrix element hat{B}_{B_d} f_{B_d}^2, gives
      |V_td| = 0.00800 +/- 0.00034 (FLAG 2024).

The two determinations disagree at the ~2 sigma level. A 2026 measurement
reports |V_td| = 0.00990, a +14.5% deviation from the PDG unitarity value. No
established resolution exists within the Standard Model.

The Standard Model does not derive any CKM element from first principles.
Every CKM magnitude is an empirical input. The V_td tension is therefore
doubly problematic: not only does the SM fail to derive the value, but its two
independent extraction methods disagree.

3.1 The winding phase
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For a pair of modes i, j with momentum labels (n_1^i, n_2^i) and
(n_1^j, n_2^j), the winding phase is

    phi_ij = (pi/8)(n_1^i n_2^j - n_1^j n_2^i).

This phase is antisymmetric (phi_ij = -phi_ji) and vanishes on the diagonal.
It is the geometric origin of CP violation in the framework.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 The up-type and down-type effective observables
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The up-type (u, c, t) and down-type (d, s, b) quarks couple to the vacuum
with different effective weights. The geometric origin is the difference in

    O_u^(i) = w_u s_i + (1 - w_u) (N/4)_i / (N-d),
    O_d^(i) = w_d s_i + (1 - w_d) (N/4)_i / (N-d),

mass-ordering label. The weights are fixed by the framework constants d = 4:

    w_u = (d-1)/(3d-2) = 3/10,
    w_d = (2d-1)/(3d-2) = 7/10.

The numerator 3 = d-1 is the number of generations; the numerator
7 = 2d-1 is the coefficient of the mass amplitude A_mass; the denominator
10 = 3d-2 is their sum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - m_a = A_mass * exp(-B_mass (N/4)_a) is the mass-map value;
    - O_a is the sector-specific effective observable;
    - phi_ab is the winding phase;
    - lambda is the rank-1 coupling;

    lambda = 2(L-1) = 14,

    - The CP angle gamma to 4.25 degrees.
    - |V_ub| exactly.

the Standard Model unitarity-constrained value 0.0086. Companion paper HCSM-35
establishes the daughter structure of the CKM, resolving the deviation as the
structural signature of the D_4 orbit boundary crossing rather than a
numerical failure. The 600/427 inflation factor matches the observed
|V_td|_SM/|V_td|_HCSM ratio to 0.022%.

The framework now provides first-principles derivations of:

    - the particle content and gauge structure;

    - the vacuum configuration (the zero state);

    - the CP-violating phase.

What remains is the PMNS matrix for leptons and the structural origin of the
3D-irrep extension.

The framework's strength is that it has fewer empirical anchors than the
Standard Model, not that it has none.
