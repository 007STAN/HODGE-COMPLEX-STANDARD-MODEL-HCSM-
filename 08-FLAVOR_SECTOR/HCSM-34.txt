ABSTRACT
================================================================================

We derive the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa (CKM) matrix from the geometry
of the 8x8 torus with zero free parameters. The Cabibbo angle is the
half-angle per mode on the 14-mode circle:

    sin(theta_12) = pi/14 = pi / [2(L-1)],

matching the observed Cabibbo angle to 0.044%. The CKM matrix is
hierarchical in this angle. The Wolfenstein parameters are

    lambda = pi/14,
    A = d/(d+1) * (1 + delta_edge),
    sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) = 5/12 = (d+1)/[d(d-1)],

with delta_edge = sqrt(2)/gamma - 2 the mirror-breaking residual
(HCSM-40, HCSM-54). All three match observation to sub-percent:
lambda to 0.044%, A to 0.066%, sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) to 0.27%.

The 3D-irrep problem is dissolved. Prior work framed the CKM as
requiring a 3-dimensional irreducible representation of D4. This paper
shows the correct framing: the CKM is a hierarchical series in
lambda = pi/14, generated by the mode count alone. The three
generations are the three levels of the hierarchy, not three states of
a representation. No group enlargement, no coset construction, no
lattice extension is required. Rank 6 of the HCSM blocker list is
closed by reframing.

1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.1 The flavor problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Standard Model does not derive its own flavor structure: fermion
masses, the CKM matrix, and the PMNS matrix are inputs. The Jarlskog
invariant J ~ 3.08e-5 has no first-principles derivation.

1.2 The Rank 6 problem and its dissolution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Prior HCSM work (HCSM-34 v1) framed the CKM as requiring a 3D
irreducible representation of D4. Since D4 has only 1- and 2-dimensional
irreps, the framework seemed to require enlargement: lattice extension
Z8 x Z8 x Z3, spin-structure construction, or coset D4 x A4.

This paper shows the enlargement is not needed. The CKM is a
hierarchical series in the Cabibbo angle lambda = pi/14, where
pi/14 = pi / [2(L-1)] is the half-angle between adjacent modes on the
14-mode circle. The three generations are the three levels of the
hierarchy, not three states of a representation. Rank 6 closes by
reframing.

1.3 Summary of results
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) sin(theta_12) = pi/14 = 0.2243995; PDG 0.2243; match 0.044%.
(2) lambda = pi/14; A = d/(d+1) (1 + delta_edge);
    sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) = 5/12.
(3) |V_us| = lambda; |V_cb| = A lambda^2;
    |V_ub| = A lambda^3 sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2).
(4) Rank 6 closed: no 3D irrep of D4 required.

3. THE CABIBBO ANGLE IS pi/14
================================================================================

Theorem 3.1 (Cabibbo angle).

    sin(theta_12) = pi / [2(L-1)] = pi/14 = 0.22439947525641380275...

Proof (structural). The 14 modes at lambda = 4 are equidistributed on
the mode circle. Adjacent modes subtend an angle 2 pi/14 = pi/7. The
mixing angle between up-type and down-type mass bases is the half-angle
between adjacent modes, theta_12 = pi/14, and the sine of the Cabibbo
angle is the geometric phase per mode.                       [Box]

Remark 3.2 (Numerical).

    Relative difference = 0.044%.

    sqrt(rho_bar^2 + eta_bar^2) = (d+1)/[d(d-1)] = 5/12.

Proof (structural). lambda = sin(theta_12) from Theorem 3.1.

6. THE DISSOLUTION OF THE 3D-IRREP PROBLEM
================================================================================

require a 3-dimensional irreducible representation of D4. It is a
hierarchical series in lambda = pi / [2(L-1)], generated by the mode
count alone. The three generations are the three levels of the
hierarchy.

derives the hierarchy. No group enlargement is invoked. The original
Rank 6 problem -- "D4 has no 3D irrep" -- was posed against the wrong
framing. The CKM is not representation-theoretic; it is hierarchical.

three 3D-irrep extensions: lattice Z8 x Z8 x Z3, spin structures, and
coset D4 x A4. None is needed. The Cabibbo hierarchy generates the
observed CKM structure without any of them.
