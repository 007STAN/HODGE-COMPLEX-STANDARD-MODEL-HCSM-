\begin{abstract}
We derive the classical Maxwell-Lorentz sector of the Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) from the discrete substrate $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}_8 \times \mathbb{Z}_8$.

The substrate is the periodic $8 \times 8$ torus with $N = 64$ sites. The Hodge complex $\Omega^0 \oplus \Omega^1 \oplus \Omega^2$ has total dimension $256$. The Hodge-Dirac operator $D = d + d^*$ satisfies $D^2|_{\Omega^k} = \Delta$ exactly on each form degree. The self-paired eigenvalue $\lambda = 4$ of the discrete Laplacian has multiplicity $2(L-1) = 14$, identified with one Standard Model generation. The $56$-dimensional Hodge lift $V_{56} = 14\Omega^0 + 28\Omega^1 + 14\Omega^2$ is the arena of the physical world.

The gauge algebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{SM}} = \mathfrak{su}(3)_c \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2)_L \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)^5$ is derived as a Lie subalgebra of the $D_4$ commutant on the $14$-mode multiplet. The hypercharge functional $Y$ is uniquely determined by the sector-coarse correspondence and is anomaly-free: all four one-loop conditions and all three cubic gauge anomalies vanish in rational arithmetic.

After electroweak symmetry breaking, the unbroken $\mathfrak{u}(1)_{\mathrm{EM}}$ is identified. The photon is its gauge boson: $A_\mu = \sin\theta_W W^3_\mu + \cos\theta_W B_\mu$, with $\sin^2\theta_W(M_Z) = 0.2312$ matching observation to $0.02\%$. The $W$ and $Z$ acquire Proca masses $m_W = 80.35$ GeV, $m_Z = 91.63$ GeV, with mass ratio $m_W/m_Z = \cos\theta_W = 0.8769$ matching PDG to $0.5\%$.

The kinematic continuum limit is derived from the Hodge star on $\ker D$. The Hodge star restricted to the $4$-dimensional kernel has eigenvalues $\{+1, -1, +i, -i\}$, yielding a canonical Lorentzian metric of signature $(3,1)$. The isotropic dispersion relation $\lambda(n) = (\pi^2/16)(n_1^2 + n_2^2) + O(n^4)$ is derived. The asymptotic expansion of the discrete exterior calculus operators is computed explicitly: $d_0/a \to \partial_\mu$ and $d_0^* d_0/a^2 \to \Delta$ with corrections of order $a^2$.

The dynamical continuum limit is closed by the Tensor Product Theorem: $V_{56} \cong V_{14} \otimes \ker D$ as $D_4$ representations. This exact representation-theoretic identity forces the bundle construction over $\ker D$ with fiber $V_{14}$, identifies the coarse-graining procedure as projection onto the $D_4$-invariant sector, and yields the continuum Yang-Mills action plus corrections suppressed by $\epsilon^2$.

The renormalization group is a structural flow from the finite lattice to the infrared. The hidden sector $\Omega^1|_4 \oplus \Omega^2|_4$ has $42$ modes with $D_4$ content $6A_1 \oplus 4A_2 \oplus 5B_1 \oplus 5B_2 \oplus 11E$, corresponding to $5$ weak doublets, $6$ color triplets, and $31$ singlets. The contributions to the one-loop beta functions are $b_1^{\mathrm{hid}} = 0$, $b_2^{\mathrm{hid}} = 5/3$, $b_3^{\mathrm{hid}} = 2$. The $kL$ crossover provides the RG scale: $kL_{\mathrm{bare}} = 38.4$ and $kL_{\mathrm{phys}} = 38.442527$.

We state explicitly what is derived, what is disclosed, and what remains open.

\begin{theorem}[Hodge-Dirac square]

\[
