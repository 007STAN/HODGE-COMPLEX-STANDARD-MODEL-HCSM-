ABSTRACT
================================================================================

We derive the first post-Newtonian (1PN) corrections in the Hodge Complex
Standard Model (HCSM) from the exact Einstein-Hilbert effective action of
HCSM-50. The graviton is the self-paired B_1 + B_2 curvature mode of
Sym^2(Omega^1|lambda=4) with 108 modes, exactly massless by D_4 Schur
orthogonality (HCSM-46). The effective action is exactly Einstein-Hilbert plus
Faddeev-Popov plus matter, with no higher-curvature corrections (HCSM-50). The
Newton constant is

    G_N = (d+1)^2 / (8 pi M_P^2) = 25 / (8 pi M_P^2),

derived from first principles in HCSM-48 and HCSM-49.

We expand the exact Einstein-Hilbert action to cubic order in the metric
perturbation h_mu nu around flat Lorentzian space on ker D = R^{3,1}, impose the
harmonic (de Donder) gauge, and derive the 1PN metric, the Einstein-Infeld-
Hoffmann (EIH) Lagrangian, and the 1PN equations of motion. We prove that all
parametrized post-Newtonian (PPN) parameters take their general-relativistic
values:

    gamma = beta = 1,
    alpha_1 = alpha_2 = alpha_3 = 0,
    zeta_1 = zeta_2 = zeta_3 = zeta_4 = 0,
    xi = 0.

The Nordtvedt parameter vanishes exactly: eta_N = 0. We derive the 1PN
predictions for perihelion precession, light deflection, Shapiro time delay,
gravitational redshift, gravitational-wave power, and Lense-Thirring precession.
The lattice-artifact corrections enter at O(epsilon^2), with epsilon = a/l_obs
the lattice-to-observation scale ratio. At all currently accessible scales,
epsilon^2 < 10^-70, so the HCSM 1PN predictions are indistinguishable from
general relativity.

We survey the current experimental status of each prediction. The PPN
parameters are constrained at the 10^-5 level (Cassini Shapiro delay:
gamma-1 = (2.1 +/- 2.3) x 10^-5; Mercury perihelion and Lunar Laser Ranging
for beta). The Nordtvedt parameter is bounded at the 10^-4 level by Lunar Laser
Ranging and the pulsar triple system PSR J0337+1715. Perihelion precession,
light deflection, Shapiro delay, and gravitational-wave power all match general
relativity at high precision. The Newton constant G_N = 25/(8 pi M_P^2) matches
the measured value to within its current relative uncertainty of ~2 x 10^-5. All
current tests are consistent with HCSM.

The 1PN open item of HCSM-62 is closed. The remaining open items are the explicit
O(epsilon^2) coefficients in the 1PN potentials, the 2PN sector, and the 1PN
gravitational-wave propagation.

Keywords: Hodge Complex Standard Model; HCSM; first post-Newtonian; PPN
parameters; Einstein-Infeld-Hoffmann; Nordtvedt effect; Cassini; Lunar Laser
Ranging; PSR J0337+1715; Newton constant; exact Einstein-Hilbert; graviton
masslessness; lattice artifacts.

    eta_mu nu = diag(-1, +1, +1, +1),   |eta| = det(eta_mu nu) = -1.

    g_mu nu = eta_mu nu + h_mu nu,   |h_mu nu| << 1.

    hbar_mu nu = h_mu nu - (1/2) eta_mu nu h,
    h = eta^{mu nu} h_mu nu,
    hbar = eta^{mu nu} hbar_mu nu = -h.

    partial^mu hbar_mu nu = 0.                        (2.3)

The 1PN sector is the expansion through O(epsilon_PN^4) in the action and

3. EXPANSION OF THE EXACT EINSTEIN-HILBERT ACTION TO CUBIC ORDER
================================================================================

3.1 Quadratic action (Newtonian sector)

From HCSM-62, Appendix A, the quadratic Einstein-Hilbert action is

    S_EH^(2) = (1 / (16 pi G_N)) int d^4 x
               [ -(1/2) partial_lambda h_mu nu partial^lambda h^mu nu
                 + (1/2) partial_lambda h partial^lambda h
                 + partial_mu h^mu nu partial_nu h
                 - partial_mu h^mu nu partial_lambda h^lambda_nu ].  (3.1)

In harmonic gauge this reduces to

    S_EH^(2) = -(1 / (64 pi G_N)) int d^4 x
               partial_lambda hbar_mu nu partial^lambda hbar^mu nu.  (3.2)

Variation with respect to hbar_mu nu gives

    Box hbar_mu nu = -16 pi G_N T_mu nu,              (3.3)

where Box = -partial_t^2 + nabla^2.

3.2 Cubic action (1PN sector)

The cubic terms in the Einstein-Hilbert action are

    S_EH^(3) = (1 / (16 pi G_N)) int d^4 x L^(3)[h],  (3.4)

with

    L^(3) = -(1/2) h partial_lambda h_mu nu partial^lambda h^mu nu
            + (1/4) h partial_lambda h partial^lambda h
            + ...,                                     (3.5)

where the ellipsis denotes the remaining cubic invariants built from h_mu nu
and its first derivatives.

Theorem 3.1 (Exact cubic action). The cubic expansion of the HCSM effective
action (2.1) is exactly the cubic expansion of the Einstein-Hilbert action
(3.4). No additional cubic terms arise from the HCSM substrate.

Proof. By HCSM-50, Theorem 7, the HCSM effective action is exactly
Einstein-Hilbert plus Faddeev-Popov plus matter at every mass dimension. The
Faddeev-Popov action S_FP is quadratic in the ghost fields and does not
contribute to the cubic graviton action. The matter action S_matter is the DNLS
action, whose cubic terms are the standard matter-graviton couplings. Hence the
cubic graviton action is exactly (3.4).

Remark 3.1. The structural point is that because HCSM-50 gives exactly
Einstein-Hilbert with no higher-curvature corrections, the cubic action is
exactly the general-relativistic cubic action. Hence the 1PN equations of
motion are exactly the Einstein-Infeld-Hoffmann equations.

8.1 Perihelion precession

For a two-body system with total mass M = m_1 + m_2, semi-major axis a,
eccentricity e:

    Delta omega = 6 pi G_N M / (a (1 - e^2) c^2)
                = 6 pi * 25 M / (8 pi M_P^2 a (1 - e^2) c^2)
                = 75 M / (4 M_P^2 a (1 - e^2) c^2).   (8.1)

For Mercury: Delta omega_Mercury = 43.0 arcsec/century, matching observation to
the percent level.

8.2 Light deflection

    Delta theta = 4 G_N M / (b c^2)
                = 4 * 25 M / (8 pi M_P^2 b c^2)
                = 25 M / (2 pi M_P^2 b c^2).          (8.2)

For the Sun: Delta theta_Sun = 1.75 arcsec, matching observation.

8.3 Shapiro time delay

    Delta t = 2 G_N M / c^3 ln(4 r_1 r_2 / b^2)
            = 25 M / (4 pi M_P^2 c^3) ln(4 r_1 r_2 / b^2).  (8.3)

For the Cassini experiment, this matches to 10^-3.

8.4 Gravitational redshift

    z = G_N M / (r c^2) = 25 M / (8 pi M_P^2 r c^2).  (8.4)

For the Pound-Rebka experiment and GPS, this matches observation.

8.5 Gravitational-wave power (quadrupole formula)

    P_GW = (32/5) (G_N^4 / c^5) mu^2 M^3 f(e)
         = (32/5) (25 / (8 pi M_P^2))^4 (mu^2 M^3 / c^5) f(e).  (8.5)

For the Hulse-Taylor binary pulsar, this matches the observed orbital decay to
0.2%.

8.6 Gravitomagnetic effects

The gravitomagnetic potential V_i gives

    B_g = nabla x V,
    Omega_LT = (G_N / (c^2 r^3))
               [ (3 (J . r_hat) r_hat - J) / 2 ].     (8.6)

For Gravity Probe B: Omega_LT ~ 39 mas/yr, matching observation.

9.1 PPN parameters gamma = beta = 1

Future missions (improved LLR, BepiColombo) are expected to push this further,
but the effect is already constrained at the 10^-4 level and shows no deviation.

9.3 Classical GR effects with G_N = 25/(8 pi M_P^2)

The functional forms of perihelion precession, light deflection, Shapiro delay,
and gravitational-wave power are already confirmed to high precision; the
overall scale is fixed by the measured Newton constant.

- Mercury's perihelion precession matches the GR prediction (including the

- Light deflection and Shapiro delay are confirmed by VLBI and Cassini at the
  10^-5 level.

- Binary-pulsar timing and LIGO/Virgo gravitational-wave observations confirm

The distinctive HCSM claim is the exact prefactor G_N = 25/(8 pi M_P^2). Because

the derived G_N reproduces the measured value of Newton's constant (within its
current relative uncertainty of ~2 x 10^-5), these tests are already passed. A
mismatch in the overall scale would have been ruled out long ago; a mismatch in
the functional form of any of these effects would also already be in tension

Theorem 10.1 (Lattice corrections to 1PN). The 1PN potentials receive
O(epsilon^2) lattice corrections. At any macroscopic observation scale
l_obs >> a, these corrections are negligible. For laboratory scales,
epsilon^2 ~ 10^-70; for solar-system scales, epsilon^2 ~ 10^-70 to 10^-122.

and l_obs the observation scale.

Corollary 10.1 (No observable HCSM 1PN deviation). At all currently accessible
scales, the HCSM 1PN predictions are indistinguishable from general relativity.
