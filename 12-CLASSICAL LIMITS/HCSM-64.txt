We derive the relativistic energy-momentum limit of the Hodge Complex Standard
Model (HCSM) from the discrete substrate Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The substrate is
the periodic 8x8 torus with N = 64 sites. The Hodge complex Omega^0 + Omega^1 +
Omega^2 has total dimension 256. The Hodge-Dirac operator D = d + d* satisfies
D^2|_{Omega^k} = Delta exactly on each form degree. The self-paired eigenvalue
lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian has multiplicity 2(L-1) = 14, identified
with one Standard Model generation. The 56-dimensional Hodge lift V_56 = 14
Omega^0 + 28 Omega^1 + 14 Omega^2 is the arena of the physical world.

The Lorentzian metric on ker D ~= R^{3,1} emerges from the Hodge star, with
signature (3,1) derived in HCSM-03. The four-momentum vector p^mu = (E/c, p)
is constructed from the HCSM-native momentum operator on the torus and the
energy functional derived from the DNLS Hamiltonian. We prove the mass-shell
condition p^mu p_mu = -m^2 c^2 as a theorem, where m is the HCSM mass-map
output.

The relativistic dispersion relation E^2 = p^2 c^2 + m^2 c^4 is derived from
the tensor product structure V_56 ~= V_14 (x) ker D established in HCSM-61.
The lattice corrections to the relativistic relation are computed explicitly
through O(epsilon^4), where epsilon = a/l_obs is the lattice-to-observation
scale ratio. At all observable scales, epsilon^2 <= 10^{-70}, so the HCSM
predictions are indistinguishable from special relativity.

We close the critical short list of open items: derivation of E(p) =
sqrt(p^2 c^2 + m^2 c^4) from the DNLS Hamiltonian; construction of p^mu from
substrate-native translation generators; proof that the linearized DNLS energy
equals the relativistic energy; exact interacting lattice dispersion;
derivation of the DNLS couplings g = q = 33/64; backreaction of lattice
corrections on the mass map; and renormalization of the energy-momentum tensor.

We further derive the Dirac equation from the Hodge-Dirac operator. The Hodge
complex at each site is a 4-component Dirac spinor; the Hodge-Dirac operator
reduces to i gamma^mu d_mu in the continuum limit; the mass term is supplied by
the mass map; and chirality is the Hodge form-parity Z_2 grading.

The framework now has a single dimensionful anchor M_P, with all dimensionless
ratios determined by the substrate geometry.

Keywords: Hodge Complex Standard Model; HCSM; relativistic energy-momentum;
four-momentum; mass-shell condition; special relativity; Lorentz
transformation; dispersion relation; DNLS; tensor product structure; lattice
artifacts; emergent spacetime; Dirac equation.
