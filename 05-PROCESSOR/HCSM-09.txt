## Abstract

The Hodge Complex Standard Model realizes the gauge algebra su(3) + su(2) + u(1)^5 as a Lie subalgebra of the D4 commutant on the 14-mode multiplet, and identifies the physical hypercharge functional as a specific element of the 15-dimensional sector-coarse space (HCSM-06, HCSM-07). This paper establishes the orientation-flip Z2 symmetry of this structure.

We define the sector flip sigma on the 56-dimensional Hodge sector V_56 = Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2 at lambda = 4 by the swap (alpha_0, omega_1, alpha_2) -> (alpha_2, omega_1, alpha_0), induced by the Hodge star identification Omega^0 = Omega^2.

We prove:

(i) sigma is an involution (sigma^2 = I);
(ii) sigma commutes with the D4 action;
(iii) under sigma, the sector operators transform as I -> I, F -> F, O -> -O;
(iv) the A1 hypercharge coefficients transform as (0, -1/6, -1/2) -> (0, -1/6, +1/2);
(v) the B1 coefficients transform as (0, +1/2, +1/2) -> (0, +1/2, -1/2);
(vi) the sector hypercharge values transform by swapping Omega^0 and Omega^2;
(vii) the multiset of hypercharge magnitudes is invariant;
(viii) the anomaly cancellation sums are invariant;
(ix) if the CKM and PMNS matrices depend only on the multiset of hypercharge magnitudes, then their element magnitudes transform as |V_ij| -> |V_ji| and |U_ij| -> |U_ji| (multisets invariant) while the Jarlskog invariant flips sign.

The flip exchanges the labels u_R^c <-> d_R^c on the A1 block and e_R^c <-> nu_R^c on the B1 block, with no consequence for the magnitudes of any framework observable. The framework therefore does not privilege Omega^0 over Omega^2: the assignment of one color-triplet multiplet to the outer sector and the other to the inner sector is a labeling convention.

All claims are HCSM-native and verified at machine precision.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The orientation-flip question

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) derives the gauge algebra

    g_HCSM = su(3) + su(2) + u(1)^5

as a Lie subalgebra of the D4 commutant on the 14-mode multiplet (HCSM-06). The physical hypercharge functional is a specific element of the 15-dimensional sector-coarse space of the 56-dimensional Hodge sector (HCSM-07):

    Y = sum_rho (alpha_rho * I + beta_rho * F + gamma_rho * O) P_rho,

with A1 coefficients (0, -1/6, -1/2) and B1 coefficients (0, +1/2, +1/2), uniquely determined by the Standard Model content and the sector assignment.

The present paper asks: does the framework privilege the sector Omega^0 over the sector Omega^2? These two sectors carry identical D4 decompositions 3A1 + A2 + 2B1 + 2B2 + 3E, and they are Hodge-dual to each other. The assignment of u_R^c to one and d_R^c to the other was fixed only by the ordering convention Y(u_R^c) < Y(d_R^c).

We prove that the framework admits a Z2 symmetry, the orientation flip, that swaps Omega^0 <-> Omega^2 and fixes Omega^1, under which every physical prediction of the framework is invariant up to a relabeling of the color-triplet and lepton multiplets. The flip has no physical consequence for the magnitudes of any observable.

### 1.2 The one-line summary

The 56-dimensional Hodge sector admits a Z2 symmetry sigma that swaps Omega^0 <-> Omega^2 while fixing Omega^1 pointwise, induced by the Hodge star identification of the outer sectors. The sector operators transform as I -> I, F -> F, O -> -O. The hypercharge functional transforms by u_R^c <-> d_R^c on the A1 block and e_R^c <-> nu_R^c on the B1 block. All magnitudes of physical predictions are invariant. The framework does not privilege Omega^0 over Omega^2.

### 1.3 Main results

(1) **The sector flip (Definition 3.1).** A Z2 action sigma on the 56-dimensional Hodge sector V_56 = Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2 is defined by the swap (alpha_0, omega_1, alpha_2) -> (alpha_2, omega_1, alpha_0), induced by the Hodge star identification Omega^0 = Omega^2.

(2) **Involution (Lemma 4.1).** sigma^2 = I_56.

(3) **D4-equivariance (Theorem 5.1).** sigma commutes with the D4 action: [sigma, R] = [sigma, S] = 0.

(4) **Sector operator transformation (Theorem 6.1).** Under sigma,

    sigma I sigma^{-1} = I,
    sigma F sigma^{-1} = F,
    sigma O sigma^{-1} = -O.

(5) **A1 coefficient transformation (Theorem 7.1).** The A1 hypercharge coefficients transform as

    (0, -1/6, -1/2) -> (0, -1/6, +1/2).

(6) **B1 coefficient transformation (Theorem 7.2).** The B1 hypercharge coefficients transform as

    (0, +1/2, +1/2) -> (0, +1/2, -1/2).

(7) **Sector value swap (Theorem 7.3).** The sector hypercharge values transform by swapping Omega^0 and Omega^2:

    Y_A1: (-2/3, +1/6, +1/3) -> (+1/3, +1/6, -2/3),
    Y_B1: (+1, -1/2, 0) -> (0, -1/2, +1).

(8) **Magnitude multiset invariance (Corollary 8.1).** The multiset of hypercharge magnitudes is invariant under sigma.

(9) **Anomaly preservation (Corollary 9.1).** The four one-loop anomaly cancellation conditions and the three cubic gauge anomaly cancellations are invariant under sigma.

(10) **Physical-prediction invariance (Corollary 8.3).** If a framework prediction depends only on the multiset of hypercharge magnitudes and on the D4 structure of the multiplet, then it is invariant under sigma up to the relabeling u_R^c <-> d_R^c and e_R^c <-> nu_R^c. In particular, the CKM magnitudes transform as |V_ij| -> |V_ji| (multiset invariant), the PMNS magnitudes transform as |U_ij| -> |U_ji| (multiset invariant), and the Jarlskog invariant J flips sign.

(11) **Labeling convention (Corollary 10.3).** The sign of O is a labeling convention, fixed by the requirement Y(u_R^c) < Y(d_R^c). Flipping the convention produces an equivalent formulation of the framework.

## 2. Setup

### 2.1 The Hodge complex

The substrate is the periodic square lattice Lambda = Z8 x Z8 with N = 64 sites (HCSM-01). The Hodge complex of differential forms is

    Omega^0 = R^64, Omega^1 = R^128, Omega^2 = R^64,

of total dimension 256 (HCSM-02). The Hodge-Dirac operator D = d + d* satisfies D^2|_{Omega^k} = Delta exactly on each form degree.

At the self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4, the multiplicities are 14 in Omega^0, 28 in Omega^1, and 14 in Omega^2, giving the 56-dimensional Hodge sector

    V_56 = Omega^0|_{lambda=4} + Omega^1|_{lambda=4} + Omega^2|_{lambda=4},

with D4 decomposition (HCSM-02, Theorem 7.1)

    V_56 = 9A1 + 5A2 + 7B1 + 7B2 + 14E.

Dimension check: 9 + 5 + 7 + 7 + 28 = 56.

### 2.2 The Hodge star

The discrete Hodge star on the two-dimensional torus with standard orientation is

    star_0: Omega^0 -> Omega^2, star_0 = id;
    star_1: Omega^1 -> Omega^1, (star_1 omega)_x = omega_y, (star_1 omega)_y = -omega_x;
    star_2: Omega^2 -> Omega^0, star_2 = id.

In block form on Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2,

    star = [[0, 0, star_0],
            [0, star_1, 0],
            [star_2, 0, 0]].

The Hodge star satisfies star_0^2 = +I_{Omega^0}, star_1^2 = -I_{Omega^1}, star_2^2 = +I_{Omega^2}, and commutes with D^2 = Delta (HCSM-03).

### 2.3 The D4 action

The dihedral group D4 of order 8 acts on Lambda by the point group of the square, generated by the rotation r: (n_1, n_2) -> (-n_2, n_1) mod 8 and the reflection s: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_1, -n_2) mod 8, subject to r^4 = s^2 = (rs)^2 = e.

### 2.4 Sector operators

**Definition 2.1 (Sector operators).** On V_56, define the three sector operators

    I = P_{Omega^0} + P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^2},
    F = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^2},
    O = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^2}.

Restricted to the three sectors (Omega^0, Omega^1, Omega^2), their signatures are

    I|_{sectors} = (1, 1, 1),
    F|_{sectors} = (1, -1, 1),
    O|_{sectors} = (1, 0, -1).

### 2.5 The hypercharge functional

The hypercharge functional is a specific element of the 15-dimensional sector-coarse space (HCSM-07):

    Y = sum_rho (alpha_rho * I + beta_rho * F + gamma_rho * O) P_rho,

with A1 and B1 coefficients uniquely determined by the Standard Model content and the sector assignment:

    (alpha_A1, beta_A1, gamma_A1) = (0, -1/6, -1/2),
    (alpha_B1, beta_B1, gamma_B1) = (0, +1/2, +1/2).

The corresponding sector hypercharge values are

    Y_A1: (-2/3, +1/6, +1/3),
    Y_B1: (+1, -1/2, 0).

## 3. The sector flip sigma

**Definition 3.1 (Sector flip).** The sector flip sigma: V_56 -> V_56 is the R-linear map

    sigma(alpha_0 + omega_1 + alpha_2) = star_0 alpha_2 + omega_1 + star_2 alpha_0,

where alpha_0 in Omega^0|_{lambda=4}, omega_1 in Omega^1|_{lambda=4}, alpha_2 in Omega^2|_{lambda=4}, and star_0, star_2 are the Hodge star maps.

**Remark 3.2 (Simplification).** Because star_0 and star_2 are the identity maps on their respective sectors (HCSM-02), the flip takes the simple form

    sigma(alpha_0, omega_1, alpha_2) = (alpha_2, omega_1, alpha_0).

That is, sigma swaps the Omega^0 and Omega^2 sectors while fixing Omega^1 pointwise.

**Remark 3.3 (Topological character).** The flip sigma is defined using the Hodge star, which depends only on the orientation of the torus, not on a metric. It is therefore a topological symmetry: it is insensitive to continuous deformations of the metric and depends only on the cohomological (de Rham) structure of the Hodge complex. This is the "gauge topology" content of the paper's title.

## 4. Involution

**Lemma 4.1 (Involution).** sigma^2 = I_56.

**Proof.** For v = (alpha_0, omega_1, alpha_2) in V_56,

    sigma^2(v) = sigma(alpha_2, omega_1, alpha_0) = (alpha_0, omega_1, alpha_2) = v.

Hence sigma is an involution. []

**Corollary 4.2 (Z2 action).** sigma generates a Z2 action on V_56: the group Z2 = {e, sigma} acts on V_56 by e . v = v and sigma . v = sigma(v).

**Remark 4.3 (Non-degeneracy).** sigma != I_56, since sigma exchanges nontrivial subspaces Omega^0|_{lambda=4} != Omega^2|_{lambda=4} (as subspaces of V_56). The Z2 action is therefore faithful.

## 5. D4-equivariance

**Theorem 5.1 (D4-equivariance).** The sector flip commutes with the D4 action on V_56:

    [sigma, R] = [sigma, S] = 0.

**Proof.** It suffices to check the two generators R = rho(r) and S = rho(s) on each sector.

*On Omega^0 + Omega^2.* The D4 action on Omega^0 and Omega^2 are site-permutation actions induced by the point group of the square. Because the Hodge star identification Omega^0 = Omega^2 is D4-equivariant (HCSM-02, Proposition 2.5), the two actions coincide under this identification. Hence for v = (alpha_0, 0, alpha_2),

    sigma R v = sigma(R alpha_0, 0, R alpha_2) = (R alpha_2, 0, R alpha_0) = R sigma v.

The same computation holds for S.

*On Omega^1.* sigma acts trivially on Omega^1, so

    sigma R v = sigma(R omega_1) = R omega_1 = R sigma v

for v = (0, omega_1, 0). The same holds for S.

Combining the two cases, sigma commutes with R and S on V_56. []

**Corollary 5.2 (Sector-coarse compatibility).** The flip sigma preserves the D4 isotypic structure of V_56. In particular, sigma commutes with the isotypic projectors P_rho for all rho in {A1, A2, B1, B2, E}.

## 6. Sector-operator transformation

**Theorem 6.1 (Sector-operator transformation).** Under the flip sigma, the sector operators transform as

    sigma I sigma^{-1} = I,
    sigma F sigma^{-1} = F,
    sigma O sigma^{-1} = -O.

**Proof.** The sector projections transform under sigma as

    sigma P_{Omega^0} sigma^{-1} = P_{Omega^2},
    sigma P_{Omega^1} sigma^{-1} = P_{Omega^1},
    sigma P_{Omega^2} sigma^{-1} = P_{Omega^0}.

(Conjugating P_{Omega^0} by sigma gives the projector onto the image sigma(Omega^0) = Omega^2, and similarly for the other sectors.)

Then:

    sigma I sigma^{-1} = P_{Omega^2} + P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^0} = I,
    sigma F sigma^{-1} = P_{Omega^2} - P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^0} = F,
    sigma O sigma^{-1} = P_{Omega^2} - P_{Omega^0} = -O.

[]

**Corollary 6.2 (Algebraic transformation).** The flip sigma acts on the sector-coarse algebra as the involution

    (alpha, beta, gamma) -> (alpha, beta, -gamma)

on the coefficients of alpha I + beta F + gamma O.

**Proof.** Immediate from Theorem 6.1: the span of {I, F, O} is preserved by sigma, with the stated action on coordinates. []

## 7. Hypercharge functional transformation

**Theorem 7.1 (A1 coefficient transformation).** Under sigma, the A1 hypercharge coefficients transform as

    (alpha_A1, beta_A1, gamma_A1) = (0, -1/6, -1/2) -> (0, -1/6, +1/2).

**Proof.** The flip acts on the sector-coarse algebra as (alpha, beta, gamma) -> (alpha, beta, -gamma). Applying this to the A1 coefficients:

    (0, -1/6, -1/2) -> (0, -1/6, +1/2).

The projectors P_rho are invariant under sigma (Theorem 5.1), so the action on the full functional is well-defined. []

**Theorem 7.2 (B1 coefficient transformation).** Under sigma, the B1 hypercharge coefficients transform as

    (alpha_B1, beta_B1, gamma_B1) = (0, +1/2, +1/2) -> (0, +1/2, -1/2).

**Proof.** Same as Theorem 7.1 applied to the B1 coefficients. []

**Theorem 7.3 (Sector-value swap).** Under sigma, the sector hypercharge values transform by swapping Omega^0 and Omega^2:

    Y_A1: (-2/3, +1/6, +1/3) -> (+1/3, +1/6, -2/3),
    Y_B1: (+1, -1/2, 0) -> (0, -1/2, +1).

**Proof.** Direct evaluation of the transformed coefficients on the sector signatures.

For A1 with transformed coefficients (0, -1/6, +1/2):

    Y'_A1(Omega^0) = 0 + (-1/6) + (+1/2) = +1/3,
    Y'_A1(Omega^1) = 0 - (-1/6) = +1/6,
    Y'_A1(Omega^2) = 0 + (-1/6) - (+1/2) = -2/3.

So Y_A1 on (Omega^0, Omega^1, Omega^2) transforms from (-2/3, +1/6, +1/3) to (+1/3, +1/6, -2/3).

For B1 with transformed coefficients (0, +1/2, -1/2):

    Y'_B1(Omega^0) = 0 + (+1/2) + (-1/2) = 0,
    Y'_B1(Omega^1) = 0 - (+1/2) = -1/2,
    Y'_B1(Omega^2) = 0 + (+1/2) - (-1/2) = +1.

So Y_B1 transforms from (+1, -1/2, 0) to (0, -1/2, +1).

In both cases, the transformation is exactly the swap Omega^0 <-> Omega^2. []

## 8. Physical consequences

### 8.1 Magnitude-multiset invariance

**Corollary 8.1 (Magnitude multiset invariant).** The multiset of hypercharge magnitudes

    {|Y_A1(Omega^0)|, |Y_A1(Omega^1)|, |Y_A1(Omega^2)|} = {2/3, 1/6, 1/3}

on the A1 block and

    {|Y_B1(Omega^0)|, |Y_B1(Omega^1)|, |Y_B1(Omega^2)|} = {1, 1/2, 0}

on the B1 block are invariant under sigma.

**Proof.** By Theorem 7.3, the flip swaps Omega^0 and Omega^2 while fixing Omega^1. On the A1 block, the sector values {-2/3, +1/6, +1/3} map to {+1/3, +1/6, -2/3}, which has the same multiset of absolute values. On the B1 block, {+1, -1/2, 0} maps to {0, -1/2, +1}, again with the same multiset of absolute values. []

### 8.2 Relabeling of the color-triplet and lepton multiplets

**Corollary 8.2 (Multiplet relabeling).** Under sigma, the color-triplet multiplet assignment transforms as

    u_R^c <-> d_R^c,

and the lepton-multiplet assignment transforms as

    e_R^c <-> nu_R^c.

The multiplet Q_L (color-triplet, weak doublet) and L_L (color-singlet, weak doublet), both on Omega^1, are invariant.

**Proof.** By Theorem 7.3, sigma swaps Omega^0 and Omega^2. Under the sector assignment (Input I5), u_R^c occupies Omega^0 and d_R^c occupies Omega^2; hence they are swapped. Similarly for e_R^c and nu_R^c on the B1 block (which carry the sector values +1 on Omega^0 and 0 on Omega^2). []

### 8.3 Invariance of physical-prediction magnitudes

**Corollary 8.3 (Physical-prediction invariance).** Any framework prediction that depends only on (i) the multiset of hypercharge magnitudes and (ii) the D4 structure of the multiplet is invariant under sigma up to the relabeling u_R^c <-> d_R^c and e_R^c <-> nu_R^c. In particular:

(a) **CKM magnitudes.** The magnitudes of the CKM matrix transform as

    |V_ij| -> |V_ji|.

The multiset {|V_ij|} is invariant. The equality |V_ij| = |V_ji| does not hold in general; the transformation is a relabeling of the quark generations, not an identity on individual elements.

(b) **PMNS magnitudes.** The magnitudes of the PMNS matrix transform as

    |U_ij| -> |U_ji|.

The multiset {|U_ij|} is invariant.

(c) **Jarlskog invariant.** The Jarlskog invariant J flips sign:

    J = c_12 s_12 c_23 s_23 c_13^2 s_13 sin(delta) -> -J.

The magnitude |J| is preserved.

**Proof.** Under u <-> d exchange, the CKM matrix transforms as V -> V^dagger. The magnitudes satisfy |(V^dagger)_ij| = |V_ji|. The multiset of magnitudes is preserved because the map (i,j) -> (j,i) is a bijection on the index set. The Jarlskog invariant, in the standard PDG parametrization, is J = c_12 s_12 c_23 s_23 c_13^2 s_13 sin(delta). Under complex conjugation of the CKM matrix (equivalent to the u <-> d exchange combined with a phase redefinition of the quark fields), delta -> -delta, so sin(delta) -> -sin(delta). Hence J -> -J. The magnitude |J| is preserved.

The same argument applies to the PMNS matrix U under e <-> nu exchange. []

**Remark 8.4 (Status of the flavor-sector application).** The flavor-sector predictions (CKM and PMNS matrices) are the subject of HCSM-34 and HCSM-36. The corollary above is stated as a symmetry of the HCSM sector-coarse structure, and applies to any framework prediction that inherits its dependence on the SM content through the hypercharge multiset.

The corollary does not claim that CKM and PMNS are derived in HCSM-09; it claims that if they are derived in the flavor sector with the standard dependence on hypercharge magnitudes, then sigma acts on them as V -> V^dagger and U -> U^dagger.

## 9. Anomaly preservation

**Corollary 9.1 (Anomaly preservation).** The four one-loop anomaly cancellation conditions

    Tr(Y) = 0,
    Tr(Y^3) = 0,
    SU(2)^2 U(1) = 0,
    SU(3)^2 U(1) = 0

and the three cubic gauge anomaly cancellations are invariant under sigma.

**Proof.** Under sigma, the sector hypercharge values are permuted: Omega^0 and Omega^2 exchange their values, and Omega^1 is fixed. The anomaly sums are weighted traces over the sector content; the weights depend on the SM representation multiplicities attached to each sector.

For the mixed SU(3)^2 U(1) anomaly, the sector-coarse form is (HCSM-08, Proposition 18)

    Y_0 + 2 Y_1 + Y_2 = 0.

Under sigma, this becomes

    Y_2 + 2 Y_1 + Y_0 = 0.

Since the sum is symmetric in Y_0 and Y_2, the condition is preserved.

For the other three conditions, the invariance follows from the multiset invariance of Corollary 8.1: any symmetric function of the sector hypercharge values that vanishes before sigma also vanishes after. Specifically, Tr(Y), Tr(Y^3), and SU(2)^2 U(1) are all symmetric sums of hypercharge values weighted by sector content, and the flip permutes Omega^0 and Omega^2 without changing the multiset of values.

The cubic gauge anomalies vanish by content structure (HCSM-08, Theorem 11) regardless of the sector assignment, so they are trivially invariant. []

**Remark 9.2 (Physical reading).** The anomaly preservation means that if the HCSM hypercharge functional is anomaly-free with the assignment u_R^c in Omega^0, d_R^c in Omega^2, it is also anomaly-free with the flipped assignment u_R^c in Omega^2, d_R^c in Omega^0. The anomaly cancellation does not distinguish the two assignments.

**Corollary 9.3 (Labeling convention).** The sign of O is a labeling convention, fixed by the requirement Y(u_R^c) < Y(d_R^c). Flipping the convention produces an equivalent formulation of the framework.

**Proof.** Immediate from Corollary 8.3 and Remark 9.2. The two assignments produce identical physical predictions up to the relabeling u_R^c <-> d_R^c, e_R^c <-> nu_R^c. []

### 10.3 What is not claimed

We do not claim to derive the CKM or PMNS matrices. These are the subject of HCSM-34 and HCSM-36. We claim that if the flavor-sector predictions inherit their dependence on the SM content through the multiset of hypercharge magnitudes, then they are invariant under sigma up to the relabeling.

We do not claim to privilege any specific sector assignment. The framework is invariant under sigma: the assignment u_R^c in Omega^0, d_R^c in Omega^2 and the flipped assignment u_R^c in Omega^2, d_R^c in Omega^0 produce equivalent formulations. The convention Y(u_R^c) < Y(d_R^c) is a labeling convention, not a physical content.

## 12. Conclusion

This paper has established the orientation-flip Z2 symmetry of the HCSM sector-coarse structure. The flip sigma, defined by the Hodge-star identification of the outer sectors Omega^0 = Omega^2, is a well-defined involution on the 56-dimensional Hodge sector that commutes with D4 and swaps Omega^0 <-> Omega^2 while fixing Omega^1.

Under sigma, the sector operators transform as I -> I, F -> F, and O -> -O. The hypercharge coefficients transform as (0, -1/6, -1/2) -> (0, -1/6, +1/2) on the A1 block and (0, +1/2, +1/2) -> (0, +1/2, -1/2) on the B1 block. The sector hypercharge values swap Omega^0 <-> Omega^2, exchanging u_R^c <-> d_R^c and e_R^c <-> nu_R^c.

All framework predictions of the magnitudes of physical observables are invariant under sigma. The hypercharge magnitude multiset is preserved, the anomaly cancellation conditions are preserved, and (conditional on the flavor-sector dependence) the CKM and PMNS element magnitudes transform as |V_ij| -> |V_ji| and |U_ij| -> |U_ji| (multisets invariant) while the Jarlskog invariant flips sign. The framework does not privilege Omega^0 over Omega^2: the two outer sectors are Hodge-dual, carry identical D4 decompositions, and are distinguished only by the labeling convention for the orientation operator O.

The framework derives the space of hypercharge functionals (HCSM-06), the coefficient values (HCSM-07), the anomaly cancellation (HCSM-08), and the Z2 symmetry that acts on all of them (this paper). The Z2 symmetry closes the gauge-sector structure of the framework.
