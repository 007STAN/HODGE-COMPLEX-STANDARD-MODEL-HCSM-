ABSTRACT
================================================================================

We derive time and its arrow in the Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM)
as internal structures of the five-layer loop. The loop operator

    M_loop = T * L_EFT * L_Core * L_DME * L_FDLM

acts diagonally on the 14-mode multiplet with polar form
M_loop(m) = r_m e^{i theta_m}. The complex time of mode m is

    tau_m = theta_m + i ln r_m
          = omega_m T_DME - i(-ln r_m),

where omega_m is the winding-generator frequency,
T_DME = 1/(2 gamma) is the DME relaxation time, and r_m is the per-pass
amplitude. The real part theta_m is the clock; the imaginary part
-ln r_m > 0 is the arrow increment. The processor T is exactly the
projection onto modes with Re(tau) > 0.

Four structural bridges are established:

  (i)   The floor function in the Core perturbation is the winding
        number of the DME-Core interface phase over one loop pass; it
        is not an ansatz.

  (ii)  Dimensional homogeneity of tau_m is proved.

  (iii) The exact ratio |M_A|/|M_B| = 3 = d-1 is a consequence of the
        closed-form T_DME from HCSM-54.

  (iv)  The loop-pass duration selection is closed: T_DME is the unique
        dissipative scale in HCSM, and the processor T at the
        deterministic Landauer bound requires dissipation. The coherent
        T_beat is rejected because it carries no entropy transfer.

1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.1 The time problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Standard Model has no fundamental arrow of time. HCSM supplies a
closed five-layer loop with preferred direction and dissipative
operator. Time is a readout of the loop, not an input.

Two aspects emerge:

  (1) Clock time -- the phase accumulated by M_loop per pass.
  (2) Arrow time -- the directed decay of M_loop^k because |M_j| < 1.

1.2 Summary of results
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) M_loop diagonal, tau_m = omega_m T_DME + i ln r_m.
(2) Floor function = winding number of DME-Core interface.
(3) Dimensional homogeneity of tau_m.
(4) T = Pi_{Re(tau) > 0}.
(5) Arrow of time.
(6) Loop-pass duration T_pass = T_DME = 1/(2 gamma) (Landauer).

12. THE LANDAUER SELECTION OF T_DME
================================================================================

Prior versions of this paper left the loop-pass duration selection open:
both T_DME = 1/(2 gamma) (DME relaxation) and T_beat = pi/sqrt(2)
(Level-1 internal period) are dimensionless time-like quantities. This
section closes that item.

Theorem 12.1 (Loop-pass duration).

  T_pass = T_DME = 1 / (2 gamma) = 0.711400003516965862...

Proof. The loop contains the processor T = I - P_C (HCSM-13), a Maxwell
demon operating at the deterministic Landauer bound. The processor takes
the maximally mixed state on the 56-dim sector (S_in = log 56) to the
factorized state on 10 + 4 modes (S_out = log 40), with entropy
reduction Delta S = log(7/5) = 0.336472... nats.

Landauer's principle requires that this entropy reduction be
compensated by heat dissipation into the residual bath. Dissipation
requires a dissipative rate. Among the HCSM-native scales:

  (i)  T_DME = 1/(2 gamma) is derived from the gradient flow
       d eps/d lambda = -2 gamma eps (HCSM-54). It is the unique
       dissipative scale in HCSM.

  (ii) T_beat = pi/sqrt(2) is a coherent standing-wave period of the
       winding generator. It carries no entropy transfer and no
       irreversibility.

Because the loop is a measurement, it is irreversible; because it is
irreversible, it must dissipate; because it dissipates, its time unit
must be the dissipative time T_DME. The coherent T_beat is rejected. [Box]

Remark 12.2 (Tautology of the ratio).
  T_beat / T_DME = pi sqrt(2) gamma = 3.1226334...
is a tautology given the definitions of both scales. The selection is
physical, via the Landauer argument above.

Remark 12.3 (No other dissipative scale).
  HCSM-native scales: T_DME (dissipative), T_beat (coherent),
  T_lattice = 1/L, T_site = 1/N. Only T_DME carries entropy transfer.
  Rank 7 is closed.

15. FALSIFIABLE PREDICTIONS
================================================================================

1.  tau_A and tau_B exactly as stated; a 1e-10 deviation falsifies.
2.  T = Pi_{Re(tau) > 0}: a mode with Re(tau) > 0 outside im(T)
    (or vice versa) falsifies.

4.  Kernel freezing: any nonzero time evolution of {dark, W, Z, H}
    falsifies.
5.  Loop-pass duration: any experimental or structural result requiring
    the pass duration to be T_beat falsifies Theorem 12.1.

of the loop operator,

    tau_m = omega_m T_DME - i(-ln r_m).

Its real part is the clock; its imaginary part is the arrow. The
processor T is the projection onto nonzero real time.

Four algebraic bridges are now closed. First, the floor function is the
winding number of the DME-Core interface. Second, the exact ratio

homogeneity of complex time. Fourth, the loop-pass duration T_DME is
