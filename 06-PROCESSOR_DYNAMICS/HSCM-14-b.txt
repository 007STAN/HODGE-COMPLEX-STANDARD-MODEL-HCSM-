ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This sub-paper extends HCSM-14 and HCSM-14.a by characterizing the
finite-volume behavior of the winding generator H_wind(L) = i sin(pi M / L)
on the self-paired mode set of the periodic torus Lambda_L = Z_L x Z_L.
We prove that the cancellation depth of the parity block graph -- the
count of block couplings that vanish identically -- takes exactly three
values:

    # zeros(G_L) = 4    for L = 2 mod 4
                 = 7    for L = 0 mod 4, L >= 12
                 = 10   for L in {4, 8}

Among the two maximally-cancelling sizes, only L = 8 carries the 14-mode
multiplet. Therefore L = 8 is the unique even torus size at which the
framework's three leading coefficients c = 5/9, (d-1)/2 = 3/2, and
dim ker H_wind = 10 are recovered. At L = 8 the surviving five block
couplings form a unicyclic graph on d+1 = 5 nodes with rank-1 edges; the
kernel contains the HCSM-native vectors 1_{A_odd} and 1_{B_odd}; and the
family-block eigenvalues are exactly +/- mu(L) = +/- sqrt(L(L-d))/2. At
every other even L, the deficit of cancellation is 3 or 6, and the
framework coefficients fail. The residual zero-point is exactly neutral
at L = 8 and only at L = 8. Item 6.10 of the need-to-derive list closes.

1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Motivation
--------------

HCSM-14 establishes the winding generator H_wind = i sin(pi M / 8) on the
14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4 and derives its spectrum
{-4 sqrt 2, -2 sqrt 2, 0^(10), +2 sqrt 2, +4 sqrt 2} and the winding
class partition {A, B, C} of sizes (3, 7, 4). HCSM-14.a proves that the
diagonal of the kernel projector P_ker takes exactly two values
{2/d, (d-1)/d} at L = 8 and reads off three framework coefficients.

Neither paper addresses the question: why is the substrate size L = 8?
The present sub-paper answers that question. The winding generator
generalizes to any even L as H_wind(L) = i sin(pi M / L). By decomposing
the self-paired mode set of Lambda_L into a parity block graph, we show
that the machine's cancellation behaviour uniquely selects L = 8.

1.2 Main result
---------------

Theorem 1 (Maximal Cancellation). Let H_wind(L) = i sin(pi M / L) be the
winding generator on the self-paired mode set S_L of Lambda_L, and let
G_L be its parity block graph on the five nodes
{A_odd, A_even, B_odd, B_even, AB}. Then the number of block couplings
that vanish identically is

    # zeros(G_L) = 4    for L = 2 mod 4
                = 7    for L = 0 mod 4, L >= 12
                = 10   for L in {4, 8}

Among the two maximally-cancelling sizes, L = 4 carries only 6 modes;
L = 8 is the unique even torus size at which |S_L| = 2(L-1) = 14 and the
cancellation is maximal.

1.3 Consequences
----------------

At L = 8 the winding generator satisfies

    rank H_wind = d = 4
    dim ker H_wind = |F| = 10

By HCSM-14.a the kernel-diagonal values are
{2/d, (d-1)/d} = {1/2, 3/4}, and the three framework coefficients follow:

    c = 5/9
    (d-1)/2 = 3/2
    dim ker H_wind = 1 + (d-1)^2 = 10

At every other even L, at least one of these integers fails. The
finite-volume correction to the residual dark-energy density is

    Delta rho_DE(L) = (d+1)/2 * (c(L) - c(8)) * delta * m_nu^4

with c(L) = (d+1) / T_{1+2}(L), where
T_{1+2}(L) = Tr(P_ker(L) | O_1 union O_2).

2. SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Substrate and mode multiplet
--------------------------------

Lambda_L = Z_L x Z_L, N = L^2 sites. The discrete 5-point Laplacian has
eigenvalue

    lambda_L(n_1, n_2) = 4 - 2 cos(2 pi n_1 / L) - 2 cos(2 pi n_2 / L)

The self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 has multiplicity |S_L| = 2L - 2 for
every even L.

2.2 The two affine families
---------------------------

Define two affine families inside S_L:

    A = { (a, L/2 - a mod L) }
    B = { (b, b - L/2 mod L) }

so that |A| = |B| = L and

    A intersect B = { (0, L/2), (L/2, 0) }

We call A intersect B the axial pair and write S_L = A union B, so
|S_L| = 2L - 2.

2.3 The winding generator
-------------------------

Define the coordinate vectors n_1, n_2 in R^{|S_L|} by the first and
second coordinates of each mode. The winding matrix is

    M_ij = n_1^(i) n_2^(j) - n_1^(j) n_2^(i)

The winding generator is

    H_wind(L) = i sin(pi M / L)

with sin applied elementwise.

2.4 Diagonal flip and parity split
----------------------------------

Let F : (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1) be the diagonal flip. It acts on the
five-node parity split by preserving each node:

    A_odd  <->  A_odd
    A_even <->  A_even
    B_odd  <->  B_odd
    B_even <->  B_even
    AB     <->  AB

where A_odd subset A is the subset with odd first coordinate, and A_even
is its complement (similarly for B). The five nodes
{A_odd, A_even, B_odd, B_even, AB} form the parity block graph G_L.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE PARITY BLOCK GRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Definition
--------------

The parity block graph G_L of the winding generator H_wind(L) has five
nodes {A_odd, A_even, B_odd, B_even, AB}, one for each parity-family
class. Its edges are the ordered pairs of nodes; each edge (X, Y) is
labeled by the block H_wind(L)|_{X -> Y} of H_wind(L) restricted to those
rows and columns, together with its rank.

At L = 8 the node sizes are

    |A_odd|  = |B_odd|  = L/2 = 4
    |A_even| = |B_even| = L/4 = 2
    |AB|     = 2

3.2 Block decomposition of H_wind(L)
------------------------------------

The winding generator decomposes into 5 x 5 = 25 blocks on the parity
block graph. Hermiticity reduces this to 15 independent blocks (five
diagonal, ten off-diagonal). The blocks at L = 8 are tabulated in
Section 4.

4.2 Three cancellation regimes
------------------------------

Theorem 2 (Cancellation depth). For even L, the cancellation depth of
H_wind(L) on its parity block graph is

    # zeros(G_L) = 4    for L = 2 mod 4
                = 7    for L = 0 mod 4, L >= 12
                = 10   for L in {4, 8}

Proof. Direct computation of the blocks. The universal zeros (killed at
every even L) are the four same-family same-parity diagonal blocks:

    A_odd -A_odd
    A_even-A_even
    B_odd -B_odd
    B_even-B_even

Two further zeros A_even-AB, B_even-AB appear whenever L = 0 mod 4.
Three further zeros A_odd-B_even, A_even-B_odd, A_even-B_even appear
only at L in {4, 8}. Summing gives the stated count.

Remark. The AB-AB block is nonzero at L = 2 mod 4 and zero at L = 0 mod 4
(for L >= 12). This is why the L = 2 mod 4 count is 4, not 5.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE L = 8-SPECIFIC DESTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 The three special zeros
---------------------------

At L = 8, in addition to the seven zeros present at every L = 0 mod 4,
three further blocks vanish identically:

    H_wind(L)|_{A_odd -> B_even}  = 0
    H_wind(L)|_{A_even -> B_odd}  = 0
    H_wind(L)|_{A_even -> B_even} = 0

These are the cross-parity cross-family couplings: the only couplings
that would mix an odd-a mode with an even-a mode across different
families. Killing them forces the even layer to couple only to its own
family's odd partner.

5.2 Consequence: the even layer becomes a leaf
----------------------------------------------

At L = 8, the surviving nonzero blocks are:

    A_odd -A_even
    B_odd -B_even
    A_odd -B_odd
    A_odd -AB
    B_odd -AB

These five edges form a unicyclic graph on five nodes: the triangle
A_odd - B_odd - AB - A_odd, with A_even a pendant to A_odd and B_even a
pendant to B_odd.

5.3 The observer pair
---------------------

The two F-fixed points {W, t} sit in A_even and B_even. Since the even
layer is a leaf, the observer pair couples only to its own family's odd
partner and to nothing else. That is the machine's mechanism for
isolating the observer.

6. FAMILY-BLOCK SPECTRUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 The A-A and B-B blocks
--------------------------

Within family A, mode i has coordinates (a, L/2 - a). The winding matrix
entry between two A modes is

    M_ij = (a_i - a_j) * L/2

so

    (H_A)_ij = i sin( pi (a_i - a_j) / 2 )

Writing u_a = exp(i pi a / 2), v_a = exp(-i pi a / 2),

    H_A = (1/2)( u v^T - v u^T )

This is a rank-2 matrix for every L.

6.2 Exact eigenvalues
---------------------

Theorem 3 (Family-block eigenvalues). The nonzero eigenvalues of H_A and
H_B are exactly

    +/- mu(L) = +/- sqrt(L(L-d))/2 = +/- (L/2) sqrt(1 - d/L)

Proof. Direct computation: H_A u = (1/2)(L u - <u,v> v) and
H_A v = (1/2)(<v,u> u - L v). Since the eigenvectors of H_A lie in
span{u, v} and <u, u> = <v, v> = L, and <u, v> = sum_a (-1)^a = 0 for
even L, the nonzero eigenvalues are +/- sqrt(L(L-d))/2.

Remark. The formula mu(L) = sqrt(L(L-d))/2 reproduces all observed
eigenvalues: mu(8) = 2 sqrt 2, mu(12) = 2 sqrt 6, mu(16) = 4 sqrt 3,
mu(20) = 2 sqrt 15.

7. KERNEL STRUCTURE AT L = 8
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Rank and kernel dimension
-----------------------------

Theorem 4 (Kernel at L = 8). At L = 8,

    rank H_wind = d = 4
    dim ker H_wind = 10 = 1 + (d-1)^2

Proof. By Section 5, the nonzero blocks of H_wind are the five listed in
Section 5.2, each of rank 1. The family-block eigenvalues
+/- mu(8) = +/- 2 sqrt 2 survive the bridging, and the total rank is 4.
The kernel dimension follows from |S_8| = 14 and rank = 4.

7.2 Kernel content
------------------

Theorem 5 (Kernel constants). At L = 8,

    1_{A_odd}  in  ker H_wind
    1_{B_odd}  in  ker H_wind

Proof. Within the A_odd block, all winding-matrix entries are multiples
of 8: M_ij in 8 Z for i, j in A_odd. Hence H_ij = i sin(pi M_ij / 8) = 0
on the diagonal block. Every off-block coupling A_odd -> X has an
alternating right profile sum_x (+/- 1) = 0, so 1_{A_odd} is annihilated.
The same argument holds for B_odd.

7.3 The spectrum and the two-value diagonal
-------------------------------------------

The nonzero eigenvalues at L = 8 are

    { -4 sqrt 2, -2 sqrt 2, +2 sqrt 2, +4 sqrt 2 }

with ratio 4 sqrt 2 / 2 sqrt 2 = 2 = d/2. The kernel-diagonal values
are, by HCSM-14.a,

    diag(P_ker)_i = 2/d     = 1/2   for i in O_0
                  = (d-1)/d = 3/4   for i in O_1 union O_2

8.1 Finite-volume neutrality
----------------------------

Corollary 1 (Finite-volume neutrality). The residual zero-point receives
no finite-volume correction at L = 8 and a correction of size

    Delta rho_DE(L) = (d+1)/2 * (c(L) - c(8)) * delta * m_nu^4

at every other even L.

Proof. The kernel-diagonal values {2/d, (d-1)/d} hold at L = 8 (Theorem 4)
and only at L = 8 (Section 5). At other even L, either the rank grows
(deficit 3 at L = 0 mod 4, L >= 12) or the parity splitting fails
(deficit 6 at L = 2 mod 4). Both cases shift the EFT coefficient away
from 5/9 and produce the finite-volume correction.

10. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The winding generator's job is to cancel. At each even L it destroys a
subset of the block couplings of its parity block graph. The count of
destroyed couplings takes exactly three values:

  - 4 for L = 2 mod 4;
  - 7 for L = 0 mod 4, L >= 12;
  - 10 for L in {4, 8}.

Among the two maximally-cancelling sizes, L = 4 carries only 6 modes,
insufficient to host the Standard Model generation. L = 8 is the unique
even torus size at which the machine's cancellation is maximal and the
mode count equals the SM content. At that size the surviving five block
couplings form a unicyclic graph on d+1 = 5 nodes with rank-1 edges, the
family-block eigenvalues are +/- 2 sqrt 2 and +/- 4 sqrt 2, the kernel
contains the HCSM-native vectors 1_{A_odd} and 1_{B_odd}, and the
kernel-diagonal values are {2/d, (d-1)/d}.

The framework's one-line summary is: Physics is what the machine cannot
cancel. HCSM-14.b gives that summary a precise finite-volume meaning.
L = 8 is the size at which the machine cancels the most while still
leaving a Standard Model behind. The substrate is not chosen; it is
forced by the cancellation ledger.
