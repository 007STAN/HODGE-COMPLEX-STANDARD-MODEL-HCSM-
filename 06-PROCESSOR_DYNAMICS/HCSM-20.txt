The self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 has multiplicity 14. The mode set is

    K_14 = {(0,4),(4,0),(1,3),(1,5),(2,2),(2,6),(3,1),(3,7),

2.2 The two coordinate vectors
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    M = n_1 n_2^T - n_2 n_1^T,

The entries of phi are even-integer multiples of pi/8, hence multiples

3. THE WINDING GENERATOR AND ITS SPECTRUM
================================================================================

3.1 Definition and basic properties
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Definition 3.1 (Winding generator). The winding generator on C^14 is

    H_wind = i sin(phi),                                             (6)

where sin is applied elementwise.

Lemma 3.2 (Entry structure). The matrix W = sin(phi) is real antisymmetric
with entries in {0, +/- 1/sqrt(2), +/- 1}.

Proof. phi_ij = (pi/8) M_ij with M_ij even integers. Thus
(pi/8) M_ij = (pi/4)(M_ij/2) is an integer multiple of pi/4. The values
of sin at integer multiples of pi/4 are {0, +/- 1/sqrt(2), +/- 1}. [Box]

Since phi is real antisymmetric, sin(phi) is real antisymmetric, and
H_wind = i sin(phi) is Hermitian with entries in Q(sqrt(2)).

3.2 Trace identities
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lemma 3.3 (Trace identities). Let H_wind = i W. Then

    tr(H_wind) = 0,
    tr(H_wind^2) = 80,
    tr(H_wind^3) = 0,
    tr(H_wind^4) = 2176.                                             (7)

Proof. Direct computation on the explicit 14 x 14 matrix. Since H_wind is
Hermitian with real eigenvalues, the traces equal sum_i lambda_i^k.
Alternatively, tr(H_wind^2) = ||W||_F^2 = sum_{i,j} W_ij^2. Since
W_ij in {0, +/- 1/sqrt(2), +/- 1}, we have W_ij^2 in {0, 1/2, 1}. Direct
evaluation gives sum_{i,j} W_ij^2 = 80. Similarly,
tr(H_wind^4) = sum_{i,j} (W^2)_ij^2 = 2176. Verified at machine precision
in Appendix A. [Box]

3.3 Characteristic polynomial
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 3.4 (Characteristic polynomial). The characteristic polynomial of
H_wind is

    p(lambda) = det(lambda I - H_wind)
              = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8) (lambda^2 - 32)
              = lambda^14 - 40 lambda^12 + 256 lambda^10.             (8)

Proof. Write p(lambda) = lambda^14 + a_1 lambda^13 + a_2 lambda^12 + ...
Newton's identities relate the coefficients to the power sums
p_k = tr(H_wind^k):

    a_1 = -p_1 = 0.
    2 a_2 = -(p_2 + a_1 p_1) = -80,   a_2 = -40.
    3 a_3 = -(p_3 + a_1 p_2 + a_2 p_1) = 0,   a_3 = 0.
    4 a_4 = -(p_4 + a_1 p_3 + a_2 p_2 + a_3 p_1)
          = -(2176 - 3200) = 1024,   a_4 = 256.

Direct computation verifies that a_5 = ... = a_14 = 0 (equivalently, that
the eigenvalues of H_wind are roots of lambda^10 (lambda^4 - 40 lambda^2
+ 256) and no others). The quartic factors as
(lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 - 32), giving the roots. [Box]

Corollary 3.5 (Spectrum). The spectrum of H_wind is

    spec(H_wind) = {-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^(10), +2 sqrt(2), +4 sqrt(2)}   (9)

with multiplicities 1, 1, 10, 1, 1.

4.1 The position operator on K_14
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Let v_i(x,y) = cos(2 pi (n_1^{(i)} x + n_2^{(i)} y)/8) / ||.|| be the
normalized position-space mode vectors on the torus. Define the position
operator on K_14 by

    (X_1)_ij = <v_i | x_hat | v_j> = sum_{x,y=0}^{7} x v_i(x,y) v_j(x,y).   (11)

Lemma 4.1 (Position matrix entries). Every entry (X_1)_ij is a rational
number of the form p/4 with p in Z.

Proof. Use cos A cos B = (1/2)[cos(A-B) + cos(A+B)]. The sum over y
vanishes unless the y-frequency is zero modulo 8. The sum over x of
x cos(2 pi b x / 8) for integer b is either 28 (when b = 0 mod 8) or a
finite algebraic sum. Direct evaluation on the 14 modes gives the stated
rational form. [Box]

4.2 Fourier expansion of the trajectory
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Let psi_j(t) = e^{i H_wind t} e_j and
<x>_j(t) = <psi_j(t) | X_1 | psi_j(t)>.

Theorem 4.2 (Fourier expansion). The trajectory
<x>_j(t) - <x>_j(0) has the Fourier expansion

    <x>_j(t) - <x>_j(0)
        = sum_{omega in Omega_j} 2 Re[B_j(omega) e^{i omega t}],       (12)

where Omega_j is a subset of {2 sqrt(2), 4 sqrt(2), 6 sqrt(2)} and the
complex coefficients

    B_j(omega) = sum_{lambda_l = lambda_k = omega} p_k^* p_l (X_1)_kl,
        p_k = <v_k | j>,                                                (13)

satisfy B_j(omega) in Q(sqrt(2)).

Proof. Since H_wind is Hermitian, its eigenvectors form an orthonormal
basis of C^14. Diagonalizing H_wind = V Lambda V^dagger gives
e^{i H_wind t} = V e^{i Lambda t} V^dagger, from which
<x>_j(t) = sum_{k,l} p_k^* p_l (X_1)_kl e^{i (lambda_l - lambda_k) t}.
All frequencies are integer multiples of 2 sqrt(2), so Omega_j is a
subset of {2 sqrt(2), 4 sqrt(2), 6 sqrt(2)}. The eigenvectors have
components in Q(sqrt(2)); (X_1)_kl in Q by Lemma 4.1. Hence
B_j(omega) in Q(sqrt(2)). [Box]

6.1 The five layers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Motion is the composition of five layers of the substrate, each with a
well-defined state space and a unique operator:

    Layer 1: FDLM/WIN,  C^14 mode vectors, Hermitian (unitary evolution)
    Layer 2: DME,       eps in R,          scalar constraint
    Layer 3: Core,      eps(lambda),       gradient flow
    Layer 4: EFT,       field config,      damped wave equation
    Layer 5: Processor T, C^14 projected,  orthogonal projection

6.2 The loop operator
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The loop operator is

    M_loop = T * L_EFT * L_Core * L_DME * L_FDLM.                    (18)

On the mode basis, M_loop is diagonal:

    M_loop(m) = r_m e^{i theta_m}.                                   (19)

The five layers are not temporally sequential within a pass. They are
algebraically composed.

6.3 The per-layer eigenvalues
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lemma 6.1 (Per-layer eigenvalues). For each surviving mode m,

    |L_FDLM(m)| = 1,
    |L_DME(m)|  = 1,
    |L_Core(m)| = e^{-2 gamma lambda_EFT(m)}
                = |w+1| / eps_0(m)
                = |w+1| / (|omega_m T_DME| delta_edge),
    |L_EFT(m)|  = e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2}.                    (20)

Proof. The FDLM layer is unitary (|L_FDLM(m)| = 1 at the beat period,
Theorem 4.5). The DME layer is a scalar constraint and contributes no
amplitude reduction. The Core layer satisfies eps(lambda) = eps_0
e^{-2 gamma lambda} with lambda_EFT(m) chosen so that
eps(lambda_EFT(m)) = |w+1|; hence
|L_Core(m)| = |w+1| / eps_0(m) with
eps_0(m) = floor(omega_m T_DME) delta_edge (see WIN-Motion for the
derivation of the floor rule from the DME-Core winding condition). The
EFT layer contributes the per-beat decay factor
e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2}. [Box]

6.4 Closed-form eigenvalue magnitudes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 6.2 (Loop-eigenvalue magnitudes). For each surviving mode m,

    |M_loop(m)| = |w+1| / (|omega_m T_DME| delta_edge)
                  * e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2}.                  (21)

For m in ker T, M_loop(m) = 0.

Proof. Multiplying the per-layer magnitudes from Lemma 6.1 gives the
result. For m in ker T, the processor T projects to zero, so
M_loop(m) = 0. [Box]

Corollary 6.3 (Exact forms). For the two non-trivial classes,

    |M_B| = |w+1| / (6 delta_edge) * e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2}.   (22)

Numerically, |M_A| = 0.575684... and |M_B| = 0.191895...

7. THE EXACT RATIO |M_A| / |M_B| = 3
================================================================================

Theorem 7.1 (Exact ratio). The ratio of the two non-trivial
loop-eigenvalue magnitudes is exactly

    |M_A| / |M_B| = 3 = d - 1.                                       (23)

The framework constants |w+1|, delta_edge, and the EFT damping factor do
not appear in the ratio.

Proof. From the loop-decay decomposition,

    r_m = |w+1| / eps_0(m) * D_factor,
        eps_0(m) = n_m delta_edge,
        n_m = floor(omega_m T_DME).                                  (24)

Therefore

    r_A / r_B = n_B / n_A.                                           (25)

Now compute n_A and n_B exactly:

    omega_A T_DME = 2 sqrt(2) / (2 gamma) = sqrt(2) / gamma,
    omega_B T_DME = 6 sqrt(2) / (2 gamma) = 3 sqrt(2) / gamma.       (26)

With gamma = c_2 / I(2) from HCSM-54:

    sqrt(2) / gamma = 2 + delta_edge,
    3 sqrt(2) / gamma = 6 + 3 delta_edge,
    delta_edge = sqrt(2) / gamma - 2 = 0.012143...                   (27)

Since 0 < delta_edge < 1/3,

    n_A = floor(2 + delta_edge) = 2,
    n_B = floor(6 + 3 delta_edge) = 6.                               (28)

Hence r_A / r_B = n_B / n_A = 6/2 = 3. [Box]

Remark 7.2 (Relation to the exact generation count). The ratio
|M_A| / |M_B| = 3 has a natural interpretation: the two loop classes A
and B have amplitudes in the ratio 1:3, matching the ratio of the mode
frequencies omega_A : omega_B = 1:3 and matching the Standard Model
generation count d - 1 = 3. The exactness is a consequence of the DME
time quantum and the DME-Core winding condition, and is not fitted.


================================================================================
8. THE PROCESSOR T AND CHARGE BOOKKEEPING
================================================================================

8.1 Definition of the processor
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Definition 8.1 (Processor T). Let P_mesh be the orthogonal projection onto
the 9-dimensional mesh resonance subspace (delta != 0) and let P_tau be
the rank-1 projection onto the unique mode with zero-state amplitude
s = +1 (tau). The processor is

    T = P_mesh + P_tau = I - P_sub + P_tau.                          (29)

The F/B split is

    F = {delta != 0} union {s = +1},
    B = {delta = 0} setminus {s = +1}.                               (30)

8.2 Properties
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 8.2 (Processor properties). T is a rank-10 orthogonal projection:

    T^2 = T,   T^dagger = T,   rank(T) = 10.

Its image is exactly the 10 Standard Model fermions
F = {nu, e, u, d, s, mu, c, b, t, tau} and its kernel is exactly the 4
Standard Model bosons B = {dark, W, Z, H}.

Proof. T^2 = T and T^dagger = T follow from the orthogonality of P_mesh
and P_tau. The rank-10 property follows from 9 + 1 = 10 for the ranks
of P_mesh and P_tau. The identification of the image with the fermions
and the kernel with the bosons is established in HCSM-13 and WIN-Processor.
[Box]

8.3 Charge bookkeeping
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 8.3 (Charge bookkeeping). The charges satisfy

    sum_{j in image(T)} Q_j = -2,
    sum_{j in ker(T)} Q_j = +1,
    sum_{j=1}^{14} Q_j = -1.                                         (31)

Proof. Direct computation using the charge assignment of HCSM-24. The
image of T is the 10 fermions; the kernel of T is the 4 bosons. The sum
over the image is -2 and the sum over the kernel is +1; the total is -1.
[Box]

Remark 8.4. The framework's decomposition into outer mesh (+2/3, 6 modes)
and middle mesh (-5/3, 8 modes) uses support classes. The processor
decomposition uses the projector T. Both sum to -1, matching the vacuum
charge invariant.

9. THE HCSM MOTION THEOREM
================================================================================

Theorem 9.1 (HCSM Motion Theorem). Let Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 be the
substrate with the 14-mode multiplet K_14 at lambda = 4. Let
H_wind = i sin((pi/8) M) be the winding generator. Then:

(1) H_wind is the unique Hermitian generator on C^14 determined by the
    substrate and the spectral constraint
    spec(H_wind) = {+/- 4 sqrt(2), +/- 2 sqrt(2), 0^(10)}. Its
    characteristic polynomial is lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8) (lambda^2 - 32).

(2) The standing-wave amplitudes are exactly the rationals 28/9, 7/18,
    21/16 for supports 64, 48, 32, derived from the complex Fourier
    coefficients B_j(omega) in Q(sqrt(2)).

(3) The five layers FDLM/WIN -> DME -> Core -> EFT -> Processor have
    well-defined state spaces and unique operators.

(4) The loop operator M_loop = T * L_EFT * L_Core * L_DME * L_FDLM is
    diagonal, with closed-form eigenvalue magnitudes

        |M_A| = |w+1| / (2 delta_edge) * e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2},
        |M_B| = |w+1| / (6 delta_edge) * e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2}.

(5) |M_A| / |M_B| = 3 exactly.

(6) The charge bookkeeping is sum Q_image = -2, sum Q_kernel = +1,
    total -1.

(7) The loop is dissipative: |M_j| < 1 for all j.

Proof. Parts (1)-(7) are established in Theorems 3.4, 4.3, 6.2, 7.1,
8.2, and 8.3 above. The uniqueness of H_wind is Corollary 5.3. The
dissipativity follows from |w+1| / delta_edge = 1.1574... and
e^{-(zeta H / rho) T_beat / 2} < 1, giving |M_A| = 0.5757... and
|M_B| = 0.1919..., both < 1. [Box]

exact ratio |M_A| / |M_B| = 3.

The winding generator H_wind = i sin((pi/8) M) is the unique Hermitian
generator on C^14 determined by the substrate and the spectral constraint
{+/- 4 sqrt(2), +/- 2 sqrt(2), 0^(10)}. Its characteristic polynomial is
lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8) (lambda^2 - 32).

The exact ratio |M_A| / |M_B| = 3 = d - 1 is a consequence of the DME
time quantum and the DME-Core winding condition, and matches the Standard
Model generation count. All framework constants cancel.

The processor T = P_mesh + P_tau is the unique rank-10 orthogonal
projection producing the Standard Model fermion/boson split. Its image is
exactly the 10 fermions; its kernel is exactly the 4 bosons. The charge
bookkeeping sums to -2, +1, and -1 respectively.

The present paper follows the HCSM-06/07 pattern: it derives everything
in its own domain that can be derived, discloses the three physical
