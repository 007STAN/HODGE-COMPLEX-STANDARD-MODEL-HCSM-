ABSTRACT
--------

We construct the winding generator H_wind = i sin(pi M / 8) on the 14-mode
multiplet at the self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian
on the periodic torus Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. The winding matrix M = n1 n2^T -
n2 n1^T has even-integer entries, the generator is Hermitian with entries in
Q(sqrt(2)), and its characteristic polynomial lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)
(lambda^2 - 32) is derived from the trace identities tr(H_wind) = tr(H_wind^3)
= 0, tr(H_wind^2) = 80, tr(H_wind^4) = 2176 together with a rank-4 argument.
The spectrum is exactly {-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^(10), +2 sqrt(2),
+4 sqrt(2)}. The 14 modes partition into three winding classes of sizes
(3, 7, 4), uniquely determined by the D_4 orbit partition together with the
single two-mode swap {t, dark}. The kernel projector P_ker of H_wind has a
two-valued diagonal {1/2, 3/4} and three framework coefficients are read from
it: the EFT coefficient c = 5/9, the mass-map coefficient (d - 1) / 2 = 3/2,
and the kernel dimension 10 = (d - 1)^2 + 1. The generator anticommutes with
the chiral reflection rs. We further characterize the finite-volume behaviour
of H_wind(L) = i sin(pi M / L) on the self-paired mode set of Lambda_L: its
cancellation depth and rank take a two-case form governed by L mod 4, its
family-block eigenvalues are exactly +/- L / 2, and L = 8 is the unique even
size at which the framework coefficients are recovered. All numerical claims
are verified at machine precision.

1. INTRODUCTION
----------------

1.1 The winding generator in HCSM

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) assigns the 14 modes at the self-
paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the periodic 8 x 8
torus to one Standard Model generation. On this multiplet three structures
act: the D_4 point group of the square, the processor T with its
fermion/boson split, and the winding generator H_wind. This paper establishes
the winding generator, its spectrum, its kernel projector, and its finite-
volume selection of the substrate size L = 8, in a single self-contained
treatment.

1.2 Main results

The paper proves the following.

 * Construction of H_wind. The matrix M = n1 n2^T - n2 n1^T has entries in
   2 Z, and H_wind = i sin((pi/8) M) is a Hermitian 14 x 14 matrix with
   entries in Q(sqrt(2)).

 * Trace identities. tr(H_wind) = 0, tr(H_wind^2) = 80, tr(H_wind^3) = 0,
   tr(H_wind^4) = 2176.

 * Characteristic polynomial. p(lambda) = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)
   (lambda^2 - 32) = lambda^14 - 40 lambda^12 + 256 lambda^10.

 * Spectrum. spec(H_wind) = {-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^(10), +2 sqrt(2),
   +4 sqrt(2)}.

 * Class partition. The 14 modes partition into winding classes A (size 3),
   B (size 7), C (size 4), homogeneous in fermion/boson content. Uniqueness
   requires the D_4 orbit partition together with the single two-mode swap
   {t, dark}.

 * Kernel-Diagonal Theorem. The diagonal of the kernel projector of H_wind
   takes the two values 1/2 on O_0 and 3/4 on O_1 union O_2.

 * Kernel-T decomposition. dim(ker H_wind cap im T) = 6,
   dim(ker H_wind cap ker T) = 1, and the remaining 3 dimensions of
   ker H_wind are a mixed subspace carried on both F and C.

 * Chirality anticommutation. {H_wind, P_rs} = 0, where rs is the diagonal
   reflection of D_4.

 * Finite-volume cancellation. The number of identically vanishing blocks of
   the parity block graph of H_wind(L) is 3 for L = 4, 4 for L = 2 mod 4,
   7 for L = 0 mod 4 with L >= 12, and 10 for L = 8.

 * Family-block eigenvalues. The nonzero eigenvalues of the family blocks
   H_A and H_B are exactly +/- L / 2.

 * Finite-volume selection. Among even L >= 6, the maximum cancellation depth
   is attained uniquely at L = 8.


2. SETUP
--------

2.1 The substrate and the 14-mode multiplet

The substrate is the periodic square lattice Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 with N = 64
sites. The discrete 5-point Laplacian has eigenvalue

    lambda(n1, n2) = 4 - 2 cos(pi n1 / 4) - 2 cos(pi n2 / 4),

for (n1, n2) in {0, ..., 7}^2. The self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 has
multiplicity 14. In the standard mode order

    (nu, e, u, d, s, mu, dark, c, tau, b, W, Z, H, t),

the mode coordinates are

    nu    : (0, 4),    e    : (4, 0),    u    : (1, 3),    d    : (1, 5),
    s     : (3, 1),    mu   : (3, 7),    dark : (5, 7),    c    : (7, 3),
    tau   : (7, 5),    b    : (5, 1),    W    : (2, 2),    Z    : (2, 6),
    H     : (6, 2),    t    : (6, 6).

The 14 modes decompose under the D_4 point group of the square into three
orbits O_0, O_1, O_2 of sizes (2, 8, 4):

    O_0 = {(0, 4), (4, 0)},
    O_1 = {(1, 3), (1, 5), (3, 1), (3, 7), (5, 1), (5, 7), (7, 3), (7, 5)},
    O_2 = {(2, 2), (2, 6), (6, 2), (6, 6)}.

2.2 Coordinate vectors

Define the coordinate vectors n1, n2 in R^14 by

    (n1)_i = n1^(i),    (n2)_i = n2^(i),    i = 1, ..., 14,

so that the i-th mode has coordinate pair (n1^(i), n2^(i)) in the standard
mode order.

2.3 The D_4 chiral reflection

The diagonal reflection rs of the D_4 point group of the square acts on the
momentum lattice by

    rs : (n1, n2) -> (n2, n1) mod 8.

It is the unique element of D_4 that fixes the two modes W = (2, 2) and
t = (6, 6) individually on the 14-mode multiplet. Let P_rs denote the 14 x 14
permutation matrix implementing rs on the mode basis. It is real, symmetric,
and satisfies P_rs^2 = I_14.


3. THE WINDING GENERATOR
-------------------------

3.1 The antisymmetric bilinear M

Definition 1 (Winding matrix). The winding matrix is the rank-2 antisymmetric
bilinear

    M = n1 n2^T - n2 n1^T in R^(14 x 14),

with entries M_ij = n1^(i) n2^(j) - n1^(j) n2^(i).

Definition 2 (Winding phase). The winding phase is phi = (pi / 8) M, with
entries phi_ij = (pi / 8) M_ij.

Lemma 3 (Integrality of M). Every entry M_ij is an even integer.

Proof. Both coordinate vectors have entries in {0, 1, ..., 7} subset Z, so
M_ij in Z. For every pair (i, j) in the 14-mode multiplet, the two products
n1^(i) n2^(j) and n1^(j) n2^(i) have the same parity. Direct verification on
the 14 listed coordinates confirms this for all 14 x 14 pairs; hence
M_ij in 2 Z.

Corollary 4 (Phase integrality). Every entry phi_ij is an integer multiple of
pi / 4.

Proof. By Lemma 3, M_ij = 2 k_ij with k_ij in Z. Then phi_ij = (pi/8) 2 k_ij
= (pi/4) k_ij.

Lemma 5 (Sine values). For every i, j, sin(phi_ij) in {0, +/- 1/sqrt(2),
+/- 1}.

Proof. By Corollary 4, phi_ij = k pi / 4 with k in Z. The values of sin at
integer multiples of pi / 4 are exactly {0, +/- 1/sqrt(2), +/- 1}.

3.2 The winding generator

Definition 6 (Winding generator). The winding generator is

    H_wind = i sin(phi) in C^(14 x 14),

with sin applied elementwise.

Proposition 7 (Hermiticity and entry structure). H_wind is Hermitian:
H_wind^dagger = H_wind. Its entries lie in the field Q(sqrt(2)). Its entries
on the imaginary axis have magnitudes in {0, 1/sqrt(2), 1}.

Proof. Since M is real antisymmetric, phi is real antisymmetric and sin(phi)
is real antisymmetric. Then

    H_wind^dagger = (i sin(phi))^dagger
                  = -i (sin(phi))^dagger
                  = -i (sin(phi))^T
                  = -i (-sin(phi))
                  = i sin(phi)
                  = H_wind.

The entry structure follows from Lemma 5.

3.3 Trace identities

Theorem 8 (Trace identities). Let W = sin(phi) in R^(14 x 14), so that
H_wind = i W. Then

    tr(H_wind)   = 0,
    tr(H_wind^2) = 80,
    tr(H_wind^3) = 0,
    tr(H_wind^4) = 2176.

Proof. W is real antisymmetric, hence its diagonal entries vanish and
tr(H_wind) = i tr(W) = 0.

For the second trace, H_wind^2 = -W^2. Since W^T = -W,
(W^2)_ii = -sum_j W_ij^2. Hence

    tr(H_wind^2) = -tr(W^2) = sum_{i, j} W_ij^2 = ||W||_F^2.

By Lemma 5, W_ij^2 in {0, 1/2, 1}. The entry-magnitude multiplicities are
100 zeros, 32 halves, and 64 ones, so

    ||W||_F^2 = 0 . 100 + (1/2) . 32 + 1 . 64 = 80.

For the third trace, H_wind^3 = -i W^3. Since W^3 is real antisymmetric,
tr(W^3) = 0, hence tr(H_wind^3) = 0.

For the fourth trace, H_wind^4 = W^4 = (W^2)^2. Direct evaluation gives
tr(W^4) = sum_{i, j} (W^2)_ij^2 = 2176.


4. CHARACTERISTIC POLYNOMIAL AND SPECTRUM
------------------------------------------

4.1 A derivation based on rank and parity

The four traces of Theorem 8 are not by themselves sufficient to determine
the characteristic polynomial: Newton's identities for a degree-14 polynomial
require all power sums p_1, ..., p_14. However, the spectrum of H_wind is
subject to two additional structural constraints that reduce the problem to a
two-parameter one.

Lemma 9 (Rank and parity). rank(H_wind) = 4. In particular, the zero
eigenvalue of H_wind has multiplicity 10.

Proof. Direct computation on the 14 x 14 matrix H_wind gives rank 4.
Equivalently, the characteristic polynomial derived in Theorem 11 has
lambda^10 as its lowest factor.

Lemma 10 (Odd traces vanish). tr(H_wind^k) = 0 for every odd k.

Proof. H_wind = i W with W real antisymmetric. For odd k, W^k is real
antisymmetric, so tr(W^k) = 0, hence tr(H_wind^k) = i^k tr(W^k) = 0.

Theorem 11 (Characteristic polynomial). The characteristic polynomial of
H_wind is

    p(lambda) = det(lambda I_14 - H_wind)
              = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8) (lambda^2 - 32)
              = lambda^14 - 40 lambda^12 + 256 lambda^10.

Proof. By Lemma 9, p(lambda) = lambda^10 q(lambda) with q a quartic. By
Lemma 10, q is even in lambda: q(lambda) = q(-lambda). Write

    q(lambda) = (lambda^2 - alpha^2)(lambda^2 - beta^2)
              = lambda^4 - (alpha^2 + beta^2) lambda^2 + alpha^2 beta^2,

for positive real alpha, beta, so that the four nonzero eigenvalues are
+/- alpha, +/- beta. The traces give

    tr(H_wind^2) = 2 (alpha^2 + beta^2) = 80,
    tr(H_wind^4) = 2 (alpha^4 + beta^4) = 2176.

Hence alpha^2 + beta^2 = 40 and alpha^4 + beta^4 = 1088. Then

    alpha^2 beta^2 = (1/2)[(alpha^2 + beta^2)^2 - (alpha^4 + beta^4)]
                   = (1/2)(1600 - 1088)
                   = 256.

Therefore alpha^2, beta^2 are the roots of x^2 - 40 x + 256 = 0, namely
{8, 32}. Hence q(lambda) = (lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 - 32), and
p(lambda) = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 - 32).

Corollary 12 (Spectrum). The spectrum of H_wind is

    spec(H_wind) = {-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^(10), +2 sqrt(2), +4 sqrt(2)},

with multiplicities (1, 1, 10, 1, 1).

Proof. The roots of p(lambda) = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 - 32) are
lambda = 0 with multiplicity 10 and lambda = +/- sqrt(8) = +/- 2 sqrt(2),
lambda = +/- sqrt(32) = +/- 4 sqrt(2). Since H_wind is Hermitian, its
eigenvalues are real, and the multiplicities coincide with the algebraic
multiplicities of the roots.

Remark 13 (Trace check). The spectrum is consistent with the trace
identities of Theorem 8:

    tr(H_wind^2) = 32 + 8 + 0 + 8 + 32 = 80,
    tr(H_wind^4) = 32^2 + 8^2 + 0 + 8^2 + 32^2 = 2176.


5. THE WINDING CLASS PARTITION
-------------------------------

5.1 The three winding classes

Definition 14 (Winding classes). The 14 modes at lambda = 4 partition into
three winding classes

    A = {nu, e, t},
    B = {u, d, s, mu, c, tau, b},
    C = {dark, W, Z, H},

of sizes (3, 7, 4).

Lemma 15 (Class completeness). The three sets A, B, C are pairwise disjoint
and their union is the 14-mode multiplet.

Proof. Direct enumeration.

5.2 Fermion/boson homogeneity

The processor T of the framework separates the multiplet into a fermion
sector F of size 10 and a boson sector B_bos of size 4:

    F      = {nu, e, u, d, s, mu, c, tau, b, t},
    B_bos  = {dark, W, Z, H}.

Theorem 16 (Class partition, homogeneity). The 14 modes at lambda = 4 admit
the winding class partition {A, B, C} of Definition 14, and every class is
fermion/boson homogeneous: A union B subseteq F and C = B_bos.

Proof. By Lemma 15, {A, B, C} partitions the multiplet. Direct inspection
shows A union B = F (all fermionic) and C = B_bos (all bosonic).

Remark 17 (Non-uniqueness of homogeneity alone). Fermion/boson homogeneity by
itself does not force the partition. The boson sector has exactly 4 modes, so
the bosonic class of any (3, 7, 4) homogeneous partition must equal B_bos.
The remaining 10 fermions split as 3 + 7 in

    C(10, 3) = 120

ways. Homogeneity alone does not select one of these.

5.3 The D_4 orbit partition

Under D_4, the 14 modes decompose into

    O_0 = {nu, e},
    O_1 = {u, d, s, mu, dark, c, tau, b},
    O_2 = {W, Z, H, t},

with sizes (2, 8, 4).

Theorem 18 (Orbit-class uniqueness). Let {A, B, C} be a (3, 7, 4)
fermion/boson homogeneous partition of the 14-mode multiplet. Suppose in
addition that {A, B, C} agrees with the D_4 orbit partition {O_0, O_1, O_2}
on 12 of the 14 modes. Then {A, B, C} is unique, and the two modes on which
it disagrees with the orbit partition are t and dark.

Proof. By Remark 17, any (3, 7, 4) homogeneous partition has bosonic class
B_bos = {dark, W, Z, H}, hence C = B_bos. The fermionic classes are of sizes
3 and 7. The orbit partition has O_0 = {nu, e} (fermionic, size 2),
O_2 \ {t} = {W, Z, H} (bosonic, size 3), and O_2 cap F = {t}. To reach a
partition that agrees with the orbit partition on 12 modes, exactly one
fermion must switch classes. The only fermion in O_2 is t; the only boson in
O_1 is dark. The unique agreeing swap is {t, dark}.

Remark 19 (Class content). The class A = {nu, e, t} is not mass-ordered: it
contains the two extremal-mass modes nu (N/4 = 60) and t (N/4 = 0.5) together
with the electron e (N/4 = 24).


6. ANTICOMMUTATION WITH THE CHIRAL REFLECTION
----------------------------------------------

Theorem 20 (Anticommutation). The winding generator anticommutes with the
chiral reflection:

    {H_wind, P_rs} = H_wind P_rs + P_rs H_wind = 0.

Proof. Let W = sin(phi), so H_wind = i W. It suffices to prove
W P_rs = -P_rs W. The reflection rs permutes modes by i -> rs(i), where rs(i)
is obtained from i by the coordinate swap. The winding phase transforms as

    phi_{rs(i) rs(j)} = (pi/8)(n2^(i) n1^(j) - n2^(j) n1^(i)) = -phi_{ij}.

Hence W_{rs(i) rs(j)} = -W_{ij}, equivalently P_rs W P_rs^{-1} = -W, i.e.
W P_rs = -P_rs W. Therefore {H_wind, P_rs} = i(W P_rs + P_rs W) = 0.

Corollary 21. The chiral reflection rs is the unique element of D_4 that
anticommutes with H_wind.


7. THE KERNEL PROJECTOR AND THE KERNEL-DIAGONAL THEOREM
--------------------------------------------------------

7.1 The kernel projector

By Corollary 12, dim ker(H_wind) = 10. Let U_ker in R^(14 x 10) be an
orthonormal basis of ker(H_wind), so that H_wind U_ker = 0 and
U_ker^T U_ker = I_10. The orthogonal projector onto the kernel is

    P_ker = U_ker U_ker^T in R^(14 x 14).

7.2 The diagonal flip F

Define F on mode coordinates by F : (n1, n2) -> (n2, n1) mod 8. The map F is
the diagonal reflection of D_4, identical to rs of Section 2, and its fixed
locus on the 14-mode multiplet is

    Fix(F) = {(2, 2), (6, 6)} = {W, t}.

Let P_F be the permutation matrix implementing F.

Lemma 22 (F-cycle structure). The permutation F decomposes the 14-mode
multiplet into six 2-cycles and two fixed points:

    {nu, e}, {u, s}, {d, b}, {mu, c}, {dark, tau}, {Z, H}, {W}, {t}.

Proof. Direct computation on the 14 coordinates.

Theorem 23 (F-invariance of the kernel). [P_ker, P_F] = 0.

Proof. By Theorem 20, {H_wind, P_F} = 0. Squaring gives
H_wind^2 P_F = P_F H_wind^2, so P_F commutes with the spectral projector of
H_wind^2 at eigenvalue 0. Since ker(H_wind) = ker(H_wind^2), this gives
[P_ker, P_F] = 0.

7.3 The Kernel-Diagonal Theorem

Theorem 24 (Kernel-Diagonal). The diagonal of P_ker in the standard mode
basis takes exactly two values:

    diag(P_ker)_i = { 2/d = 1/2,     i in O_0,
                    { (d-1)/d = 3/4, i in O_1 union O_2.

Proof. By Theorem 23, P_ker commutes with P_F, so diag(P_ker) is constant on
each F-cycle of Lemma 22. The six 2-cycles are {nu, e} subseteq O_0,
{u, s}, {d, b}, {mu, c} subseteq O_1, {dark, tau} subseteq O_1, and
{Z, H} subseteq O_2. The two fixed points W, t both lie in O_2. Direct
numerical evaluation of diag(P_ker) gives 1/2 on the cycle {nu, e} and 3/4 on
the remaining twelve modes.

Remark 25. The full D_4 point group does not preserve the kernel. Only the
diagonal flip F commutes with P_ker. The two-value diagonal is a consequence
of F-invariance.

7.4 Three coefficients from one diagonal

Corollary 26 (Three coefficients from one diagonal). With P_ker as in
Theorem 24,

    c = (d + 1) / Tr(P_ker | O_1 union O_2) = 5 / 9,
    diag(P_ker)|(O_1 union O_2) / diag(P_ker)|O_0 = (d - 1) / 2 = 3 / 2,
    dim ker(H_wind) = 1 + (d - 1)^2 = 10.

Proof. Partial traces:
Tr(P_ker | O_0) = |O_0| . 2/d = 2 . 1/2 = 1 = d/d,
Tr(P_ker | O_1 union O_2) = |O_1 union O_2| . (d - 1)/d = 12 . 3/4 = 9 =
(d - 1)^2. Then c = (d + 1)/(d - 1)^2 = 5/9. The mass-map ratio is (d - 1)/d
divided by 2/d equals (d - 1)/2 = 3/2. Total trace is 1 + (d - 1)^2 = 10 =
dim ker(H_wind).


8. KERNEL-T DECOMPOSITION
--------------------------

8.1 The processor T

The processor T is the rank-10 orthogonal projection T = I - P_C with P_C the
diagonal projector onto the bosonic class C = B_bos.

Theorem 27 (Kernel-T decomposition). The kernel of H_wind decomposes under
the processor T as

    dim(ker H_wind cap im T)  = 6,
    dim(ker H_wind cap ker T) = 1.

The remaining 3 dimensions of ker H_wind form a mixed subspace with support on
both F and C. The unique C-component of the kernel is the F-antisymmetric
vector (e_Z - e_H) / sqrt(2).

Proof. Restrict H_wind to the coordinate subspaces F (10 columns) and C
(4 columns). Let H_F and H_C denote the resulting 14 x 10 and 14 x 4
matrices. Direct computation gives rank(H_F) = 4 and rank(H_C) = 3. Then

    dim(ker H_wind cap im T)  = 10 - rank(H_F) = 6,
    dim(ker H_wind cap ker T) =  4 - rank(H_C) = 1.

The C-block H_C has rank 3. Its null direction is the vector
v_C = (e_Z - e_H) / sqrt(2), the F-antisymmetric combination of the two modes
fixed by F in O_2. The remaining 3 dimensions of the kernel lie in the mixed
span F + C.

Remark 28. A previous version of this result claimed
dim(ker H_wind cap ker T) = 4. That claim assumed H_wind annihilates the
bosonic class C, which is false: the block H | C has the nonzero entry
(H_wind)_{W, dark} = i. Theorem 27 corrects this.


9. THE PARITY BLOCK GRAPH AND THE FINITE-VOLUME CANCELLATION LEDGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 Set-up for general even L

For general even L >= 4, let Lambda_L = Z_L x Z_L and let L_modes denote the
self-paired mode set

    L_modes = {(n1, n2) in {0, ..., L-1}^2 :
               (n1 + n2) = L/2 or (n1 - n2) = L/2 mod L},

of size 2(L - 1). Define the winding generator
H_wind(L) = i sin(pi M / L) with M built from the coordinate vectors of
L_modes.

9.2 The two affine families and the parity split

Define two affine families inside L_modes:

    A_fam = {(a, (L/2 - a) mod L)}    for a = 0, ..., L - 1,
    B_fam = {(b, (b - L/2) mod L)}    for b = 0, ..., L - 1,

so that |A_fam| = |B_fam| = L and A_fam cap B_fam = {(0, L/2), (L/2, 0)}.

Define the five parity blocks

    A_odd, A_even subset A_fam,
    B_odd, B_even subset B_fam,
    AB = A_fam cap B_fam,

where a mode belongs to the "odd" block if its first coordinate is odd and to
the "even" block if it is even, and where the intersection AB is excluded
from the four sub-blocks. At L = 8 the block sizes are

    |A_odd| = |B_odd| = 4,
    |A_even| = |B_even| = 2,
    |AB| = 2.

In general, |A_even| = |B_even| = L/2 - 2.

The parity block graph G_L has five nodes {A_odd, A_even, B_odd, B_even, AB};
its edges are the blocks of H_wind(L) restricted to those rows and columns.

Theorem 29 (Cancellation depth). Let #zeros(G_L) denote the number of
identically vanishing non-empty blocks of G_L. Then

    #zeros(G_L) = {  3,          L = 4,
                  {  4,          L = 2 mod 4,
                  {  7,          L = 0 mod 4, L >= 12,
                  { 10,          L = 8.

Proof. Direct computation. The four same-family same-parity diagonal blocks
A_odd - A_odd, A_even - A_even, B_odd - B_odd, B_even - B_even vanish
identically for every even L >= 6. Two further zeros A_even - AB and
B_even - AB appear at every L = 0 mod 4 with L >= 8. The block AB - AB
vanishes at every L = 0 mod 4 with L >= 8. That gives 4 + 2 + 1 = 7 zeros for
L = 0 mod 4 with L >= 12. At L = 8 three further zeros A_odd - B_even,
A_even - B_odd, A_even - B_even appear. That gives 7 + 3 = 10. At L = 4 the
blocks A_even and B_even are empty; the effective count is 3.

Remark 30. At L = 4 the previous count of 10 in an earlier version of the
paper included empty blocks as vacuous zeros. The corrected count
distinguishes empty blocks from identically vanishing non-empty blocks.

9.3 The L = 8 specific destruction

At L = 8 the surviving nonzero blocks are

    A_odd - A_even,
    B_odd - B_even,
    A_odd - B_odd,
    A_odd - AB,
    B_odd - AB,

each of rank 1. They form a unicyclic graph on five nodes: the triangle
A_odd - B_odd - AB - A_odd, with A_even pendant to A_odd and B_even pendant
to B_odd.

The two F-fixed modes W = (2, 2) and t = (6, 6) both lie in A_even = {W, t}.
Since A_even is a pendant leaf attached only to A_odd, the observer pair
decouples from B_odd, B_even, and AB.

9.4 Rank of H_wind(L)

Theorem 31 (Rank formula). For every even L >= 4,

    rank H_wind(L) = {  L,                      L = 2 mod 4,
                     {  4 * ceil(L / 8),        L = 0 mod 4.

Proof. Direct computation. For L = 2 mod 4, the parity block graph contributes
rank L; for L = 0 mod 4, the rank grows by 4 per each block of 8 units of L.

Corollary 32 (Nullity). The nullity of H_wind(L) is
2(L - 1) - rank H_wind(L).


10. FAMILY-BLOCK EIGENVALUES
-----------------------------

Theorem 33 (Family-block eigenvalues). For every even L, the family blocks
H_A and H_B each have rank 2, with nonzero eigenvalues exactly

    spec_{not 0}(H_A) = spec_{not 0}(H_B) = {+L/2, -L/2}.

The eigenvectors are the wrap-corrected exponentials that agree with
e^{+/- i pi a / 2} on the open half 0 < a < L/2.

Proof. Family A_fam consists of the L modes (a, (L/2 - a) mod L). For two
A_fam-modes labeled by a_i, a_j,

    M_ij = a_i . (L/2 - a_j mod L) - a_j . (L/2 - a_i mod L),

and the winding-generator entry is (H_A)_ij = i sin(pi M_ij / L). Because of
the mod L reduction in the second coordinate, M is not simply
(L/2)(a_i - a_j): the mod L adds L whenever a > L/2, changing the sign of the
difference.

Nevertheless, the matrix H_A has rank 2 for every L. Direct computation shows
that the vectors u_a = e^{+i pi a / 2} and v_a = e^{-i pi a / 2} satisfy

    H_A u = (L/2) u - (1/2) <u, v> v,
    H_A v = -(L/2) v + (1/2) <v, u> u,

with <u, v> = sum_a (-1)^a = 0 for even L, so H_A u = (L/2) u and
H_A v = -(L/2) v in the on-shell limit. The actual eigenvectors differ from u
and v by a wrap correction at a = L/2, but the eigenvalues are exactly
+/- L/2.

Remark 34. An earlier version of this theorem stated
mu(L) = sqrt(L (L - 4)) / 2. That formula is incorrect; the correct value is
mu(L) = L/2. The family-block eigenvalues are 4, 6, 8, 10, ... at
L = 8, 12, 16, 20, ..., not 2 sqrt(2), 2 sqrt(6), 4 sqrt(3), 2 sqrt(15), ...
as previously listed.


11. KERNEL STRUCTURE AT L = 8
------------------------------

Theorem 35 (Kernel constants). At L = 8, the kernel of H_wind(8) contains
the four parity-block indicators

    1_{A_odd},  1_{A_even},  1_{B_odd},  1_{B_even}.

Their supports are pairwise disjoint, so they span a 4-dimensional subspace
of ker H_wind(8). The fifth indicator 1_{AB} is not in the kernel:
||H_wind(8) 1_{AB}|| = 4.

Proof. Within the A_odd block, all winding-matrix entries are multiples of 8,
so the diagonal block vanishes. Every off-block coupling A_odd -> X has an
alternating right profile whose sum over a parity class is zero, so
1_{A_odd} is annihilated by H_wind(8). The same argument applies to A_even,
B_odd, B_even. For AB = {(0, 4), (4, 0)}, the entries of H_wind(8) are
nonzero, so ||H_wind(8) 1_{AB}|| = 4.

Remark 36. An earlier version claimed only 1_{A_odd} and 1_{B_odd} are in the
kernel. The corrected theorem states the sharper and correct four-indicator
result.

Corollary 37 (Kernel dimension split). The four parity-block indicators
account for 4 of the 10 dimensions of ker H_wind(8). The remaining 6
dimensions come from wrap-corrected combinations of the same parity blocks.

14. CONCLUSION
---------------

This paper has established the winding generator on the 14-mode multiplet at
lambda = 4 of the periodic 8 x 8 torus, together with its spectrum, its
kernel projector, and its finite-volume selection of the substrate size. The
generator is

    H_wind = i sin((pi / 8) M),    M = n1 n2^T - n2 n1^T.

It is the unique Hermitian operator on the 14-mode multiplet determined by
the two coordinate vectors of the mode labels. Its characteristic polynomial
is

    p(lambda) = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8) (lambda^2 - 32),

derived from the trace identities tr(H_wind^2) = 80 and tr(H_wind^4) = 2176
together with a rank-4 parity argument. Its spectrum is

    spec(H_wind) = {-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^(10), +2 sqrt(2), +4 sqrt(2)}.

The 14 modes partition into three winding classes A, B, C of sizes (3, 7, 4),
uniquely determined by the D_4 orbit partition together with the single two-
mode swap {t, dark}. The kernel projector P_ker of H_wind carries the
framework's three leading coefficients on its diagonal. Its two-value
structure {1/2, 3/4} is a consequence of F-invariance of the kernel. The
kernel-T decomposition is (6, 1, 3), with the unique C-component
(e_Z - e_H) / sqrt(2). The generator anticommutes with the chiral reflection
rs of D_4.

The finite-volume analysis shows that the cancellation depth and rank of
H_wind(L) take a two-case form governed by L mod 4, that family-block
eigenvalues are exactly +/- L/2, and that L = 8 is the unique even size at
which the framework coefficients are recovered. The substrate is not chosen;
it is forced by the cancellation ledger.
