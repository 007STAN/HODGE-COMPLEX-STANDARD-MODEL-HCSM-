ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge Complex Standard Model identifies the 14 modes at the self-paired
eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the periodic 8 x 8 torus
with one Standard Model generation (HCSM-04). This paper establishes the
winding generator on that multiplet. From the two coordinate vectors
n_1, n_2 in R^14 of the mode labels, we construct the unique rank-2
antisymmetric bilinear M = n_1 n_2^T - n_2 n_1^T, the winding phase
phi = (pi/8) M, and the winding generator H_wind = i sin phi (elementwise
sine). We prove: (i) the entries of M are even integers, so the entries of
phi are integer multiples of pi/4; (ii) H_wind is Hermitian with entries in
Q(sqrt 2); (iii) the trace identities tr(H_wind) = 0, tr(H_wind^2) = 80,
tr(H_wind^3) = 0, tr(H_wind^4) = 2176; (iv) Newton's identities applied to
these traces give the characteristic polynomial
p(lambda) = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 - 32); (v) the spectrum is
exactly {-4 sqrt 2, -2 sqrt 2, 0^(10), +2 sqrt 2, +4 sqrt 2}; (vi) the 14
modes partition into three winding classes {A, B, C} of sizes (3, 7, 4),
uniquely forced by the requirement that each class be fermion/boson
homogeneous under the processor T of HCSM-13; (vii) the class partition
differs from the D_4 orbit partition of HCSM-05 by the single two-mode swap
{t, dark}; and (viii) H_wind anticommutes with the diagonal reflection rs of
D_4, the algebraic input to the Chirality Selection Theorem of HCSM-26. All
claims are HCSM-native and verified at machine precision.

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 The winding generator in HCSM

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) assigns the 14 modes at the self-paired
eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the periodic 8 x 8 torus to
one Standard Model generation [HCSM-00, HCSM-01, HCSM-04]. On this multiplet,
three structures act: the D_4 point group (HCSM-05), the processor T with its
fermion/boson split (HCSM-13), and the winding generator H_wind.

The winding generator is the framework's fundamental Hermitian operator on the
multiplet. Its spectrum partitions the 14 modes into three winding classes, and
its class projectors are the operators on which the loop operator of the
framework acts. HCSM-13 states the class partition as a primitive; the present
paper derives it.


1.2 The one-line summary

The 14-mode multiplet carries a unique antisymmetric bilinear in its two
coordinate vectors. The elementwise-sine of the winding phase gives a Hermitian
generator whose characteristic polynomial lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 -
32) is derived from two trace identities by Newton's identities. The resulting
spectrum partitions the 14 modes into three winding classes of sizes 3, 7, 4
that are homogeneous in fermion/boson content. The class partition is the
unique fermion/boson-homogeneous refinement of the D_4 orbit partition.


1.3 Main results

  - Construction of H_wind. The matrix M = n_1 n_2^T - n_2 n_1^T has entries
    in 2 Z, and H_wind = i sin((pi/8) M) is a Hermitian 14 x 14 matrix with
    entries in Q(sqrt 2).

  - Trace identities. tr(H_wind) = 0, tr(H_wind^2) = 80, tr(H_wind^3) = 0,
    tr(H_wind^4) = 2176.

  - Characteristic polynomial.
    p(lambda) = lambda^10 (lambda^2 - 8)(lambda^2 - 32)
              = lambda^14 - 40 lambda^12 + 256 lambda^10.

  - Spectrum. spec(H_wind) = {-4 sqrt 2, -2 sqrt 2, 0^(10), +2 sqrt 2,
    +4 sqrt 2}.

  - Class partition. The 14 modes partition into winding classes A (size 3),
    B (size 7), C (size 4), homogeneous in fermion/boson content.

  - Orbit-class reconstruction. The class partition is the D_4 orbit partition
    modified by the single two-mode swap {t, dark}.

  - Chirality anticommutation. {H_wind, P_rs} = 0, where rs is the diagonal
    reflection of D_4.

2. SETUP

2.1 The substrate and the 14-mode multiplet

The substrate is the periodic square lattice Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 with N = 64
sites [HCSM-01]. The discrete 5-point Laplacian has eigenvalue

    lambda(n_1, n_2) = 4 - 2 cos(pi n_1 / 4) - 2 cos(pi n_2 / 4),
        (n_1, n_2) in {0, ..., 7}^2.

The self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 has multiplicity 14 [HCSM-04]. In the
standard mode order

    (nu, e, u, d, s, mu, dark, c, tau, b, W, Z, H, t),

the mode coordinates are

    nu:   (0,4),    e:     (4,0),    u:     (1,3),    d:     (1,5),
    s:    (3,1),    mu:    (3,7),    dark:  (5,7),    c:     (7,3),
    tau:  (7,5),    b:     (5,1),    W:     (2,2),    Z:     (2,6),
    H:    (6,2),    t:     (6,6).

The 14 modes decompose under the D_4 point group of the square into three
orbits O_0, O_1, O_2 of sizes (2, 8, 4) [HCSM-04, HCSM-05]:

    O_0 = {(0,4), (4,0)},
    O_1 = {(1,3), (1,5), (3,1), (3,7), (5,1), (5,7), (7,3), (7,5)},
    O_2 = {(2,2), (2,6), (6,2), (6,6)}.


2.2 Coordinate vectors

Define the coordinate vectors n_1, n_2 in R^14 by

    (n_1)_i = n_1^(i),    (n_2)_i = n_2^(i),    i = 1, ..., 14,

so that the i-th mode has coordinate pair (n_1^(i), n_2^(i)) in the standard
mode order.


2.3 The D_4 chiral reflection

The diagonal reflection rs of the D_4 point group of the square acts on the
momentum lattice by

    rs: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1)  (mod 8).

It is the unique element of D_4 that fixes exactly the pair {(2,2), (6,6)} =
{W, t} on the 14-mode multiplet, and it anticommutes with H_wind (proved in
Section 7). Let P_rs denote the 14 x 14 permutation matrix implementing rs on
the mode basis. It is real, symmetric, and satisfies P_rs^2 = I_14.

3. THE WINDING GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 The antisymmetric bilinear M

Definition (Winding matrix). The winding matrix is the rank-2 antisymmetric
bilinear

    M = n_1 n_2^T - n_2 n_1^T in R^(14 x 14).

Equivalently, M_{ij} = n_1^(i) n_2^(j) - n_1^(j) n_2^(i).

Definition (Winding phase). The winding phase is

    phi = (pi/8) M,    phi_{ij} = (pi/8) M_{ij}.

Lemma (Integrality of M). Every entry M_{ij} is an even integer.

Proof. Both coordinate vectors n_1^(i) and n_2^(i) lie in {0, 1, ..., 7} subset
Z. The product n_1^(i) n_2^(j) in Z and the difference M_{ij} = n_1^(i) n_2^(j)
- n_1^(j) n_2^(i) in Z. That M_{ij} is even follows from a parity check on the
standard mode coordinates: for every pair (i, j) of the 14 modes, the two
products n_1^(i) n_2^(j) and n_1^(j) n_2^(i) have the same parity (both are
even, or both are odd), so their difference is even. Direct verification on the
14 listed coordinates confirms this claim for all 14 x 14 pairs. []

Corollary (Phase integrality). Every entry phi_{ij} is an integer multiple of
pi/4.

Proof. By the previous lemma, M_{ij} = 2 k_{ij} for some k_{ij} in Z. Then
phi_{ij} = (pi/8) * 2 k_{ij} = (pi/4) k_{ij}. []

Lemma (Sine values). For every i, j,

    sin(phi_{ij}) in {0, +/- 1/sqrt 2, +/- 1}.

Proof. By the corollary, phi_{ij} = k pi / 4 with k in Z. The values of sin at
integer multiples of pi/4 are exactly {0, +/- 1/sqrt 2, +/- 1}. []


3.2 The winding generator

Definition (Winding generator). The winding generator is

    H_wind = i sin phi in C^(14 x 14),

where sin is applied elementwise.

Proposition (Hermiticity and entry structure). H_wind is Hermitian:
H_wind^dagger = H_wind. Its entries lie in the field Q(sqrt 2). Its entries on
the real axis are integer multiples of 1 or 1/sqrt 2; its entries off the real
axis are purely imaginary, with magnitudes in {0, 1/sqrt 2, 1}.

Proof. Since phi is real antisymmetric (as M is real antisymmetric), sin phi is
real antisymmetric:

    (sin phi)^T = sin(phi^T) = sin(-phi) = -sin phi.

Then

    H_wind^dagger = (i sin phi)^dagger = -i (sin phi)^dagger = -i (sin phi)^T
                  = -i (-sin phi) = i sin phi = H_wind.

The entry structure follows from the previous lemma. []
