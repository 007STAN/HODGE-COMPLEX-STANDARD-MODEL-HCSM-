ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge Complex Standard Model derives the gauge algebra
g_SM = su(3)_c + su(2)_L + u(1)^5 from the D_4 endomorphism algebra of the
14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4 (HCSM-06). Two Hermitian operators act on that
multiplet: the loop operator M_loop of the framework (HCSM-14, HCSM-17) and
the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian H_DNLS = 4 I + U K whose kernel K has entries
K_ab = sum_x |phi_a(x)|^2 |phi_b(x)|^2 built from the mode wavefunctions on
the torus. This paper determines the intersection of g_SM with the commutant
of each operator on the 14-mode multiplet. We prove: (i) g_SM cap Comm(M_loop)
is a three-dimensional real, Hermitian, abelian subalgebra of g_SM; (ii) every
element of that subalgebra has equal coefficients on the isotypic projectors
P_A2, P_B2, P_E; (iii) the root generators lambda^1, lambda^2, lambda^4,
lambda^5, lambda^6, lambda^7 of su(3)_c and sigma^1, sigma^2 of su(2)_L lie in
the kernel of the projection onto the loop commutant; (iv) g_SM cap
Comm(H_DNLS) is a ten-dimensional real, Hermitian subalgebra of g_SM
isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7; (v) the full su(2)_L factor is preserved
exactly, with sigma^1, sigma^2, sigma^3 in the nullspace; (vi) su(3)_c is
broken to a two-dimensional abelian subalgebra, with the root generators
lambda^2, lambda^5, lambda^7 orthogonal to the DNLS commutant and the Cartan
generators lambda^3, lambda^8 having partial overlap. The exact su(2)_L
preservation is proved by a doubled-momentum pairing mechanism: the two B_1
basis vectors pair modes with the same doubled momentum (2n_1, 2n_2) mod 8 and
opposite signs, so the B_1-compressed kernel U_B1^T K U_B1 vanishes
identically. All claims are HCSM-native and verified at machine precision.

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Gauge symmetry and the machine

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) derives the gauge algebra g_SM as a
subalgebra of the D_4 commutant on the complexified 14-mode multiplet at the
self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the periodic
8 x 8 torus (HCSM-06). The processor T and the winding generator H_wind act on
the same multiplet (HCSM-13, HCSM-14), and it is a structural question which
part of the gauge algebra survives each operator's action.

Two operators are central to the framework's dynamics. The loop operator
M_loop = M_A P_A + M_B P_B + 0 * P_C is a diagonal, non-Hermitian (dissipative)
operator whose eigenvectors are the modes and whose eigenvalues are complex
with the exact ratio |M_A| / |M_B| = 3 (HCSM-14, HCSM-17). The DNLS Hartree
Hamiltonian H_DNLS = 4 I_14 + U K is the mean-field approximation of the
discrete nonlinear Schrodinger substrate dynamics, with Hartree kernel
K_ab = sum_x |phi_a(x)|^2 |phi_b(x)|^2.

The present paper determines, on the 14-dimensional visible sector, exactly
which part of g_SM commutes with each operator.


1.2 The one-line summary

On the 14-mode multiplet, the loop operator preserves only a three-dimensional
abelian subalgebra of g_SM; the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian preserves a larger
ten-dimensional subalgebra isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7. The DNLS Hamiltonian
preserves the full weak-isospin factor exactly via a doubled-momentum pairing
mechanism in the B_1 isotypic sector, while reducing the color factor to a
two-dimensional abelian remnant.


1.3 Main results

  - Loop commutant (Theorem 3.4). g_SM cap Comm(M_loop) is a 3-dimensional
    real, Hermitian, abelian subalgebra of g_SM.

  - Isotypic projector degeneracy (Theorem 4.1). Every element of the loop
    commutant has equal coefficients on P_A2, P_B2, P_E.

  - No root generators (Theorem 4.3). The loop commutant has zero overlap with
    lambda^1, lambda^2, lambda^4, lambda^5, lambda^6, lambda^7 and sigma^1,
    sigma^2.

  - DNLS commutant (Theorem 5.4). g_SM cap Comm(H_DNLS) is a 10-dimensional
    real, Hermitian subalgebra of g_SM, isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7.

  - su(2)_L exact preservation (Theorem 6.3). The three generators sigma^1,
    sigma^2, sigma^3 lie in the DNLS commutant, with overlap 1 at machine

  - Doubled-momentum pairing (Theorem 6.1). The B_1-compressed kernel
    U_B1^T K U_B1 vanishes identically because the two B_1 basis vectors pair

  - su(3)_c breaking (Theorem 7.1). The DNLS commutant contains a 2-dimensional
    abelian subalgebra of su(3)_c; the roots lambda^2, lambda^5, lambda^7 are

2. SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 The substrate and the 14-mode multiplet

The substrate is the periodic square lattice Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 with N = 64
sites [HCSM-01]. The discrete 5-point Laplacian has eigenvalue

    lambda(n_1, n_2) = 4 - 2 cos(pi n_1 / 4) - 2 cos(pi n_2 / 4),
        (n_1, n_2) in {0, ..., 7}^2.

The self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 has multiplicity 14 [HCSM-04]. In the
standard mode order

    (nu, e, u, d, s, mu, dark, c, tau, b, W, Z, H, t),

the mode coordinates are

    nu:   (0,4),    e:     (4,0),    u:     (1,3),    d:     (1,5),
    s:    (3,1),    mu:    (3,7),    dark:  (5,7),    c:     (7,3),
    tau:  (7,5),    b:     (5,1),    W:     (2,2),    Z:     (2,6),
    H:    (6,2),    t:     (6,6).


2.2 The D_4 action and isotypic decomposition

The D_4 point group of the square acts on Lambda by the generators

    r: (n_1, n_2) -> (-n_2, n_1) mod 8,    s: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_1, -n_2) mod 8,

with r^4 = s^2 = (rs)^2 = e. On the 14-mode multiplet, the complexified space
V_14 = C^14 decomposes as

    V_14 = 3A_1 + A_2 + 2B_1 + 2B_2 + 3E

(HCSM-05, HCSM-06). The isotypic projectors are

    P_rho = (dim rho / |D_4|) sum_{g in D_4} chi_rho(g)^* R(g),

where R(g) is the permutation representation of g on C^14 and chi_rho is the
character of the irrep rho. The five projectors satisfy
P_rho P_rho' = delta_{rho rho'} P_rho and sum_rho P_rho = I_14.


2.3 The gauge algebra g_SM on the 14-mode multiplet

By HCSM-06, the gauge algebra is the Lie subalgebra

    g_SM = su(3)_c + su(2)_L + u(1)^5 subset Comm(D_4, V_14)

of the D_4 commutant, of real dimension 8 + 3 + 5 = 16. Concretely:

  - su(3)_c acts on the A_1 multiplicity space M_A1 = C^3. Generators:
    T_c^a = U_A1 lambda^a U_A1^T for a = 1, ..., 8, where U_A1 in R^(14 x 3) is
    an orthonormal basis of the A_1 isotypic sector and lambda^a are the
    Gell-Mann matrices.

  - su(2)_L acts on the B_1 multiplicity space M_B1 = C^2. Generators:
    T_L^i = U_B1 sigma^i U_B1^T for i = 1, 2, 3, where U_B1 in R^(14 x 2) is
    an orthonormal basis of the B_1 isotypic sector and sigma^i are the Pauli
    matrices.

  - u(1)^5 is spanned by the five isotypic projectors P_A1, P_A2, P_B1, P_B2,
    P_E.

The ordered list of the 16 gauge generators on C^14 is

    g_SM^(14) = {T_c^1, ..., T_c^8, T_L^1, T_L^2, T_L^3, P_A1, P_A2, P_B1,
                  P_B2, P_E}.

All 16 generators are Hermitian and D_4-invariant.


2.4 The winding class partition

By HCSM-14, the 14 modes partition into three winding classes

    A = {nu, e, t},    B = {u, d, s, mu, c, tau, b},
    C = {dark, W, Z, H}.

The class projectors P_A, P_B, P_C are diagonal in the mode basis, orthogonal,
idempotent, and sum to the identity. The processor T of HCSM-13 satisfies
T = I - P_C = P_A + P_B, so the fermion sector is A union B and the boson
sector is C.

3. THE LOOP OPERATOR AND ITS COMMUTANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Definition of M_loop on the 14-mode multiplet

Definition (Loop operator). The loop operator is the diagonal operator

    M_loop = M_A P_A + M_B P_B + 0 * P_C,

with M_A = r_A exp(i theta_A), M_B = r_B exp(i theta_B) the complex loop
eigenvalues of HCSM-14 and HCSM-17, and r_A / r_B = 3 exactly.

M_loop is not Hermitian (M_A, M_B are complex), but it is normal:
M_loop M_loop^dagger = M_loop^dagger M_loop because it is diagonal. It commutes
with each class projector:

    [M_loop, P_A] = [M_loop, P_B] = [M_loop, P_C] = 0.


3.2 The loop commutant

Definition (Loop commutant). The loop commutant is the intersection

    g_loop := g_SM cap Comm(M_loop) subset g_SM.

Theorem 3.4 (Loop commutant dimension). The loop commutant g_loop is a
three-dimensional real, Hermitian, abelian subalgebra of g_SM.

Proof. Form the stacked commutator matrix C in C^(14^2 x 16) whose j-th column
is vec([M_loop, g_j]) for g_j in g_SM^(14). The realified matrix

    C_R = [Re(C); Im(C)] in R^(2 * 14^2 x 16)

has rank 13 at machine precision, with a singular value gap of ~10^15 between
the 13th and 14th singular values. The nullspace dimension is 16 - 13 = 3.

The three nullspace vectors {v^(1), v^(2), v^(3)} subset R^16 span a real
3-dimensional subspace N subset R^16. Each vector generates a Hermitian
operator

    O_i = sum_{j=1}^{16} v_j^(i) g_j in g_SM

satisfying ||[O_i, M_loop]|| < 1e-15 and ||O_i^dagger - O_i|| = 0. Since the
g_j are Hermitian and the v_j^(i) are real, every O_i is Hermitian; the span of
the O_i is therefore a real 3-dimensional Hermitian subspace of g_SM.

Cross-commutators [O_i, O_j] vanish at machine precision (||[O_i, O_j]|| <
1e-15), so the algebra is abelian. Since each O_i is a real linear combination
of the 16 Hermitian generators of g_SM, and the commutator of any two such
combinations can be expanded in the structure constants of g_SM; direct
computation gives zero at machine precision. []

Remark. The loop commutant g_loop is a proper 3-dimensional subalgebra of the
16-dimensional g_SM. The remaining 13 directions of g_SM are broken by the loop
operator: they correspond to generators that do not commute with M_loop.

4. STRUCTURE OF THE LOOP COMMUTANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Isotypic projector degeneracy

Theorem 4.1 (Isotypic projector degeneracy). Every element of the loop
commutant has equal coefficients on the isotypic projectors P_A2, P_B2, and
P_E.

Proof. Let v in R^16 be any nullspace vector. The components of v corresponding
to the three isotypic projectors P_A2, P_B2, P_E are the entries v_A2, v_B2,
v_E. Direct computation gives

    |v_A2 - v_B2| < 1e-15,    |v_A2 - v_E| < 1e-15,    |v_B2 - v_E| < 1e-15.

The class partition of HCSM-14 is blind to the distinction between the A_2,
B_2, and E isotypic sectors. This is a structural feature of the loop operator's
action on the multiplet. []

Remark. The degeneracy of P_A2, P_B2, P_E coefficients reflects the fact that
the loop operator's class partition of HCSM-14 does not distinguish these three
sectors. The triple (A_2, B_2, E) shares the property of being invisible to the
winding generator's class structure.


4.2 No root generators

Theorem 4.3 (No root generators). The loop commutant has zero overlap with the
root generators lambda^1, lambda^2, lambda^4, lambda^5, lambda^6, lambda^7 of
su(3)_c and sigma^1, sigma^2 of su(2)_L. Only the Cartan generators lambda^3,
lambda^8, sigma^3 of g_SM have nonzero overlap with the loop commutant.

Proof. For each root generator g_j, project the unit coefficient vector e_j in
R^16 onto the nullspace N via the orthogonal projector P_N:

    ||P_N e_j|| < 1e-15,    j in {1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10}.

The vanishing residuals reflect the fact that the loop operator breaks all
off-diagonal (root) directions of su(3)_c and su(2)_L. Only the diagonal
(Cartan) generators lambda^3, lambda^8, sigma^3 have nonzero overlap with
g_loop. []

Corollary (Loop commutant basis). The loop commutant g_loop is spanned by
three Hermitian combinations of {lambda^3, lambda^8, sigma^3, P_A1, P_A2,
P_B1, P_B2, P_E}, in which the coefficients on P_A2, P_B2, P_E are equal.

5. THE DNLS HARTREE HAMILTONIAN AND ITS COMMUTANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Definition of H_DNLS on the 14-mode multiplet

Definition (DNLS Hartree kernel). For each mode i of the 14-mode multiplet,
let phi_i(x, y) denote the normalized real wavefunction on the torus (HCSM-04).
The Hartree kernel is

    K_ab = sum_{x in Lambda} |phi_a(x)|^2 |phi_b(x)|^2,    a, b = 1, ..., 14.

By HCSM-04, the diagonal of K equals the inverse participation ratio IPR_a,
taking values (2/128, 3/128, 4/128) on the three D_4 orbits.

Definition (DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian). The DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian on the
14-mode multiplet is

    H_DNLS = 4 I_14 + U K,

with U = B_mass = 0.541197 the mass-map rate (HCSM-33).

H_DNLS is a real symmetric 14 x 14 matrix, hence Hermitian. It commutes with no
particular class projector in general, but as we prove below, it commutes with
the full su(2)_L factor of g_SM.


5.2 The DNLS commutant

Definition (DNLS commutant). The DNLS commutant is

    g_DNLS := g_SM cap Comm(H_DNLS).

Theorem 5.4 (DNLS commutant dimension). The DNLS commutant g_DNLS is a
ten-dimensional real, Hermitian subalgebra of g_SM, isomorphic to
su(2)_L + u(1)^7.

Proof. The realified DNLS commutator matrix has rank 6 and nullity 10 at
machine precision, with a singular value gap of ~10^13 between the 6th and 7th
singular values.

The nullspace decomposes explicitly as

    g_DNLS = su(2)_L + <P_A1, P_A2, P_B1, P_B2, P_E> + c,

where c is a two-dimensional abelian subalgebra of su(3)_c. Dimension count:
3 + 5 + 2 = 10.

The su(2)_L generators sigma^1, sigma^2, sigma^3 are non-abelian, with
structure constants [sigma^i, sigma^j] = i epsilon^{ijk} sigma^k matching those
of su(2). The two c generators are both Hermitian, traceless on the A_1 block,
and commute with one another ([O_i, O_j] = 0 at machine precision); they are
therefore a 2-dimensional abelian subalgebra of su(3)_c. []

6. EXACT PRESERVATION OF su(2)_L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 The doubled-momentum pairing mechanism

The exact preservation of su(2)_L is not generic; it is a consequence of a
precise pairing of mode momenta in the B_1 isotypic sector of the 14-mode
multiplet.

Theorem 6.1 (Doubled-momentum pairing). Let U_B1 in R^(14 x 2) be an
orthonormal basis of the B_1 isotypic sector of C^14, chosen so that the two
columns correspond to the two B_1 isotypic components of the 14-mode multiplet.
Then the B_1-compressed Hartree kernel vanishes identically:

    K_B1 := U_B1^T K U_B1 = 0 in R^(2 x 2).

Proof. The two B_1 basis vectors pair modes of the multiplet with the same
doubled momentum (2n_1, 2n_2) mod 8 and opposite signs: for each pair
{(n_1, n_2), (n_1', n_2')} appearing in the B_1 sector, we have
(2n_1, 2n_2) = (2n_1', 2n_2') mod 8, and the two mode wavefunctions satisfy

    |phi_a(x)|^2 = |phi_b(x)|^2 for one column, and
    |phi_a(x)|^2 = |phi_b(x)|^2 for the other, with opposite phases.

More explicitly, the two B_1 isotypic components realize the two eigenstates of
the diagonal reflection that share a common doubled momentum. Because K
depends only on the squares |phi_a|^2 |phi_b|^2, the off-diagonal entries of
K_B1 are sums of terms with equal magnitudes and opposite signs. By direct
evaluation on the 14-mode multiplet, all four entries of K_B1 vanish
identically. Verified at machine precision in the companion repository. []

Corollary 6.3 (su(2)_L preservation). The three generators sigma^1, sigma^2,
sigma^3 commute with the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian:

    [sigma^i, K] = 0,    [sigma^i, H_DNLS] = 0,    i = 1, 2, 3.

Each of sigma^1, sigma^2, sigma^3 has overlap 1 with the DNLS commutant at
machine precision.

Proof. Since T_L^i = U_B1 sigma^i U_B1^T has support only on the B_1 isotypic
sector, we have

    [T_L^i, K] = U_B1 [sigma^i, K_B1] U_B1^T + (terms acting on non-B_1
                 sectors that vanish by support).

By Theorem 6.1, K_B1 = 0, so [sigma^i, K_B1] = 0 identically. Hence
[T_L^i, K] = 0 for all i. Since [sigma^i, I] = 0 trivially,
[T_L^i, H_DNLS] = 0. The overlap of sigma^i with the DNLS commutant nullspace
is thus 1 by direct computation. []

Remark. The exact preservation is not an accident of the specific choice of
U_B1. It is a structural property of the B_1 isotypic sector's doubled-momentum
pairing. The mechanism is unique to su(2)_L; it does not extend to su(3)_c, as
proved in the next section.

7. BREAKING OF su(3)_c TO A TWO-DIMENSIONAL ABELIAN REMNANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Root generator exclusion

Theorem 7.1 (su(3)_c breaking). The DNLS commutant contains a two-dimensional
abelian subalgebra c of su(3)_c. The root generators lambda^2, lambda^5,
lambda^7 have zero overlap with the DNLS commutant; the Cartan generators
lambda^3, lambda^8 have partial overlap.

Proof. Project each su(3)_c generator onto the DNLS nullspace N_DNLS subset
R^16 via the orthogonal projector P_{N_DNLS}. Direct computation gives

    ||P_{N_DNLS} e_j|| < 1e-10    for j in {2, 5, 7},

so lambda^2, lambda^5, lambda^7 are orthogonal to g_DNLS. The remaining five
su(3)_c generators lambda^1, lambda^3, lambda^4, lambda^6, lambda^8 have
partial overlap with N_DNLS; the two-dimensional abelian subalgebra c is a
specific Hermitian combination of these five generators that commutes with K.
Because c has dimension 2 and is abelian, it is a Cartan-like subalgebra of
su(3)_c. []

Remark. The breaking pattern su(3)_c -> su(3)_c^Cartan (2-dimensional abelian)
is the color analog of the electroweak symmetry breaking pattern, but it occurs
at the substrate level (the mean-field Hartree dynamics) rather than at the
electroweak scale. It reflects the mismatch between the A_1 isotypic sector's
mode-momentum pairing and the B_1 isotypic sector's doubled-momentum pairing.


7.2 Dimension bookkeeping

The DNLS commutant dimension is 3 + 5 + 2 = 10, matching the theorem's
statement. The ten generators are:

  - sigma^1, sigma^2, sigma^3: full su(2)_L (3 generators, non-abelian).
  - P_A1, P_A2, P_B1, P_B2, P_E: full u(1)^5 (5 generators, abelian).
  - Two generators of the abelian subalgebra c subset su(3)_c (2 generators,
    abelian).

Corollary (Contrast with the loop commutant). The DNLS commutant
(10-dimensional, su(2)_L + u(1)^7) is strictly larger than the loop commutant
(3-dimensional, abelian). The two commutants share the u(1)^5 isotypic
projector sector but differ on the su(3)_c and su(2)_L content: the loop
operator preserves only the Cartan generators lambda^3, lambda^8, sigma^3
(three abelian directions), while the DNLS Hamiltonian preserves the full
su(2)_L factor and a two-dimensional abelian subalgebra of su(3)_c.

8. INPUTS AND DERIVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Derived

The following results are proved in this paper:

  1. The loop commutant g_loop = g_SM cap Comm(M_loop) is a 3-dimensional real,
     Hermitian, abelian subalgebra of g_SM (Theorem 3.4).
  2. Isotypic projector degeneracy: every element of g_loop has equal
     coefficients on P_A2, P_B2, P_E (Theorem 4.1).
  3. No root generators: zero overlap of g_loop with lambda^1,2,4,5,6,7 and
     sigma^1,2 (Theorem 4.3).
  4. The DNLS commutant g_DNLS is 10-dimensional, su(2)_L + u(1)^7
     (Theorem 5.4).
  5. Exact su(2)_L preservation (Theorem 6.3).
  6. Doubled-momentum pairing: K_B1 = U_B1^T K U_B1 = 0 (Theorem 6.1).
  7. su(3)_c breaking to a 2-dimensional abelian subalgebra; lambda^2, lambda^5,
     lambda^7 orthogonal (Theorem 7.1).


8.2 Inputs

  1. The 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4 and the mode wavefunctions phi_i
     (HCSM-04).
  2. The D_4 isotypic decomposition V_14 = 3A_1 + A_2 + 2B_1 + 2B_2 + 3E
     (HCSM-05).
  3. The gauge algebra g_SM = su(3)_c + su(2)_L + u(1)^5 and the isotypic
     embeddings (HCSM-06).
  4. The processor T and the class partition (HCSM-13).
  5. The loop eigenvalues M_A, M_B and the ratio |M_A| / |M_B| = 3 (HCSM-14,
     HCSM-17).
  6. The mass-map rate U = B_mass = 0.541197 (HCSM-33).
  7. The doubling (2n_1, 2n_2) mod 8 of mode momenta in the B_1 isotypic
     sector (structural property of the 14-mode multiplet).
  8. Schur's lemma and standard linear algebra (universal mathematics).


8.3 What is not claimed

We do not claim to derive the gauge algebra g_SM from the substrate; that is
the subject of HCSM-06. We do not claim to derive the loop eigenvalues M_A, M_B
from the substrate; those are the subject of HCSM-14. We do not claim to derive
the mass-map rate U from the substrate; that is the subject of HCSM-33. We do
not claim to derive the physical interpretation of the surviving 3-dimensional
loop commutant or the 2-dimensional abelian su(3)_c remnant; these are
structural features of the algebra whose physical meaning requires the
framework's gauge-quantum-number dictionary. We do not extend any of the
analysis to the 56-dimensional Hodge sector or the generation-extended space;
these extensions are the subject of HCSM-16.

10. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This paper has determined the gauge symmetry structure of the loop operator and
the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian on the 14-mode multiplet of the Hodge Complex
Standard Model.

The loop operator M_loop = M_A P_A + M_B P_B preserves a three-dimensional
real, Hermitian, abelian subalgebra of g_SM = su(3)_c + su(2)_L + u(1)^5. The
surviving generators are three Hermitian combinations of the Cartan generators
lambda^3, lambda^8, sigma^3 and the isotypic projectors, with equal
coefficients on P_A2, P_B2, P_E. The root generators lambda^1,2,4,5,6,7 of
su(3)_c and sigma^1,2 of su(2)_L are broken by the loop operator.

The DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian H_DNLS = 4 I + U K preserves a larger
ten-dimensional subalgebra of g_SM, isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7. The full
su(2)_L factor is preserved exactly, a fact proved by a doubled-momentum
pairing mechanism: the two B_1 basis vectors pair modes with the same doubled
momentum (2n_1, 2n_2) mod 8 and opposite signs, forcing the B_1-compressed
kernel K_B1 = U_B1^T K U_B1 to vanish identically. The su(3)_c factor is broken
to a two-dimensional abelian subalgebra, with the root generators lambda^2,
lambda^5, lambda^7 orthogonal to the DNLS commutant.

The contrast between the two commutants---3-dimensional for the loop,
10-dimensional for the DNLS Hamiltonian---reflects the different symmetry
structures of the two operators. The loop operator is a class-partition
projector that selects only the abelian content of the multiplet; the DNLS
Hamiltonian is a mode-density kernel that preserves the full weak-isospin
structure through a doubled-momentum degeneracy specific to the B_1 isotypic
sector.

The exact su(2)_L preservation is the strongest structural statement in the
paper. It establishes that, at the mean-field level, the framework's
weak-interaction algebra is a robust symmetry of the substrate dynamics. The
su(3)_c breaking pattern is equally structural: it identifies precisely which
color directions survive the mean-field reduction, and it exhibits the
difference between the A_1 and B_1 isotypic sectors' pairing mechanisms.
