The mass map of the Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) has rate
B_mass = (d-1)kL^2/(2N^2). The coefficient (d-1)/2, until now assumed, is
derived here by two independent methods.

Method 1 (processor F/B split). The processor T = I - P_C of HCSM-13 splits
the 14-mode multiplet into a fermion sector F of dimension |F| = d(d+1)/2 and
a boson sector B of dimension |B| = d. The internal ratio
(|F| - |B|)/|B| = (d-1)/2. The companion ratio |F|/|B| = (d+1)/2 is the
dark-energy coefficient of HCSM-53.

Method 2 (Hartree orbit trace). The DNLS Hartree matrix K on the 14-mode
multiplet, compressed to the three D_4 orbits O_0, O_1, O_2, satisfies

    Tr K_{O_0} = Tr K_{O_2} = 2/N^2,    Tr K_{O_1} = 3/N^2,

exactly in rational arithmetic. The ratio Tr K_{O_1} / Tr K_{O_0} = 3/2 = (d-1)/2.
This is the mass-map coefficient.

The two derivations agree exactly. Rank 5 of the HCSM blocker list is closed:
the mass-map coefficient is a theorem, not a fitted parameter.
