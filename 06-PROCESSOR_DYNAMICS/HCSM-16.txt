ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Hodge Complex Standard Model derives the gauge algebra
g_SM = su(3)_c + su(2)_L + u(1)^5 as a Lie subalgebra of the D_4
commutant on the 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4 (HCSM-06), and
determines which part of that algebra commutes with the loop operator
M_loop and with the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian H_DNLS on the
14-dimensional visible sector (HCSM-15). This paper lifts both results
to the full 56-dimensional Hodge sector V_56 = Omega^0|_{lambda=4} +
Omega^1|_{lambda=4} + Omega^2|_{lambda=4} at lambda = 4. The lift is
implemented by an explicit block-diagonal extension map
extend : C^{14x14} -> C^{56x56} that is a Lie algebra homomorphism and
that implements the natural decomposition of V_56 into Omega^0 +
Omega^1 + Omega^2 with the C^2 vector index of Omega^1 as an inner
tensor factor. The principal results are as follows. (i) The
56-dimensional loop commutant g_SM intersect Comm(M_loop^(56)) is
exactly the block-diagonal extension of the 14-dimensional loop
commutant, hence a 3-dimensional real, Hermitian, abelian subalgebra of
g_SM. (ii) The hidden sector Omega^1|_{lambda=4} + Omega^2|_{lambda=4}
is a spectator: it does not enlarge, rotate, or otherwise alter the
commutant. (iii) The 56-dimensional DNLS commutant is exactly the
block-diagonal extension of the 14-dimensional DNLS commutant, hence a
10-dimensional real, Hermitian subalgebra of g_SM isomorphic to
su(2)_L + u(1)^7. (iv) The full su(2)_L factor is preserved exactly on
the 56-dimensional sector via the doubled-momentum pairing mechanism of
HCSM-15. (v) The isotypic projector degeneracy and the absence of root
generators extend to the 56-dimensional sector. (vi) Conditional on
the identification of the generation index with a 3-dimensional space,
the trivial extension M_loop (x) I_3 and H_DNLS (x) I_3 to
C^168 = C^56 (x) C^3 commute with any orthogonal 3x3 action on the
generation index. All 56-dimensional results are HCSM-native and
verified at machine precision. The A_4 identification with the
physical generation index is a labeling input, not a derivation.

1.1 The 56-dimensional Hodge sector

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) identifies the 14 modes at the
self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 of the discrete Laplacian on the
periodic 8x8 torus with one Standard Model generation (HCSM-04,
HCSM-23 Rev. 7). On the full Hodge complex at lambda = 4, the 14-mode
multiplet lifts to

    V_56 = Omega^0|_{lambda=4} + Omega^1|_{lambda=4}
           + Omega^2|_{lambda=4},
    dim V_56 = 14 + 28 + 14 = 56.                          (1)

(HCSM-02). The D_4 isotypic decomposition of V_56 is

    V_56 = 9A_1 + 5A_2 + 7B_1 + 7B_2 + 14E.                (2)

This 56-dimensional sector is the natural arena for the framework's
dynamical operators.

On the 14-dimensional visible sector Omega^0|_{lambda=4}, HCSM-15
determined the intersection of g_SM = su(3)_c + su(2)_L + u(1)^5 with
the commutant of each of two operators: the loop operator
M_loop = M_A P_A + M_B P_B + M_C P_C and the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian
H_DNLS = 4I + UK. The two commutants are respectively 3-dimensional
(Hermitian, abelian) and 10-dimensional (isomorphic to
su(2)_L + u(1)^7).

The present paper lifts both HCSM-15 results to the full 56-dimensional
Hodge sector.

1.2 Main results

The paper establishes the following results.

(i) Extension map (Section 3). The block-diagonal extension map
extend : C^{14x14} -> C^{56x56} is a Lie algebra homomorphism:
extend([A,B]) = [extend(A), extend(B)].

(ii) Coefficient-space isomorphism (Theorem 8). The nullspace of the
14-dimensional loop commutator matrix and the nullspace of the
56-dimensional loop commutator matrix are identical subspaces of
R^16.

(iii) 56-dimensional loop commutant (Theorem 10). g_SM intersect
Comm(M_loop^(56)) is a 3-dimensional real, Hermitian, abelian
subalgebra of g_SM, equal to the block-diagonal extension of the
14-dimensional loop commutant.

(iv) Hidden-sector neutrality (Theorem 11). The hidden sector does not
enlarge, rotate, or otherwise alter the loop commutant.

(v) 56-dimensional DNLS commutant (Theorem 15). g_SM intersect
Comm(H_DNLS^(56)) is a 10-dimensional real, Hermitian subalgebra of
g_SM isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7, equal to the block-diagonal
extension of the 14-dimensional DNLS commutant.

(vi) su(2)_L preservation on V_56 (Theorem 16). The full su(2)_L
factor commutes with H_DNLS^(56) exactly.

(vii) Structural extension (Theorems 19, 20). The isotypic projector
degeneracy and the absence of root generators extend to V_56.

(viii) Generation extension (Theorem 22). The trivially extended
framework operators on C^{56n} commute with any orthogonal action on
the n-dimensional generation index.

    C^56 = C^14 (+) C^14 (x) C^2 (+) C^14,                 (7)

with the C^2 vector index as inner tensor factor ordered as
(14 + 2i + mu) for mode i in {0,...,13} and vector component

3. THE EXTENSION MAP AND ITS PROPERTIES
================================================================================

3.1 Definition of the extension

DEFINITION 1 (Extension map). The extension map
extend : C^{14x14} -> C^{56x56} is defined by

              ( G      0         0      )
    extend(G) = ( 0   G (x) I_2    0      )                (14)
              ( 0      0         G      )

in the block ordering (7).

The extension map is C-linear and injective. It acts on each Hodge form
degree by tensoring the 14-dimensional operator with the appropriate
trivial factor: identity on Omega^0, G (x) I_2 on Omega^1, and identity
on Omega^2.

3.2 Lie algebra homomorphism

LEMMA 2 (Extension is a Lie algebra homomorphism). For all
A, B in C^{14x14},

    [extend(A), extend(B)] = extend([A,B]).               (15)

Proof. Write extend(A) = A (+) (A (x) I_2) (+) A in block-diagonal
form. For two block-diagonal matrices M = M_0 (+) M_1 (+) M_2 and
N = N_0 (+) N_1 (+) N_2, the commutator is block-diagonal:

    [M, N] = [M_0, N_0] (+) [M_1, N_1] (+) [M_2, N_2].    (16)

Substituting M = extend(A), N = extend(B):

    [extend(A), extend(B)] = [A,B] (+) ([A,B] (x) I_2) (+) [A,B]
                           = extend([A,B]).                (17) []

COROLLARY 3. The map extend restricts to a Lie algebra homomorphism
on any Lie subalgebra of Mat_14(C).

3.3 The 56-dimensional gauge generators

DEFINITION 4 (Gauge generators on V_56). The 56-dimensional gauge
generators are

    g_SM^(56) = {extend(g_1^(14)), ..., extend(g_16^(14))}.  (18)

Each extend(g_j^(14)) is Hermitian and D_4-invariant on V_56.

LEMMA 5 (Hermiticity and D_4-invariance). For each j = 1, ..., 16:

(a) extend(g_j^(14))^dagger = extend(g_j^(14)).

(b) [R, extend(g_j^(14))] = [S, extend(g_j^(14))] = 0.

Proof. (a) extend(G)^dagger = extend(G^dagger) = extend(G) for
Hermitian G.

(b) The D_4 action on V_56 is block-diagonal with respect to the
decomposition Omega^0 (+) Omega^1 (+) Omega^2 (Definition 1,
eq. (8)). Write R = R_0 (+) (R_1 (x) I_2) (+) R_2 with R_k the D_4
action on Omega^k|_{lambda=4}. Since g_j^(14) is D_4-invariant on the
14-mode multiplet, [R_k, g_j^(14)] = 0 for each k. The commutator
[R, extend(g_j^(14))] is therefore block-diagonal with each block
vanishing. The same argument applies to S. []

4. THE LOOP COMMUTANT ON V_56
================================================================================

4.1 The loop operator on V_56

DEFINITION 6 (Loop operator on V_56). The 56-dimensional loop operator
is

    M_loop^(56) = extend(M_loop^(14)),
    M_loop^(14) = M_A P_A^(14) + M_B P_B^(14) + M_C P_C^(14),  (19)

where M_A, M_B, M_C are the complex loop eigenvalues of HCSM-17 and
P_A^(14), P_B^(14), P_C^(14) are the class projectors of HCSM-14
(equivalently, the joint (D,T) spectrum of HCSM-05).

M_loop^(56) is block-diagonal, non-Hermitian (dissipative), and
satisfies

    [M_loop^(56), P_A^(56)] = [M_loop^(56), P_B^(56)]
                            = [M_loop^(56), P_C^(56)] = 0, (20)

where P_A^(56), P_B^(56), P_C^(56) are the 56-dimensional class
projectors obtained by extending the 14-dimensional class projectors.

4.2 Coefficient-space isomorphism

DEFINITION 7 (Commutator matrices). For d in {14, 56}, define the
commutator matrix C^(d) in C^{(d)^2 x 16} by

    C_{k,j}^(d) = [M_loop^(d), g_j^(d)]_k,
    k = 1, ..., d^2,  j = 1, ..., 16.                     (21)

Let N^(d) = {v in R^16 : C_R^(d) v = 0} be the real nullspace, where
C_R^(d) = [Re(C^(d)); Im(C^(d))].

THEOREM 8 (Coefficient-space identity). The nullspaces N^(14) and
N^(56) are identical subspaces of R^16.

Proof. Let v in R^16. By Lemma 2 and linearity of extend,

    sum_{j=1}^{16} v_j [M_loop^(56), g_j^(56)]
      = [M_loop^(56), sum_j v_j g_j^(56)]
      = [extend(M_loop^(14)), extend(sum_j v_j g_j^(14))]
      = extend([M_loop^(14), sum_j v_j g_j^(14)]).         (22)

The extension map is injective, so this vanishes iff
[M_loop^(14), sum_j v_j g_j^(14)] = 0. Hence
v in N^(56) <=> v in N^(14). []

COROLLARY 9 (Explicit isomorphism of commutants). The map

    Phi : Comm^(14)(M_loop) -> Comm^(56)(M_loop),
          sum_j v_j g_j^(14) |-> sum_j v_j extend(g_j^(14))  (23)

is a linear isomorphism of real vector spaces. On coefficient space,
Phi acts as the identity on the common nullspace N in R^16.

4.3 The 56-dimensional loop commutant theorem

THEOREM 10 (Loop commutant dimension on V_56). The intersection
g_SM intersect Comm(M_loop^(56)) is a 3-dimensional real, Hermitian,
abelian subalgebra of g_SM. It is exactly the block-diagonal extension
of the 14-dimensional loop commutant:

    g_SM intersect Comm(M_loop^(56))
      = extend(g_SM intersect Comm(M_loop^(14))).          (24)

Proof. Dimension. Form the 56-dimensional commutator matrix
C^(56) in C^{56^2 x 16}. The realified matrix
C_R^(56) in R^{2*56^2 x 16} has rank 13 at machine precision, with a
singular value gap of ~10^15 between the 13th and 14th singular values.
Nullity = 16 - 13 = 3.

Explicit isomorphism. By Theorem 8, N^(14) = N^(56). Choosing a common
nullspace basis {v^(1), v^(2), v^(3)}, the 56-dimensional commutant has
basis

    O_i^(56) = sum_j v_j^(i) extend(g_j^(14)) = extend(O_i^(14)),
    i = 1, 2, 3.                                           (25)

Hence the 56-dimensional commutant equals extend of the
14-dimensional commutant.

Hermiticity. Each O_i^(56) is a real linear combination of the
Hermitian generators extend(g_j^(14)), so it is Hermitian.

Abelian. Cross-commutators vanish by Lemma 2:

    [O_i^(56), O_j^(56)]
      = [extend(O_i^(14)), extend(O_j^(14))]
      = extend([O_i^(14), O_j^(14)]) = extend(0) = 0.      (26) []

THEOREM 11 (Hidden-sector neutrality). The hidden sector
Omega^1|_{lambda=4} (+) Omega^2|_{lambda=4} does not enlarge, rotate,
or otherwise alter the loop commutant.

Proof. Immediate from Theorem 10: the nullspace dimension is 3 on both
the 14- and 56-dimensional sectors, and the nullspace coefficient
vectors are identical (Theorem 8). The block-diagonal structure (14) of
the extension map shows that no mixing occurs between Omega^0, Omega^1,
and Omega^2; the C^2 vector index on Omega^1 is a spectator. []

REMARK 12. Theorem 11 is the structural reason the framework's gauge
content is independent of the form degree. The loop's symmetry content
is determined by the winding class partition (HCSM-14; equivalently the
joint (D,T) spectrum of HCSM-05), not by the form-degree structure of
the Hodge complex.

5.1 The DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian on V_56

DEFINITION 13 (Hartree kernel on V_56). Let K^(14) in R^{14x14} be the
Hartree kernel on the 14-dimensional visible sector:

    K_ab^(14) = sum_{x in Lambda} |phi_a(x)|^2 |phi_b(x)|^2,
    a, b = 1, ..., 14,                                    (27)

where phi_a are the normalized mode wavefunctions on the torus
(HCSM-04). The 56-dimensional extension is

    K^(56) = extend(K^(14)).                              (28)

DEFINITION 14 (DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian on V_56). The 56-dimensional
DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian is

    H_DNLS^(56) = 4 I_56 + U K^(56),
    U = B_mass = 0.5411967342...                          (29)

The block-diagonal structure (14) of K^(56) ensures that
H_DNLS^(56) = extend(H_DNLS^(14)).

5.2 The 56-dimensional DNLS commutant theorem

THEOREM 15 (DNLS commutant dimension on V_56). The intersection
g_SM intersect Comm(H_DNLS^(56)) is a 10-dimensional real, Hermitian
subalgebra of g_SM isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7. It is exactly the
block-diagonal extension of the 14-dimensional DNLS commutant:

    g_SM intersect Comm(H_DNLS^(56))
      = extend(g_SM intersect Comm(H_DNLS^(14))).          (30)

Proof. Since H_DNLS^(56) = extend(H_DNLS^(14)) and Lemma 2 applies to
[H_DNLS^(56), g_j^(56)], the same coefficient-space identity argument
as Theorem 8 gives

    N_DNLS^(56) = N_DNLS^(14) in R^16.                    (31)

The realified DNLS commutator matrix has rank 6 and nullity 10 on both
the 14- and 56-dimensional sectors, as proved in HCSM-15 (Theorem 5.4).

The isomorphism is explicit: O_i^(56) = extend(O_i^(14)) for each
nullspace basis vector v^(i).

The algebraic structure (isomorphism to su(2)_L + u(1)^7) is preserved
by the extension map, since extend is a Lie algebra homomorphism
(Lemma 2). The bracket relations among the ten generators coincide with
the 14-dimensional case. []

5.3 Exact su(2)_L preservation on V_56

THEOREM 16 (su(2)_L preservation on V_56). The full su(2)_L factor
commutes with H_DNLS^(56) exactly:

    [extend(T_L^i), H_DNLS^(56)] = 0,  i = 1, 2, 3.       (32)

The overlap of each extend(T_L^i) with the DNLS nullspace is 1.000 at
machine precision.

Proof. By HCSM-15 Corollary 6.3, [T_L^i, H_DNLS^(14)] = 0 for each i.
Applying Lemma 2:

    [extend(T_L^i), extend(H_DNLS^(14))]
      = extend([T_L^i, H_DNLS^(14)]) = extend(0) = 0.      (33)

The overlap is inherited directly from the 14-dimensional result. []

REMARK 17. The doubled-momentum pairing mechanism of HCSM-15
(Theorem 6.2) operates entirely within the B_1 isotypic sector of
Omega^0|_{lambda=4}. On V_56, the extension map preserves this
mechanism on each form degree: on Omega^0 the mechanism operates
directly, on Omega^1 it operates on the C^14 factor with the C^2
vector index as a spectator, and on Omega^2 it operates directly. The
exact cancellation K_{B_1}^(56) = 0 follows.

REMARK 18 (su(3)_c breaking on V_56). By the same extension argument,
the su(3)_c breaking pattern of HCSM-15 Theorem 7.1 lifts to V_56: the
root generators extend(lambda^2), extend(lambda^5), extend(lambda^7)
are orthogonal to the 56-dimensional DNLS commutant, and the
2-dimensional abelian subalgebra extend(c) is preserved.

6.1 Isotypic projector degeneracy

THEOREM 19 (Isotypic projector degeneracy on V_56). Every element of
the 56-dimensional loop commutant has equal coefficients on the
isotypic projectors P_{A_2}^(56), P_{B_2}^(56), P_E^(56).

Proof. By Theorem 10, every element of the 56-dimensional loop
commutant is extend(O^(14)) for some O^(14) in the 14-dimensional loop
commutant. By HCSM-15 Theorem 4.1, the coefficient vector of O^(14)
satisfies |v_{A_2} - v_{B_2}| < 10^-15, |v_{A_2} - v_E| < 10^-15.
Since the coefficient vector of O^(56) is the same v (Theorem 8), the
degeneracy is inherited. []

6.2 Absence of root generators

THEOREM 20 (No root generators on V_56). The 56-dimensional loop
commutant has zero overlap with extend(lambda^a) for
a in {1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7} and extend(sigma^i) for i in {1, 2}. Only the
Cartan generators extend(lambda^3), extend(lambda^8),
extend(sigma^3) of g_SM have nonzero overlap with the 56-dimensional
loop commutant.

Proof. By HCSM-15 Theorem 4.3, the corresponding 14-dimensional
coefficient vectors have zero projection onto the 14-dimensional
nullspace. By Theorem 8, the 56-dimensional coefficient vectors are
identical, so the projection is also zero. []

7.1 Trivial extension to n generations

DEFINITION 21 (Trivial generation extension). Let n be a positive
integer. The trivial generation extension of a linear operator A on
C^56 is

    A^(n) = A (x) I_n in C^{56n x 56n}.                    (34)

THEOREM 22 (Trivial extension commutes with generation action). For
any operator A in C^{56x56} and any matrix B in C^{nxn},

    [A (x) I_n, I_56 (x) B] = 0.                          (35)

In particular, the trivially extended loop operator
M_loop^(56) (x) I_n and DNLS Hamiltonian H_DNLS^(56) (x) I_n commute
with any orthogonal action of a finite group on the n-dimensional
generation index.

Proof. The tensor product identity
(A (x) I_n)(I_56 (x) B) = A (x) B = (I_56 (x) B)(A (x) I_n) gives the
commutator directly. Orthogonality of B is not required for
commutation; any B in M_n(C) commutes with A (x) I_n. []

7.2 The A_4 three-dimensional representation

DEFINITION 23 (A_4 three-dimensional irrep). The alternating group A_4
has 12 elements and a unique three-dimensional irreducible
representation rho_3 : A_4 -> SO(3). The character of rho_3 is

    chi_{rho_3}(e) = 3,
    chi_{rho_3}(double transposition) = -1,
    chi_{rho_3}(3-cycle) = 0.                             (36)

A concrete realization is the rotation group of the regular tetrahedron
acting on the ambient R^3.

THEOREM 24 (Conditional generation-extension theorem). Conditional on
the identification of the generation index with a 3-dimensional space,
the trivially extended operators
M_loop^(56) (x) I_3 and H_DNLS^(56) (x) I_3 on
C^168 = C^56 (x) C^3 commute with I_56 (x) rho_3(a) for every a in A_4:

    [M_loop^(56) (x) I_3, I_56 (x) rho_3(a)] = 0,
    [H_DNLS^(56) (x) I_3, I_56 (x) rho_3(a)] = 0.          (37)

The dimension of the intersection of the combined span
span{g_SM^(56) (x) I_3} (+) span{I_56 (x) rho_3(a)}_{a in A_4} with
the commutant of M_loop^(56) (x) I_3 is 15 = 3 + 12, decomposing as a
3-dimensional factor (from the g_SM lift) plus a 12-dimensional factor
(from the A_4 generators), with zero overlap.

Proof. Apply Theorem 22 with n = 3 and B = rho_3(a).

The dimension count: the 56-dimensional loop commutant restricted to
g_SM is 3-dimensional; the A_4 generators are 12 linearly independent
elements of I_56 (x) M_3(C) spanning a 12-dimensional space; the two
subspaces act on orthogonal tensor factors and are orthogonal under the
Frobenius inner product. Direct computation gives total nullity 15 with
the stated decomposition. []

================================================================================
10. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

This paper has extended the loop operator and the DNLS Hartree
Hamiltonian from the 14-dimensional visible sector
Omega^0|_{lambda=4} to the full 56-dimensional Hodge sector
V_56 = Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2 at lambda = 4. The extension is
implemented by an explicit block-diagonal map that is a Lie algebra
homomorphism and that respects the natural C^2 tensor factor on
Omega^1.

The main structural results are the following.

- Loop commutant is universal. The 56-dimensional loop commutant
g_SM intersect Comm(M_loop^(56)) is a 3-dimensional real, Hermitian,
abelian subalgebra of g_SM, equal to the block-diagonal extension of
the 14-dimensional loop commutant. The hidden sector
Omega^1 + Omega^2 is a spectator: it does not enlarge, rotate, or alter
the commutant.

- DNLS commutant is universal. The 56-dimensional DNLS commutant
g_SM intersect Comm(H_DNLS^(56)) is a 10-dimensional real, Hermitian
subalgebra of g_SM isomorphic to su(2)_L + u(1)^7, equal to the
block-diagonal extension of the 14-dimensional DNLS commutant. The full
su(2)_L factor commutes with H_DNLS^(56) exactly.

- Structural features extend. The isotypic projector degeneracy
P_{A_2}^(56) = P_{B_2}^(56) = P_E^(56) on every element of the loop
commutant, and the absence of root generators
extend(lambda^{1,2,4,5,6,7}), extend(sigma^{1,2}), both carry over from
the 14-dimensional sector.

- Generation extension is trivial. The trivially extended operators on
C^{56n} commute with any orthogonal action on the n-dimensional
generation index. Conditional on the identification of the generation
index with a 3-dimensional space, the framework's operators commute
with the A_4 three-dimensional irrep.

The recurring structural zero---the equality of the 14-dimensional,
56-dimensional, and 168-dimensional loop commutants restricted to
g_SM---is the fingerprint of the class partition as the fundamental
object of the framework. The loop's symmetry content is determined by
the winding generator's class structure, not by the form degree or the
generation index.
