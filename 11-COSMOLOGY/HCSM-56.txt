ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We construct the HCSM-native effective field theory (EFT) of the residual
vacuum on the 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4. The EFT is a real scalar
perturbation phi on the multiplet with mode equation

    phi'' + Gamma_eff phi' + (k^2/a^2 + m_eff^2) phi = 0

where the effective damping Gamma_eff = (3 + zeta H / rho) H_local and
effective mass m_eff^2 = m_s^2 + zeta R are derived HCSM-natively from the
five-layer loop and the stability coefficient gamma of HCSM-54. The EFT
parameters are derived exactly:

    w = -1 - (1-gamma)^2 / (2 pi) = -1.014054...
    zeta H / rho = (1-gamma)^2 / (6 pi) = 0.0046847...
    m_s = (M_P/N) exp(-(d+1)N/(d-1)) = 9.0247e-30 GeV

The EFT regime is underdamped, mass-dominated, and gradient-negligible. The
residual vacuum energy of the multiplet is propagated through the EFT layer
with soft-projection weight 1 + c * delta (HCSM-14.a, HCSM-52), and the
physical mechanism by which the residual is promoted to the observed
cosmological constant is derived in the companion sub-paper HCSM-56.a. All
EFT parameters are HCSM-native; no imported machinery (no entropy-driven-
force layer, no 2D mass-regulated bubble, no gamma_f = 1 convention)
appears. All numerical claims are verified at machine precision.

multiplet at lambda = 4 (HCSM-04). It carries the fermion and boson

    rho_res = (d+1)/2 * m_nu^4   (HCSM-53)

The question this paper addresses is: what is the effective field theory
of small perturbations of the residual vacuum? That is, if the multiplet
is displaced slightly from the vacuum configuration, what is the mode
equation for the perturbation phi, and what are the effective parameters
(w, zeta H / rho, m_s) that control it?

wave equation, whose coefficients are derived HCSM-natively from the

The physical mechanism by which the resulting residual vacuum energy is
promoted to the observed cosmological constant is the subject of the
companion sub-paper HCSM-56.a.

------------------------

multiplet. By D_4 invariance of the multiplet (HCSM-05) and F-invariance
of the kernel (HCSM-14.a), the EFT perturbation is D_4-invariant to

with kinetic, damping, and mass terms.

Theorem 1 (Mode equation).
The perturbation phi satisfies the damped wave equation:

    phi'' + Gamma_eff phi' + (k^2/a^2 + m_eff^2) phi = 0

with effective damping

    Gamma_eff = (3 + zeta H / rho) H_local

and effective mass

    m_eff^2 = m_s^2 + zeta R

Proof.
The EFT perturbation is sourced by the Core gradient flow (HCSM-55) and
damped by the DME dissipative rate (HCSM-21, T_DME = 1/(2 gamma)). The
scalar field satisfies the standard cosmological perturbation equation,
with the damping term Gamma_eff combining the Hubble friction 3H and the
bulk-viscosity contribution zeta H / rho. The effective mass combines the
bare scalar mass m_s (from the multiplet) and the curvature coupling
zeta R.  []

    phi'' + (m_s^2 + O((zeta H / rho)^2)) phi = 0

for all modes in the observable regime. The multiplet is scalar-uniform
under the EFT.  []

7. FALSIFICATION TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    (1) Equation of state. w = -1.014054... at EFT order. DESI DR3 +
        Euclid should test this at > 3 sigma. A measured value consistent
        with w = -1 exactly (no EFT correction) at high significance would
        falsify the EFT-layer identification.

    (2) Bulk viscosity. zeta H / rho = 0.0046847.... Euclid growth-rate
        and cosmic-shear analyses can constrain zeta H / rho; a value
        outside the interval [0.004, 0.0055] would falsify Theorem 3.

    (3) Underdamped regime. Gamma_eff / omega_0 = 5.2e-13. Any detection
        of overdamped behavior (a real characteristic root with no
        oscillation) would falsify Theorem 5.

    (4) Scalar mass. m_s = 9.0247e-30 GeV. A detected scalar at a
        different mass (from fifth-force or equivalence-principle tests)
        would falsify Theorem 4.

    (5) Uniform mode frequency. All 14 modes oscillate at omega_0 = m_s.
        A detection of mode-dependent oscillation frequencies in the
        residual vacuum would falsify Corollary 5.1.

    (6) Soft-projection weight. L_EFT | kappa_14 = (1 + 5 delta / 9) I_14.
        A measured projection weight different from 1 + 5 delta / 9 would
        falsify Theorem 6 and, by extension, the Kernel-Diagonal Theorem
        (HCSM-14.a).
