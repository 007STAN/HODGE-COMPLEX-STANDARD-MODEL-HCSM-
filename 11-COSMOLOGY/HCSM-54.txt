ABSTRACT
================================================================================

We establish the vacuum stability sector of the Hodge Complex Standard
Model (HCSM) and derive the bridge identity linking the stability
coefficient to the Higgs quartic coupling. The stability coefficient

    gamma = c_2 / I(2) =
    0.70283946799007245929257819650854315216110316215188...

controls the width of the vacuum entropy well
S(eps)/S_0 = 1 - gamma*eps^2 + O(eps^4) around D = 4. We prove four
structural results and one bridge theorem.

Structural results:

  (i)   The strip integral at alpha = 2 has the exact closed form
        I(2) = 1 - (1/2) AGM(1, sqrt(2)),
        verified at 100-digit precision.

  (ii)  The AGM constant is a substrate-level invariant of the self-dual
        square torus:
        AGM(1, sqrt(2)) = 1 / (sqrt(2) * eta(i)^2)
        with eta(i) = Gamma(1/4) / (2 pi^{3/4}).

  (iii) The second derivative c_2 = 2 I''(2) has a closed form in pi,
        Gamma(1/4), and Catalan's constant G.

  (iv)  The Brillouin-zone moments of the three crossover-zero integrands
        on the 8x8 torus are exactly 4, 12, 28 following d(2^n - 1) for
        n = 1, 2, 3 with d = 4. The mirror ratio 1/3 = 1/(d-1) is the
        generation-count denominator.

Bridge theorem. The mirror-breaking residual

    delta_edge = sqrt(2)/gamma - 2 = 0.0121430664919214255...

controls the relation between gamma and the Higgs quartic coupling
derived in HCSM-40:

    lambda_tree = (gamma^2/d) (1 - delta_edge/[2(d-1)]),
    gamma       = sqrt(d*lambda_tree) (1 + delta_edge/[4(d-1)]).

The identity holds to 8.4e-8 in lambda_tree and 1.6e-6 in gamma - both
inside the M_P-limited precision budget. The quartic coupling (HCSM-40)
and the vacuum stability coefficient (HCSM-54) are the same substrate
quantity up to the mirror-breaking residual.

Negative result. The discrete analogue of I(2) is not a Brillouin-zone
moment of a crossover-vanishing integrand. The substrate-level
derivation proceeds through the modular structure of the self-dual
square torus.

Derivation status summary:

  DERIVED (substrate-level):
    - tau = i for the square lattice.
    - eta(i) = Gamma(1/4)/(2 pi^{3/4}) (Chowla-Selberg).
    - AGM(1, sqrt(2)) = 1/(sqrt(2) eta(i)^2).
    - I(2) = 1 - 1/(2 sqrt(2) eta(i)^2).
    - Crossover-zero moments 4, 12, 28.
    - Mirror ratio 1/3 = 1/(d-1).
    - S''(0)/S_0 = -2 gamma < 0.
    - Bridge identity lambda_tree = (gamma^2/d)(1 - delta_edge/6)
      at 8.4e-8.

  DERIVED (analytic):
    - AGM identity via Beta function.
    - Closed form of c_2 = 2 I''(2).
    - Closed form of gamma = c_2/I(2).

  DISCLOSED IDENTIFICATION:
    - gamma = c_2/I(2).

  NEGATIVE RESULT:
    - No BZ moment of a crossover-vanishing integrand equals I(2).

  OPEN:
    - Exact coefficient in the kL correction (M_P-limited).
    - Finite-L corrections to the crossover moments.
    - Connection to the sigma-line structure of HCSM-24.

2. SETUP: THE STRIP INTEGRAL AND GAMMA
================================================================================

2.1 The dimensional deviation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lambda = Z8 x Z8, N = 64, vacuum manifold dimension D = 4,
deviation eps = D - 4. Entropy well:

  S(eps)/S_0 = 1 - gamma eps^2 + O(eps^4).

D = 4 is a strict local maximum since S''(0)/S_0 = -2 gamma < 0.

2.2 The strip integral
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  I(alpha) = integral_1^inf [ u^{2 alpha - 2} / sqrt(u^{2 alpha} - 1)
                              - u^{alpha - 2} ] du
           = (1/(2 alpha)) [ B(a, 1/2) - 1/a ],
             a = -1/2 + 1/(2 alpha).

At alpha = 2:  a = -1/4,  I(2) = 1 + B(-1/4, 1/2)/4.

2.3 The stability coefficient
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  gamma = c_2 / I(2),   c_2 = 2 I''(2),

  gamma = 0.70283946799007245929257819650854315216110316215188...


================================================================================
3. THE AGM CLOSED FORM
================================================================================

Theorem 3.1 (AGM identity, analytic).

  I(2) = 1 - (1/2) AGM(1, sqrt(2))
       = 1 - sqrt(2) pi^{3/2} / Gamma(1/4)^2.

Theorem 3.2 (Closed form of c_2).

  c_2 = 4 + B(-1/4, 1/2) [ (pi+4)/8 + (pi+4)^2/128 + 1/4
                            + (1-G)/8 ],

with G Catalan's constant.

Theorem 3.3 (Closed form of gamma).

  gamma = c_2 / I(2) = 0.70283946799007245929257819650854315216110316215188...

Theorem 3.4 (Substrate-level AGM).

  AGM(1, sqrt(2)) = 1 / (sqrt(2) eta(i)^2),
  eta(i) = Gamma(1/4) / (2 pi^{3/4}).

Proof sketch. The 8x8 lattice is square, so its modular parameter is
tau = i (the unique self-dual point of PSL(2, Z)). Chowla-Selberg
gives eta(i). The classical AGM-K identity
AGM(1, sqrt(2)) = sqrt(2) pi^{3/2}/Gamma(1/4)^2 then gives the result.

Corollary 3.5. I(2) = 1 - 1/(2 sqrt(2) eta(i)^2).

Remark. Two independent derivations (analytic Beta, substrate
Chowla-Selberg) agree at 100-digit precision. The transcendental
content of I(2) is carried entirely by eta(i).


================================================================================
4. CROSSOVER-ZERO MOMENTS
================================================================================

Theorem 4.1 (Crossover moments).

  <(lambda - 4)^2>_BZ = 4,
  <lambda (8 - lambda)>_BZ = 12,
  <(lambda - 4)^2 lambda (8 - lambda)>_BZ = 28.

Corollary 4.2 (Mersenne pattern). Moments = d(2^n - 1) for n = 1, 2, 3.

Corollary 4.3 (Mirror ratio). <(lambda-4)^2>/<lambda(8-lambda)> = 1/3 = 1/(d-1).

5. THE BRIDGE IDENTITY
================================================================================

Definition 5.1 (Mirror-breaking residual).

  delta_edge = sqrt(2)/gamma - 2 = 0.0121430664919214255...

This is the same residual that appears in HCSM-40 as the class-A DME
edge deviation and drives the top-Yukawa coefficient
4/3 = |O2|/(d-1).

Theorem 5.2 (Quartic-vacuum bridge).

  lambda_tree = (gamma^2/d) (1 - delta_edge/[2(d-1)]),
  gamma       = sqrt(d lambda_tree) (1 + delta_edge/[4(d-1)]).

Numerical verification. With lambda_tree = ln(kL)/[(d-1) pi^2] =
0.1232458827614323... and gamma = 0.70283946799...

  (gamma^2/d)(1 - delta_edge/6) = 0.1232458930964122...

Relative residual versus lambda_tree: 8.4e-8.
Reciprocal expression matches gamma at 1.6e-6.

Both residuals inside the M_P-limited precision budget
(~3e-7 in lambda_tree).

Remark 5.3. The denominator 2(d-1) = 6 is the non-fixed edge count per
mode. The same factor appears in the top-Yukawa coefficient
through |O2|/(d-1).

6. THE DISCRETE ANALOGUE: NEGATIVE RESULT
================================================================================

Theorem 6.1 (Negative result). The discrete analogue of I(2) is not a
Brillouin-zone moment of a crossover-vanishing integrand. The S6
spectral sum S6(infinity) = 0.40083787... is 0.023% below
I(2) = 0.40092988...; the residual is not a framework constant.

Interpretation. The crossover moments are the framework's discrete
rational skeleton (4, 12, 28, ratio 1/3); I(2) is transcendental (AGM-
based). These two structures are separate. The substrate-level
derivation proceeds through the modular structure of Section 3.

bridge identity to the Higgs sector. The stability coefficient
