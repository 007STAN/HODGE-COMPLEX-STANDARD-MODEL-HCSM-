ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Hodge complex of the periodic 8x8 torus Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 carries three
independent Z_2 structures: the Hodge form-parity grading gamma_5 (even forms
vs. odd forms), the spin-structure parity chi_spin (the NS/R parity of the
four spin structures of the torus), and the orientation Z_2 F constructed in
HCSM-43. We prove that all three operators are involutions, that they
pairwise commute on the 56-dimensional Hodge lift at lambda = 4, and that
together they generate a Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 group. The three generators have the
following physical interpretation:

  - gamma_5 grades the 56-dim Hodge lift into a bosonic sector B of dimension
    28 and a fermionic sector F of dimension 28, and satisfies
    {gamma_5, D} = 0 on the Hodge complex;

  - chi_spin distinguishes the two even spin structures (NS,NS) and (R,R)
    from the two odd spin structures (NS,R) and (R,NS), providing the
    substrate realization of left-right chirality;

  - F is the orientation flip F: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1) of HCSM-43, which
    anticommutes with the winding generator H_wind and realizes the substrate
    CP.

The three Z_2 structures combine to give the physical spin-statistics,
chirality, and CP properties of the Standard Model at the substrate level.
We prove:

    {gamma_5, D} = 0
    [F, gamma_5] = 0
    [F, chi_spin] = 0
    [gamma_5, chi_spin] = 0

and derive the consequent conservation laws on the 56-dim Hodge lift.
Applications include a structural derivation of the Majorana phases (pi, 0)
(HCSM-36), a strengthened form of the two-loop anomaly cancellation
(HCSM-40 Section 6), and a spin-statistics consistency theorem linking the
identification thesis (HCSM-23) to the Hodge grading.

1.1 The spin-statistics and chirality problems
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Standard Model contains three structural facts that are not derived from
first principles:

  (1) Spin-statistics. Half-integer-spin particles obey Fermi-Dirac
      statistics; integer-spin particles obey Bose-Einstein statistics.

  (2) Chirality. Left-handed fermions carry su(2)_L quantum numbers; right-
      handed fermions are su(2)_L singlets.

  (3) CP structure. Charge-parity acts on the Standard Model fields in a
      specific way that is not derived from any deeper principle.

In the Hodge Complex Standard Model, the substrate is the periodic 8x8 torus
Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 [HCSM-00, HCSM-01], carrying the Hodge complex
Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2 and the Hodge-Dirac operator D = d + d* with
D^2 = Delta exactly on each form degree [HCSM-02]. The framework derives the
Standard Model from the substrate but had not, until now, systematically
addressed the three structural facts above.

This paper supplies the missing structure. We show that the Hodge complex
carries three independent Z_2 structures, and that these three structures
combine to give the substrate realization of spin-statistics, chirality, and
CP.

1.2 The three Z_2 structures
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) Hodge form-parity gamma_5. The Hodge complex has a natural Z_2 grading by
    form degree: even forms (Omega^0 + Omega^2) versus odd forms Omega^1. The
    corresponding parity operator is gamma_5 = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^1}
    + P_{Omega^2}. Its anticommutation with D was proved in HCSM-40 Section 6
    as {gamma_5, D} = 0.

(2) Spin-structure parity chi_spin. The torus T^2 admits four spin structures
    classified by (Z_2)^2 boundary conditions (HCSM-36): (NS,NS), (NS,R),
    (R,NS), (R,R). The Dirac index takes values +/-1/4. The parity operator
    chi_spin has eigenvalue +1 on the two even spin structures (NS,NS) and
    (R,R), and eigenvalue -1 on the two odd structures (NS,R) and (R,NS).

(3) Orientation Z_2 F. The diagonal flip F: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1) is the
    orientation Z_2 constructed in HCSM-43. It preserves each D_4 orbit
    O_0, O_1, O_2, commutes with Delta, and anticommutes with the winding
    generator: {F, H_wind} = 0.

1.3 Summary of results
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) (Theorem 3.1). The Hodge form-parity gamma_5 satisfies gamma_5^2 = I and
    {gamma_5, D} = 0 on the 256-dim Hodge complex.

(2) (Theorem 4.1). The 56-dim Hodge lift at lambda = 4 splits as B + F with
    dim B = dim F = 28, where B = (Omega^0 + Omega^2)|lambda=4 is bosonic
    and F = Omega^1|lambda=4 is fermionic.

(3) (Theorem 4.2). The bosonic and fermionic sectors decompose under D_4 as

(4) (Theorem 5.2). The four spin structures of the torus carry a Z_2 x Z_2
    grading. The spin-structure parity chi_spin is well-defined and squares
    to I.

(5) (Theorem 6.4). All three operators commute pairwise:
    [gamma_5, chi_spin] = 0
    [gamma_5, F] = 0
    [chi_spin, F] = 0
    Together they generate Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 on the 56-dim Hodge lift.

(6) (Theorem 6.2). The spin structures (NS,R) and (R,NS) are exchanged by F,

(7) (Theorem 8.1). The 14 Standard Model particles identified with the 14
    modes of Omega^0|lambda=4 inherit their spin-statistics, chirality, and
    CP properties from their images under the Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 group.

3. THE HODGE FORM-PARITY GRADING
================================================================================

3.1 Definition and basic properties
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Definition 3.1 (Hodge form-parity). The Hodge form-parity operator is

    gamma_5 = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^2},

where P_{Omega^k} is the orthogonal projector onto Omega^k.

The operator gamma_5 acts as +1 on even forms (Omega^0 + Omega^2) and as -1
on odd forms Omega^1. It is the Hodge complex analog of the chirality operator
in the Dirac algebra, and was first introduced in HCSM-40 Section 6 in that
role.

Theorem 3.1 (Hodge form-parity). The Hodge form-parity gamma_5 satisfies:

    (i) gamma_5^2 = I on the full 256-dim Hodge complex;
    (ii) gamma_5^dagger = gamma_5;
    (iii) {gamma_5, D} = 0 exactly on Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2.

Proof. (i) and (ii) follow from the fact that the three form-degree
projectors P_{Omega^k} are orthogonal, idempotent, and sum to the identity.
For (iii), note that D = d + d* is form-degree-odd: d raises form degree by
one and d* lowers it by one. Hence D maps Omega^0 -> Omega^1,
Omega^1 -> Omega^0 + Omega^2, and Omega^2 -> Omega^1. Both Omega^0 and
Omega^2 are gamma_5-even, and Omega^1 is gamma_5-odd. Therefore D flips the
gamma_5 eigenvalue, which is exactly {gamma_5, D} = 0. [Box]

Corollary 3.2 (Regulator trace). Tr(gamma_5 f(D)) = 0 for every even
function f.

Proof. Since gamma_5 D gamma_5^{-1} = -D and gamma_5^2 = I, we have
gamma_5 f(D) gamma_5^{-1} = f(-D) for every even f. The trace is cyclic, so
Tr(gamma_5 f(D)) = Tr(f(-D) gamma_5) = -Tr(f(D) gamma_5) when f is even.
Hence Tr(gamma_5 f(D)) = 0. The argument uses only {gamma_5, D} = 0 and f
even; no Adler-Bardeen input is required. [Box]

The corollary is the substrate-level form of the two-loop anomaly
cancellation used in HCSM-40 Section 6.

5. THE SPIN-STRUCTURE PARITY
================================================================================

5.1 The four spin structures of the torus
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The two-torus T^2 admits four spin structures classified by (Z_2)^2 boundary
conditions, labeled by nu = (nu_1, nu_2) with nu_i in {NS, R} [HCSM-36]. The
Dirac index of each spin structure is

    index(nu_1, nu_2) = (2 nu_1 - 1)(2 nu_2 - 1) / 4
                      in {+1/4, -1/4, -1/4, +1/4},                  (4)

where NS = 0, R = 1. Explicitly:

    Spin structure      (nu_1, nu_2)    Index
    -------------------------------------------------
    (NS, NS)            (0, 0)          +1/4
    (NS, R)             (0, 1)          -1/4
    (R, NS)             (1, 0)          -1/4
    (R, R)              (1, 1)          +1/4

5.2 The spin-structure parity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Definition 5.1 (Spin-structure parity). The spin-structure parity operator is

    chi_spin = sign(4 * index(nu)),

with eigenvalue +1 on the two even spin structures (NS,NS) and (R,R), and
eigenvalue -1 on the two odd spin structures (NS,R) and (R,NS).

Theorem 5.2 (Spin-structure parity). The operator chi_spin is a Z_2 grading
on the space of spin structures:

    (i) chi_spin^2 = I;
    (ii) chi_spin commutes with the four-dimensional Dirac operator on each
        spin structure;
    (iii) The two eigenvalues +/-1 each have multiplicity 2.

Proof. (i) is immediate from Definition 5.1. (ii) is a standard property of
spin structures: the Dirac operator acts on sections of the spinor bundle
for a given spin structure, and the parity of the index is preserved by the
Dirac operator. (iii) follows from the table above: two spin structures have
index +1/4, two have index -1/4. [Box]

Remark 5.3 (Physical interpretation). The spin-structure parity chi_spin is
the substrate realization of left-right chirality. The two even spin
structures give the two chiralities of a Dirac fermion in the physical
four-dimensional sense; the two odd spin structures give the conjugate
chiralities. The identification of chi_spin with physical chirality is a
disclosed physical input, parallel to the identification F <-> CP of HCSM-43.

6. THE ORIENTATION Z_2 AND COMPATIBILITY
================================================================================

6.1 Recollection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

By HCSM-43, the diagonal flip F: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1) satisfies F^2 = I,
preserves each D_4 orbit individually, and [F, Delta] = 0. It acts on the
winding generator via {F, H_wind} = 0.

The action of F extends to the full Hodge complex by pullback. On Omega^0, F*
acts by permuting the sites: (F* f)(x, y) = f(y, x). On Omega^1, F* swaps the
two components of a 1-form: F*(omega_1 dx + omega_2 dy) = (F* omega_1) dy
+ (F* omega_2) dx. On Omega^2, F* acts with a sign flip from the orientation
of the wedge product: F*(f dx ^ dy) = -(F* f) dx ^ dy.

6.2 Compatibility with the Hodge form-parity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lemma 6.1. The pullback F* preserves the form degree: [F*, P_{Omega^k}] = 0
for each k = 0, 1, 2.

Proof. The pullback of a k-form under a diffeomorphism is a k-form. Hence F*
commutes with the form-degree projectors. [Box]

Theorem 6.1 (Compatibility [F, gamma_5] = 0). [F, gamma_5] = 0 on the
256-dim Hodge complex.

Proof. Immediate from Lemma 6.1 and Definition 3.1: gamma_5 is a linear
combination of the form-degree projectors, all of which commute with F*. [Box]

Remark 6.2 (Interpretation). F is a form-degree-preserving operation. It
preserves the bosonic sector B and the fermionic sector F of Theorem 4.1
individually. The orientation flip F does not mix the two sectors.

6.3 Action on spin structures
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 6.2 (Spin-structure action of F). The diagonal flip F acts on the
four spin structures of the torus by exchanging the two torus directions:

    F: (nu_1, nu_2) -> (nu_2, nu_1).

Consequently:

    (i) F fixes (NS,NS) and (R,R);
    (ii) F exchanges (NS,R) <-> (R,NS).

Proof. The diagonal flip swaps the two cycle directions x <-> y of the
torus. Spin structures are classified by the boundary conditions along each
cycle; the swap of directions exchanges nu_1 <-> nu_2. Direct check of the
four cases gives (i) and (ii). [Box]

Corollary 6.3 (Compatibility [F, chi_spin] = 0). [F, chi_spin] = 0.

Proof. By Theorem 6.2, F either fixes or exchanges the spin structures.
Under exchange, both spin structures have the same chi_spin eigenvalue (+1
for the even pair, -1 for the odd pair). Under fixing, the eigenvalue is
trivially preserved. Hence chi_spin commutes with F. [Box]

    Sector     gamma_5    chi_spin    F

8.1 Spin-statistics from the Hodge form-parity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge form-parity gamma_5 grades the 56-dim Hodge lift into the bosonic
sector B and the fermionic sector F. This is the substrate realization of
the spin-statistics theorem: forms of even degree behave as bosons; forms of
odd degree behave as fermions.

In the four-dimensional effective theory, the 14 modes at lambda = 4 in
Omega^0 are identified with one Standard Model generation [HCSM-23]. The
form-parity of each mode is determined by which sector of the 56-dim Hodge
lift contains that mode's primary image. The visible generation in
Omega^0|lambda=4 sits in the bosonic sector B; the identification thesis
assigns the physical spin-statistics (12 fermions, 2 bosons) to these modes.

8.2 Chirality from the spin-structure parity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The spin-structure parity chi_spin distinguishes the two even spin structures
(NS,NS) and (R,R) from the two odd spin structures (NS,R) and (R,NS). In the
HCSM identification, the even structures correspond to left-handed
chiralities and the odd structures to right-handed chiralities (or vice
versa; the assignment is a disclosed physical input).

This identification is the substrate realization of left-right chirality. In
the Standard Model, left-handed fermions carry su(2)_L quantum numbers; the
B_1 isotypic in Omega^0|lambda=4 has multiplicity 2 (from 2 B_1), consistent
with the two chiralities of a Dirac fermion.

8.3 CP from the orientation Z_2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The orientation flip F is the substrate realization of CP, as established in
HCSM-43. It anticommutes with the winding generator H_wind and forces
theta_QCD = 0 and theta_eff = 0 exactly.

8.4 The combined structure
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The three Z_2 structures together give the physical spin-statistics,
chirality, and CP properties of the Standard Model at the substrate level.
The physical content of a mode m in Omega^0|lambda=4 is determined by its
signature under (gamma_5, chi_spin, F).

Theorem 8.1 (Spin-statistics consistency). The 14 Standard Model particles
identified with the 14 modes of Omega^0|lambda=4 inherit their
spin-statistics, chirality, and CP properties from their images under the
Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 group:

  - The Hodge form-parity gamma_5 assigns the form-parity of the mode's
    image in the 56-dim Hodge lift;
  - The spin-structure parity chi_spin assigns the left-right chirality of
    the mode;
  - The orientation flip F assigns the CP eigenvalue of the mode.

Proof. Combination of Theorems 4.1, 5.2, and 7.1. The identification of the
14 modes with the 14 Standard Model particles is the identification thesis
[HCSM-23]; the assignment of physical properties to each mode's signature is
a disclosed input, but the assignment itself is consistent because the three
operators act on independent structural features of the Hodge complex. [Box]

9.1 Two-loop anomaly cancellation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Corollary 3.2 gives Tr(gamma_5 f(D)) = 0 for every even regulator f. This is
the substrate-level proof of two-loop anomaly cancellation used in HCSM-40
Section 6. The argument requires only {gamma_5, D} = 0; no Adler-Bardeen

9.2 Majorana phases
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Majorana phases (alpha_1, alpha_2) = (pi, 0) derived in HCSM-36 and
HCSM-00 Section 7.2 from the zero-state sign structure are consistent with
the spin-chirality structure of this paper. The two degenerate neutrino modes
with N/4 = 60 have chi_spin = +1; the third mode with N/4 = 58 has
chi_spin = -1. The phase difference between the two chiralities contributes
pi to the Majorana phases.

9.3 Strong CP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The orientation Z_2 F anticommutes with H_wind (HCSM-43). Combined with the
form-parity gamma_5 (which commutes with F), this gives a clean structural
separation of the CP-odd and CP-even sectors of the substrate:

    {F, H_wind} = 0, [F, gamma_5] = 0.

The first relation forces theta_QCD = 0; the second ensures that CP is a
symmetry of the spin-statistics structure.

9.4 Sector-coarse correspondence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The D_4 decompositions of Theorem 4.2 directly feed the sector-coarse
correspondence of HCSM-06: the A_1 isotypic in B + F maps to color triplets;
the B_1 isotypic maps to weak doublets. The bosonic and fermionic sectors
carry different multiplicities of these isotypies, which is why the visible
generation sits in Omega^0|lambda=4 while the hidden sector 5+6+31
[HCSM-38, HCSM-39] is distributed across Omega^1|lambda=4 + Omega^2|lambda=4.

11. FALSIFIABLE PREDICTIONS
================================================================================

(1) The Hodge form-parity anticommutes with D. Any computational test that
    finds {gamma_5, D} != 0 on the Hodge complex falsifies Theorem 3.1. The
    companion verification script checks this at machine precision.

(2) Two-loop anomaly cancellation is exact. The regulator trace
    Tr(gamma_5 f(D)) = 0 for any even f. A confirmed lattice anomaly at any
    scale falsifies this.

(3) The D_4 decompositions of B and F are as stated. Any representation-
    theoretic test finding different multiplicities falsifies Theorem 4.2.

(4) Majorana phases (pi, 0). Consistency of this paper with HCSM-36 requires
    the Majorana sector to remain CP-conserving. A confirmed Majorana phase
    outside {0, pi} would falsify the identification of chi_spin with
    chirality, or the assignment of N/4 labels to chi_spin eigenstates.

(5) Strong CP: theta_eff = 0 exactly. Any confirmed nonzero theta_eff
    falsifies HCSM-43, and by extension the CP structure of this paper.

(6) Chirality-chi_spin identification. Any measurement of a right-handed
    neutrino (if (NS,NS) is identified with left chirality) or other direct
    test of the chirality assignment would test the disclosed input.

12. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

We have derived the spin-chirality Z_2 grading of the Hodge Complex Standard
Model. The 56-dim Hodge lift at lambda = 4 carries three independent Z_2
structures:

(1) the Hodge form-parity gamma_5 = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^2},
    which anticommutes with D and grades the lift into the bosonic sector B
    (28) and the fermionic sector F (28);

(2) the spin-structure parity chi_spin, which distinguishes even from odd
    spin structures on the torus;

(3) the orientation Z_2 F constructed in HCSM-43.

All three operators pairwise commute, are involutions, and together generate
Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 on the 56-dim Hodge lift. They give the substrate realization
of spin-statistics (from gamma_5), left-right chirality (from chi_spin), and
CP (from F).

The three structures combine to give a clean structural explanation of the
three open problems of the Standard Model's flavor and gauge sectors:
spin-statistics, chirality, and CP violation. The Majorana phases (pi, 0) of
HCSM-36 and the strong-CP result theta_eff = 0 of HCSM-43 are both
consequences of this Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2 structure.

The framework's strength is that it has fewer empirical anchors than the
Standard Model, not that it has none.

Physics is what the machine cannot cancel. The Hodge complex is the machine.
