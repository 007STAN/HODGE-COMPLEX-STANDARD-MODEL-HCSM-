6.1 The chirality grading
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Definition 6.1 (Chirality grading). The chirality grading on the Hodge
complex is

    gamma_5 = (-1)^F = P_Omega^0 - P_Omega^1 + P_Omega^2.

It assigns eigenvalue +1 to even forms (Omega^0 + Omega^2) and -1 to odd

exactly.

Proof. The Hodge-Dirac operator is odd with respect to the Z2 grading: it
maps even forms to odd forms and odd forms to even forms (HCSM-02).
Therefore gamma_5 D = - D gamma_5, which is equivalent to the
anticommutation relation. Direct numerical verification gives
|| gamma_5 D + D gamma_5 ||_F = 0 exactly in IEEE-754 double precision.
                                                          [Box]

Corollary 6.3 (Regulator trace vanishes). For any even function f with
f(lambda) = f(-lambda),

    Tr(gamma_5 f(D)) = 0.

Proof. The spectrum of D is +/-symmetric within each sector (HCSM-02,
Theorem 6.1). The eigenvalues pair as lambda <-> -lambda with opposite
chirality. Therefore the trace of gamma_5 f(D) vanishes identically for
every even f. Numerical verification across nine regulators spanning
compact support, exponential decay, power decay, and oscillatory forms
gives residuals at the level of machine epsilon (10^-15).  [Box]

Corollary 6.4 (Index of D). Index(D) = Tr(gamma_5 P_ker D) = 0.

Proof. The Betti numbers of the torus are (b0, b1, b2) = (1, 2, 1)
(HCSM-02). The index of D is b0 - b1 + b2 = 0 (the Euler characteristic
of T^2). Direct numerical verification gives
Tr(gamma_5 P_ker D) = 0 at machine precision.             [Box]

6.2 The two-loop anomaly
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 6.5 (Two-loop anomaly cancellation). The two-loop anomaly on the
Hodge complex vanishes exactly.

Proof. The two-loop anomaly coefficient on a lattice factorizes as

    A^(2) = Tr_Hodge(gamma_5 R(D)) * Tr_content(Y^3),

where R(D) is the regulator. Each factor vanishes independently:

  Factor 1 (geometry). By Corollary 6.3, Tr(gamma_5 R(D)) = 0 for every
  even regulator R. This is exact and does not depend on the fermion
  content.

  Factor 2 (content). The Standard Model per-generation content has

      Tr(Y)          = 6 (1/6) + 3 (-2/3) + 3 (1/3) + 2 (-1/2) + 1 (1)
                       + 1 (0) = 0,
      Tr(Y^3)        = 0,

      SU(3)^2 U(1)   = 0,

  verified in exact rational arithmetic.

Both factors vanish. Their product is zero. The two-loop anomaly cancels
by two independent mechanisms.                            [Box]

Remark 6.6 (Comparison with Adler-Bardeen). The Adler-Bardeen theorem
states that the ABJ anomaly is one-loop exact: if the one-loop anomaly

preservation theorem for the DNLS Hartree Hamiltonian; and the two-loop
anomaly closure on the Hodge complex. The remaining items are named
explicitly and left for future work.

The framework's strength is that it has fewer empirical anchors than the
Standard Model, not that it has none.

Physics is what the machine cannot cancel. The Hodge complex is the machine.
