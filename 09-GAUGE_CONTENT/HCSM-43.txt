The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) derives the Standard Model from the
periodic 8x8 torus Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8. This paper closes the strong-CP
blocker of the framework. We construct the orientation Z_2 of the substrate
explicitly: it is the diagonal flip F: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1), already
defined in HCSM-00 Definition 5.3. On the 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4, F
acts as an involution that preserves each D_4 orbit O_0, O_1, O_2 individually
and commutes with the discrete 5-point Laplacian.

The central theorem of this paper is the exact anticommutation

    {F, H_wind} = 0,

where H_wind = i sin(pi M / 8) is the winding generator of HCSM-00 Section
5.1. The anticommutation is not approximate: it follows analytically from the
antisymmetry of M_{ij} = n_1^{(i)} n_2^{(j)} - n_1^{(j)} n_2^{(i)} under the
exchange of the two torus directions, and is verified at machine precision
(max |{F, H_wind}| = 0.0). Because F is an exact automorphism of the substrate
Hamiltonian and the substrate vacuum lies in an F-eigensector, every F-odd
observable has vanishing vacuum expectation value. The winding generator
H_wind is F-odd; identifying it with the substrate realization of the
topological density F_{mu nu} F~^{mu nu}, the HCSM vacuum forces

    theta_QCD = 0   exactly.

The HCSM mass map produces positive diagonal masses, so
arg det(M_u M_d) = 0 and therefore

    theta_eff = theta_QCD + arg det(M_u M_d) = 0.

No axion, no Peccei-Quinn symmetry, no Nelson-Barr mechanism, and no new
degrees of freedom are required. The strong CP problem is solved by the
orientation Z_2 of the substrate.

1.1 The strong CP problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Quantum chromodynamics admits a topological term

    L_theta = theta_QCD * (g_3^2 / 32 pi^2) * F^a_{mu nu} F~^{a mu nu},

where F~^{a mu nu} = (1/2) eps^{mu nu rho sigma} F^a_{rho sigma}. The effective
physical parameter is

    theta_eff = theta_QCD + arg det(M_u M_d),

where M_u, M_d are the up- and down-quark mass matrices. The parameter
theta_eff is CP-odd and is bounded by the neutron electric dipole moment
[PDG]:

    |theta_eff| <~ 10^{-10}.

The Standard Model offers no explanation for why theta_eff is so small:
theta_QCD and arg det(M_u M_d) are independent inputs, and their sum is not
protected by any symmetry. This is the strong CP problem.

The standard solutions require either a new light pseudoscalar (the axion
[Peccei-Quinn 1977, Weinberg 1978, Wilczek 1978]), a parity-based mechanism
with new heavy fermions (Nelson-Barr [Nelson 1984, Barr 1984]), or a
spontaneously broken left-right symmetry. All of these extend the Standard
Model with new degrees of freedom.

1.2 The HCSM setting
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge Complex Standard Model derives the Standard Model from a single
discrete substrate, the periodic 8x8 torus Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8, carrying the
Hodge complex Omega^0 + Omega^1 + Omega^2 and the Hodge-Dirac operator
D = d + d* with D^2 = Delta exactly on each form degree [HCSM-00, HCSM-02].
The 14 modes at the self-paired eigenvalue lambda = 4 are identified with one
Standard Model generation [HCSM-04, HCSM-23]. The processor T = I - P_C
[HCSM-13] acts on the 14-mode multiplet; its winding generator is

    H_wind = i sin(pi M / 8),   M_{ij} = n_1^{(i)} n_2^{(j)} - n_1^{(j)} n_2^{(i)}
                                                                        (1)

with spectrum {-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^{(10)}, +2 sqrt(2), +4 sqrt(2)}
[HCSM-14]. The matrix H_wind is Hermitian with entries in Q(sqrt(2)).

The framework already contains a natural candidate for a discrete orientation
symmetry: the diagonal flip

    F: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1),

introduced in HCSM-00 Definition 5.3 [HCSM-00] and used in HCSM-40 [HCSM-40]
to characterize the self-mirror orbit O_2. The zero state s is stated to be
F-odd [HCSM-22].

What has not been established is the anticommutation of F with H_wind. That
is the missing ingredient. In this paper we prove it, and we show that it
forces theta_eff = 0 exactly.

(1) (Theorem 3.1). F is an involution on the 14-mode multiplet, preserves

3.1 Action on the 14-mode multiplet
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 3.1 (Orientation Z_2). The diagonal flip F acts on the 14-mode
multiplet at lambda = 4 and satisfies:

    (i)   F^2 = I on the multiplet;
    (ii)  F preserves each D_4 orbit O_0, O_1, O_2 individually;
    (iii) [F, Delta] = 0.

Proof. (i) and (ii) are direct. For (i), F(F(m)) = m for every mode label m,
hence F^2 = I.

For (ii), one checks each orbit:

    F(O_0) = {(4,0), (0,4)} = O_0,
    F(O_1) = {(3,1), (5,1), (1,3), (7,3), (1,5), (7,5), (3,7), (5,7)} = O_1,
    F(O_2) = {(2,2), (6,2), (2,6), (6,6)} = O_2.

So each orbit is preserved setwise.

For (iii), write the Laplacian eigenvalue as lambda(n_1, n_2)
= 4 - 2 cos(pi n_1 / 4) - 2 cos(pi n_2 / 4). This is manifestly symmetric
in n_1 <-> n_2, so lambda(F(m)) = lambda(m) for every mode. Since Delta acts
diagonally on the plane-wave basis with eigenvalue lambda(m), we have
[Delta, F] = 0 on the multiplet. [Box]

Remark 3.2. The orbitwise preservation of Theorem 3.1(ii) is why F is a
substrate Z_2 rather than a large reorganisation: it acts within each D_4
orbit, not across orbits. This is what permits it to be simultaneously an
orientation flip and a symmetry of the D_4 orbit structure.

3.2 Fixed points
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The fixed-point set of F on the 14-mode multiplet is

    Fix(F) = {(2,2), (6,6)}.

These are exactly the two Z_2 x Z_2 fixed points of [HCSM-40, Theorem 7.1],
identified there with the W boson (2,2) and the top quark (6,6). The
fixed-point structure is thus carried over from the mirror orbit
O_2 = Fix(M) established in HCSM-40; the diagonal flip does not introduce
new fixed points.

3.3 The orbitwise sign structure
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The numerical signed-permutation search on the 14-mode multiplet (Section A
of the verification script) confirms two other commuting signed D_4 operators
with nontrivial sign patterns on (O_0, O_1, O_2), in addition to F:

  - the identity permutation with signs (-1, -1, -1) (equivalent to -I on the
    multiplet, trivially commuting with H_wind);
  - the 180-degree rotation R_180: (n_1, n_2) -> (-n_1, -n_2) with signs
    (+1, -1, -1).

Both commute with H_wind; the second will be exploited in HCSM-58 (joint
independence of the geometric kernel). The present paper uses only F, which
is the unique label-preserving Z_2 that anticommutes with H_wind.

4. THE EXACT ANTICOMMUTATION {F, H_wind} = 0
================================================================================

Theorem 4.1 (Anticommutation). On the 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4,

    {F, H_wind} = 0   exactly,

and

    [F, H_wind] != 0.

Proof. Let m_i = (n_1^{(i)}, n_2^{(i)}) in Z_8 x Z_8 for i = 1, ..., 14 be
the 14 modes, and let p_F be the permutation of indices induced by F, i.e.
m_{p_F(i)} = F(m_i) = (n_2^{(i)}, n_1^{(i)}).

The winding matrix is

    M_{ij} = n_1^{(i)} n_2^{(j)} - n_1^{(j)} n_2^{(i)}.

Under F, both momenta are exchanged, so

    M_{p_F(i) p_F(j)} = n_2^{(i)} n_1^{(j)} - n_2^{(j)} n_1^{(i)}
                      = -(n_1^{(i)} n_2^{(j)} - n_1^{(j)} n_2^{(i)})
                      = -M_{ij}.

Thus, as a matrix identity on the multiplet,

    P_F M P_F^{-1} = -M,

where P_F denotes the permutation matrix implementing F. Applying the odd
function x -> i sin(pi x / 8) elementwise,

    P_F H_wind P_F^{-1} = P_F i sin(pi M / 8) P_F^{-1}
                        = i sin(-pi M / 8)
                        = -i sin(pi M / 8)
                        = -H_wind.

Equivalently, P_F H_wind + H_wind P_F = 0, which is {F, H_wind} = 0.

The commutator [F, H_wind] = P_F H_wind - H_wind P_F = 2 P_F H_wind != 0,
since H_wind != 0 and P_F is invertible. [Box]

Remark 4.2 (Analytic, not numerical). The proof uses only the antisymmetry
of M under exchange of the two torus directions. It does not rely on any
numerical diagonalization. The companion verification script confirms
max |{F, H_wind}| = 0.0 and max |[F, H_wind]| = 2.0 in IEEE-754 double
precision, exactly consistent with the analytic proof.

Remark 4.3 (The winding spectrum is F-paired). Since H_wind anticommutes
with the invertible involution F, its spectrum is symmetric about zero:
every nonzero eigenvalue lambda pairs with -lambda via the F-conjugate
eigenvector. This is a structural necessity given the spectrum
{-4 sqrt(2), -2 sqrt(2), 0^{(10)}, +2 sqrt(2), +4 sqrt(2)} of HCSM-14. The
10-dimensional kernel is F-invariant.

5.1 Physical identification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We identify the substrate operator H_wind with the realization of the
topological density F_{mu nu} F~^{mu nu} in the HCSM effective description,
and the diagonal flip F with the substrate realization of CP. This
identification is a disclosed physical choice of the same character as
A_1 <-> color and B_1 <-> weak isospin: the substrate realizes a discrete
symmetry, and its interpretation in Standard Model language is a physical
identification, not a derivation.

The justification for this identification is threefold.

(1) Parity and CP action. On the torus, the two fundamental cycles are
    exchanged by F. This is precisely what CP does to the two chiralities of
    a fermion. The discrete statement "exchange the two cycles" is the
    substrate counterpart of "exchange left and right."

(2) The winding generator is the topological density. On a two-torus, the
    topological density F_{mu nu} F~^{mu nu} reduces to the winding number
    density. The processor's winding generator H_wind is the discrete
    realization of that density on the 14-mode multiplet.

(3) Analogy with HCSM-40. In HCSM-40 [HCSM-40], the same map F is used to
    characterize the mirror-fixed orbit O_2. Its role there is structural
    (identifying fixed points); its role here is dynamical (forcing an
    operator to vanish on the vacuum). Both uses are consistent with the
    same disclosure.

5.2 The vacuum is in an F-eigensector
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lemma 5.1. The substrate vacuum |0> lies in an eigensector of F with
eigenvalue eta in {+1, -1}.

Proof. By Theorem 3.1, F is an exact automorphism of the substrate
Hamiltonian; in particular [F, Delta] = 0. The vacuum is the ground state
of the substrate's entropy functional, which is a functional of Delta alone.
Any state that is an eigenstate of Delta can be chosen as an eigenstate of F,
because F and Delta commute. The vacuum is therefore either F-even
(eta = +1) or F-odd (eta = -1). [Box]

Remark 5.2. HCSM-22 [HCSM-22] already states that the zero state s is
F-odd; this is the substrate-level statement that the vacuum lies in the
eta = -1 eigensector. The choice of sign does not affect the corollaries
below, which depend only on F being an eigensymmetry of the vacuum.

5.3 theta_QCD = 0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 5.1 (Strong CP). Every F-odd observable has vanishing vacuum
expectation value. In particular,

    <0| H_wind |0> = 0   exactly,

and therefore

    theta_QCD = 0        exactly.

Proof. Let |0> satisfy F |0> = eta |0> with eta = +-1 (Lemma 5.1). Let O be
any operator with F O F^{-1} = -O (an F-odd operator). Then

    <0| O |0> = <0| F^{-1} (F O F^{-1}) F |0>
              = eta^2 <0| (-O) |0>
              = -<0| O |0>,

so <0| O |0> = 0.

By Theorem 4.1, H_wind is F-odd. Therefore <0| H_wind |0> = 0.

Under the physical identification of Section 5.1, H_wind is the substrate
realization of F_{mu nu} F~^{mu nu} and the corresponding effective coupling
is precisely theta_QCD. The vanishing of <H_wind> is therefore the statement
that theta_QCD = 0 exactly. [Box]

Remark 5.3 (No axion; no Peccei-Quinn). The mechanism is not spontaneous
breaking of a Peccei-Quinn symmetry, and does not introduce a light
pseudoscalar. It is a discrete symmetry of the substrate that fixes
theta_QCD at zero. The standard axion mass formula m_a ~ sqrt(chi) does not
apply; there is no axion in the HCSM spectrum.

Remark 5.4 (No Nelson-Barr). The mechanism does not introduce new heavy
fermions, and does not invoke a spontaneous CP-violating phase that is later
cancelled. It uses only the discrete substrate symmetry F.

6.1 The mass-matrix argument
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The effective parameter is

    theta_eff = theta_QCD + arg det(M_u M_d).

By Theorem 5.1, theta_QCD = 0. It remains to show that
arg det(M_u M_d) = 0 in the HCSM framework.

Lemma 6.1. The HCSM quark mass matrices are positive diagonal in the mass-
eigenstate basis, so arg det(M_u M_d) = 0 exactly.

Proof. The HCSM mass map [HCSM-33] assigns masses via

    m_i = A_mass exp(-B_mass (N/4)_i),

with A_mass > 0 and B_mass > 0 real. Every mass eigenvalue is therefore
strictly positive. The mass matrices M_u, M_d, after diagonalization by the
standard CKM procedure, are real positive diagonal. Hence det M_u > 0 and
det M_d > 0, so arg det(M_u M_d) = 0. [Box]

6.2 Corollary: theta_eff = 0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Corollary 6.1 (Vanishing theta_eff).

    theta_eff = theta_QCD + arg det(M_u M_d) = 0.

Proof. Immediate from Theorem 5.1 and Lemma 6.1. [Box]

Remark 6.2. The argument is thus genuinely structural. It does not require
fine-tuning between theta_QCD and arg det(M_u M_d); both terms vanish
separately. The neutron EDM bound |theta_eff| <~ 10^{-10} is satisfied
trivially by theta_eff = 0 exactly.

6.3 Consistency with the observed CP violation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The HCSM framework produces CP violation in the quark and lepton sectors via
the CKM phase [HCSM-34] and the Dirac PMNS phase [HCSM-36]. The result
theta_eff = 0 is consistent with these: the CKM phase is a flavour phase,
not a vacuum phase. The substrate Z_2 forces the vacuum theta-angle to
vanish while leaving the flavour structure untouched.

(1) theta_QCD = 0 exactly. Any future measurement of a nonzero theta_QCD at

The orientation Z_2 of the substrate is the diagonal flip
F: (n_1, n_2) -> (n_2, n_1) already defined in HCSM-00 Definition 5.3. On
the 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4, F preserves each D_4 orbit individually
and commutes with the discrete Laplacian. Its central structural property is
the exact anticommutation

    {F, H_wind} = 0,

with the winding generator H_wind = i sin(pi M / 8). This forces every F-odd
observable to have vanishing vacuum expectation value. Since H_wind is
F-odd and is identified with the substrate realization of
F_{mu nu} F~^{mu nu}, the HCSM vacuum forces theta_QCD = 0 exactly. The HCSM
mass map produces positive masses, so arg det(M_u M_d) = 0 as well.
Therefore

    theta_eff = theta_QCD + arg det(M_u M_d) = 0   exactly.

No axion, no Peccei-Quinn symmetry, no Nelson-Barr sector, and no new degrees
of freedom are introduced. The strong CP problem is solved by the substrate
Z_2.

The framework's strength is that it has fewer empirical anchors than the
Standard Model, not that it has none.
