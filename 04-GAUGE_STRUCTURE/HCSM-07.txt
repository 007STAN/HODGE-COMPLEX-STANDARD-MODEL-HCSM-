ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Hodge Complex Standard Model derives the gauge algebra
su(3) + su(2) + u(1)^5 from the D4 endomorphism algebra of the 14-mode
multiplet at lambda = 4, and identifies the 15-dimensional space of
D4-invariant, sector-block-diagonal, multiplicity-scalar operators as the
natural home of the physical hypercharge functional (HCSM-06). This paper
derives the specific hypercharge coefficients. On the A_1 isotypic, the
hypercharge functional is Y_{A_1} = alpha_{A_1} I + beta_{A_1} F +
gamma_{A_1} O, and matching the physical hypercharge values
Y(u_R^c) = -2/3 on Omega^0, Y(Q_L) = +1/6 on Omega^1, Y(d_R^c) = +1/3 on
Omega^2 fixes the coefficients uniquely to
(alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1}) = (0, -1/6, -1/2). On the B_1
isotypic, matching Y(e_R^c) = +1 on Omega^0, Y(L_L) = -1/2 on Omega^1,
Y(nu_R^c) = 0 on Omega^2 fixes (alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1}) =
(0, +1/2, +1/2). The resulting hypercharge values are all rational with
denominators dividing the framework constants {1, 2, 3, 6}. We prove the
quantization theorem: hypercharge values in the sector-coarse space take
values in (1/6) Z up to an overall normalization; the specific rational
assignment of the Standard Model is the unique one compatible with the
D4 and sector structure. The identification of the three color-triplet
multiplets u_R^c, Q_L, d_R^c with the Hodge sectors Omega^0, Omega^1,
Omega^2 is stated as an input (Input 3); the derivation of the
hypercharge coefficients is conditional on this input. All claims are
HCSM-native and verified at machine precision.

1.1 The hypercharge problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hodge Complex Standard Model (HCSM) derives the gauge algebra

    g_HCSM = su(3) + su(2) + u(1)^5                                     (1)

as a Lie subalgebra of the D4 commutant on the 14-mode multiplet at
lambda = 4 (HCSM-06 [HCSM-06]). On the full 56-dimensional Hodge sector,
the space of D4-invariant, sector-block-diagonal, multiplicity-scalar
operators is 15-dimensional, spanned by the three sector operators
{I, F, O} on each of the five isotypic blocks (HCSM-06 [HCSM-06],
Theorem 6.3).

The present paper asks: what specific element of this 15-dimensional
space is the physical hypercharge functional? The answer is determined by
the Standard Model content and by the sector assignment of the three
color-triplet multiplets.

The derivation is a linear-algebraic one: on the A_1 and B_1 isotypic
blocks, the physical hypercharge assignment gives two 3 x 3 linear
systems whose solutions are the hypercharge coefficients
(alpha, beta, gamma). The resulting values are rational with denominators
dividing the framework constant 6.

1.2 The one-line summary
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    On the 15-dimensional sector-coarse space of the 14-mode multiplet,
    the physical hypercharge functional is uniquely determined by (i) the
    values -2/3, +1/6, +1/3, -1/2, +1, 0 of the Standard Model content
    and (ii) the sector assignment of the three color-triplet multiplets.
    The coefficient A_1 block is (alpha, beta, gamma) = (0, -1/6, -1/2);
    the B_1 block is (0, +1/2, +1/2). All values are rational with
    denominators dividing 6.

1.3 Main results
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(1) Hypercharge space (Theorem 3.1; restated from HCSM-06). The space of
    D4-invariant, sector-block-diagonal, multiplicity-scalar operators on
    the 56-dimensional Hodge sector at lambda = 4 is exactly
    15-dimensional, spanned by {I, F, O} on each of the five D4 isotypic
    blocks.

(2) A_1 coefficient theorem (Theorem 5.1). Matching the physical
    hypercharges Y(u_R^c) = -2/3 (on Omega^0), Y(Q_L) = +1/6 (on Omega^1),
    Y(d_R^c) = +1/3 (on Omega^2), the unique coefficient triple for the
    A_1 block is

        (alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1})
            = (0, -1/6, -1/2).                                       (2)

(3) B_1 coefficient theorem (Theorem 6.1). Matching the physical
    hypercharges Y(e_R^c) = +1 (on Omega^0), Y(L_L) = -1/2 (on Omega^1),
    Y(nu_R^c) = 0 (on Omega^2), the unique coefficient triple for the
    B_1 block is

        (alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1})
            = (0, +1/2, +1/2).                                       (3)

(4) Quantization theorem (Theorem 7.1). All hypercharge values obtained
    in the sector-coarse space lie in (1/6) Z up to an overall
    normalization. The denominators {1, 2, 3, 6} are exactly the first
    four divisors of the framework constant 6 = d + 2 at d = 4.

(5) Vanishing mean (Corollary 7.3). The identity coefficients satisfy
    alpha_{A_1} = alpha_{B_1} = 0: the mean hypercharge across the three
    Hodge sectors vanishes for both the A_1 and B_1 blocks.

2. SETUP

2.1 The substrate and the mode multiplet
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The substrate is the periodic square lattice Lambda = Z_8 x Z_8 with
N = 64 sites [HCSM-01]. The 14 modes at the self-paired eigenvalue
lambda = 4 are [HCSM-04]

    O_0 = {(0,4), (4,0)},                                             (4)

    O_1 = {(1,3), (1,5), (3,1), (3,7),
           (5,1), (5,7), (7,3), (7,5)},                               (5)

    O_2 = {(2,2), (2,6), (6,2), (6,6)}.                              (6)

2.2 The D4 isotypic decomposition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The 14-mode multiplet decomposes under D4 as (HCSM-05 [HCSM-05];
HCSM-06 [HCSM-06])

    V_14 = 3A_1 + A_2 + 2B_1 + 2B_2 + 3E.                            (7)

The full 56-dimensional Hodge sector at lambda = 4 decomposes as

    V_56 = 9A_1 + 5A_2 + 7B_1 + 7B_2 + 14E.                          (8)

2.3 The sector-coarse space
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The 56-dimensional sector V_56 has a natural decomposition into three
Hodge-sector blocks:

    V_56 = Omega^0|_{lambda=4} + Omega^1|_{lambda=4}
           + Omega^2|_{lambda=4},                                     (9)

of dimensions 14 + 28 + 14.

Definition (Sector operators). On V_56, define the three sector operators

    I = P_{Omega^0} + P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^2},                     (10)

    F = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^1} + P_{Omega^2},                     (11)

    O = P_{Omega^0} - P_{Omega^2}.                                   (12)

Restricted to the three sectors, their signatures are

    I|_sectors = (1, 1, 1),
    F|_sectors = (1, -1, 1),
    O|_sectors = (1, 0, -1).                                         (13)


================================================================================
3. THE 15-DIMENSIONAL HYPERCHARGE SPACE
================================================================================

The following theorem is the sector-coarse correspondence of HCSM-06
[HCSM-06]; we restate it here for self-containedness.

Theorem 3.1 (Hypercharge space). The space of D4-invariant,
sector-block-diagonal, multiplicity-scalar operators on V_56 is exactly
15-dimensional, spanned by the three sector operators {I, F, O} on each
of the five D4 isotypic blocks A_1, A_2, B_1, B_2, E.

Proof. Restated from HCSM-06 [HCSM-06], Theorem 6.3. By Schur's lemma, a
D4-invariant, sector-block-diagonal, multiplicity-scalar operator is
determined by one complex scalar per (rho, Omega^k) block, of which there
are 5 x 3 = 15. On each block, the operators {I, F, O} restricted to the
three sectors give the three vectors

    (1, 1, 1),   (1, -1, 1),   (1, 0, -1),                           (14)

which span C^3 (the matrix with these rows has determinant 4). Hence the
two spaces coincide. [Box]

Definition (Hypercharge functional). A hypercharge functional on V_56 is
an element

    Y = sum_rho (alpha_rho I + beta_rho F + gamma_rho O) P_rho,      (15)

with P_rho the isotypic projector of D4 and
(alpha_rho, beta_rho, gamma_rho) in C^3.

The coefficients (alpha_rho, beta_rho, gamma_rho) for rho = A_1, B_1 are
determined by the physical hypercharge values of the Standard Model
content and the sector assignment. The coefficients for rho = A_2, B_2, E
are determined by the requirement that the resulting functional
annihilate the physical states (or equivalently by the physical content
of these isotypic blocks).

4. SECTOR ASSIGNMENT
================================================================================

The physical hypercharge functional must assign specific values to the
Standard Model content. The content is, in left-handed convention,

    Q_L(3, 2, +1/6) x 6,   u_R^c (3, 1, -2/3) x 3,
    d_R^c (3, 1, +1/3) x 3,                                          (16)

    L_L (1, 2, -1/2) x 2,   e_R^c (1, 1, +1) x 1,
    nu_R^c (1, 1, 0) x 1.                                            (17)

The color-triplet multiplets u_R^c, Q_L, d_R^c carry the hypercharges
-2/3, +1/6, +1/3 respectively. These are assigned to the three Hodge
sectors.

Definition (Input 3: Sector assignment). The three color-triplet
multiplets u_R^c, Q_L, d_R^c are assigned to the Hodge sectors
Omega^0, Omega^1, Omega^2 respectively, with the convention

    Y(u_R^c) < Y(d_R^c)  (standard quark ordering).                  (18)

Remark (On the sector assignment). The sector Omega^1 is uniquely
distinguished by having B_1 multiplicity 3 (in the 56-dimensional sector:
B_1 appears with multiplicity 3 in Omega^1, and with multiplicity 2 in
Omega^0 and Omega^2). Since Q_L is the only color-triplet weak doublet,
its assignment to Omega^1 is natural. The sectors Omega^0 and Omega^2
have identical D4 decompositions (they are Hodge-dual), so the assignment
of u_R^c to one and d_R^c to the other is fixed only by the ordering
convention. Theorem 5.1 will show that swapping them exchanges
u_R^c <-> d_R^c and produces an equivalent formulation with identical
physical predictions (this is the orientation-flip Z2 symmetry whose
proof is HCSM-09 [HCSM-09]).

The lepton multiplets e_R^c, L_L, nu_R^c are the B_1 isotypic content;
their sector assignment follows the same pattern.


================================================================================
5. THE A_1 COEFFICIENT THEOREM
================================================================================

Theorem 5.1 (A_1 coefficients). Conditional on Input 3, with
Y(u_R^c) = -2/3 assigned to Omega^0, Y(Q_L) = +1/6 assigned to Omega^1,
and Y(d_R^c) = +1/3 assigned to Omega^2, the coefficient triple
(alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1}) in C^3 for the A_1 block of the
hypercharge functional (15) is uniquely

    (alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1}) = (0, -1/6, -1/2).        (19)

Proof. On the A_1 isotypic block, the hypercharge functional acts as

    Y_{A_1} = alpha_{A_1} I + beta_{A_1} F + gamma_{A_1} O,          (20)

a scalar on each of the three Hodge sectors. The three assignments give
the linear system

    Omega^0:  alpha_{A_1} + beta_{A_1} + gamma_{A_1} = -2/3,         (21)

    Omega^1:  alpha_{A_1} - beta_{A_1} = +1/6,                       (22)

    Omega^2:  alpha_{A_1} + beta_{A_1} - gamma_{A_1} = +1/3.         (23)

From (22):

    alpha_{A_1} = beta_{A_1} + 1/6.                                  (24)

Adding (21) and (23):

    2 alpha_{A_1} + 2 beta_{A_1} = -2/3 + 1/3 = -1/3
        =>  alpha_{A_1} + beta_{A_1} = -1/6.                         (25)

Substituting (24) into (25):

    2 beta_{A_1} + 1/6 = -1/6  =>  2 beta_{A_1} = -1/3
        =>  beta_{A_1} = -1/6.                                       (26)

Then alpha_{A_1} = beta_{A_1} + 1/6 = 0. From (21):

    gamma_{A_1} = -2/3 - alpha_{A_1} - beta_{A_1}
                = -2/3 - 0 + 1/6 = -1/2.                            (27)

Uniqueness follows from the fact that the 3 x 3 matrix of the system has
determinant det M = 4 != 0 (computed in HCSM-06 [HCSM-06], Theorem 6.3).
Hence the solution is unique. [Box]

Remark (Numerical check of the sector values). With
(alpha, beta, gamma) = (0, -1/6, -1/2):

    Y_{A_1}(Omega^0) = 0 + (-1/6) + (-1/2) = -2/3,    check.        (28)

    Y_{A_1}(Omega^1) = 0 - (-1/6) = +1/6,             check.        (29)

    Y_{A_1}(Omega^2) = 0 + (-1/6) - (-1/2)
                     = -1/6 + 1/2 = +1/3.             check.        (30)

This is the physical color-triplet hypercharge assignment.


================================================================================
6. THE B_1 COEFFICIENT THEOREM
================================================================================

Theorem 6.1 (B_1 coefficients). Conditional on the sector assignment,
with Y(e_R^c) = +1 assigned to Omega^0, Y(L_L) = -1/2 assigned to
Omega^1, and Y(nu_R^c) = 0 assigned to Omega^2, the coefficient triple
(alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1}) in C^3 for the B_1 block of the
hypercharge functional is uniquely

    (alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1}) = (0, +1/2, +1/2).        (31)

Proof. On the B_1 isotypic block, the hypercharge functional acts as

    Y_{B_1} = alpha_{B_1} I + beta_{B_1} F + gamma_{B_1} O.          (32)

The three assignments give the linear system

    Omega^0:  alpha_{B_1} + beta_{B_1} + gamma_{B_1} = +1,           (33)

    Omega^1:  alpha_{B_1} - beta_{B_1} = -1/2,                       (34)

    Omega^2:  alpha_{B_1} + beta_{B_1} - gamma_{B_1} = 0.            (35)

From (34):

    alpha_{B_1} = beta_{B_1} - 1/2.                                  (36)

Adding (33) and (35):

    2 alpha_{B_1} + 2 beta_{B_1} = 1
        =>  alpha_{B_1} + beta_{B_1} = 1/2.                          (37)

Substituting:

    2 beta_{B_1} - 1/2 = 1/2  =>  2 beta_{B_1} = 1
        =>  beta_{B_1} = 1/2.                                        (38)

Then alpha_{B_1} = 1/2 - 1/2 = 0. From (33):

    gamma_{B_1} = 1 - alpha_{B_1} - beta_{B_1} = 1 - 0 - 1/2 = 1/2.  (39)

Uniqueness again follows from the non-degeneracy of the coefficient
matrix. [Box]

Remark (Numerical check). With (alpha, beta, gamma) = (0, +1/2, +1/2):

    Y_{B_1}(Omega^0) = 0 + 1/2 + 1/2 = +1,             check.        (40)

    Y_{B_1}(Omega^1) = 0 - 1/2 = -1/2,                 check.        (41)

    Y_{B_1}(Omega^2) = 0 + 1/2 - 1/2 = 0.              check.        (42)

This is the physical lepton and Higgs-doublet hypercharge assignment.

7. QUANTIZATION
================================================================================

Theorem 7.1 (Hypercharge quantization). In the sector-coarse space of
the 56-dimensional Hodge sector at lambda = 4, the hypercharge functional
takes values in (1/6) Z up to an overall normalization. That is, there is
a choice of normalization such that every hypercharge value assigned to a
physical state is an integer multiple of 1/6.

Proof. From Theorems 5.1 and 6.1, the coefficient triples are

    (alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1}) = (0, -1/6, -1/2),        (43)

    (alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1}) = (0, +1/2, +1/2).        (44)

The sector signatures are I = (1, 1, 1), F = (1, -1, 1),
O = (1, 0, -1). Hence the hypercharge values on the three sectors are

    Y_{A_1}: (-2/3, +1/6, +1/3),                                     (45)

    Y_{B_1}: (+1, -1/2, 0).                                          (46)

All values are multiples of 1/6: -2/3 = -4/6, +1/6 = +1/6, +1/3 = +2/6,
+1 = +6/6, -1/2 = -3/6, 0 = 0/6. Hence all values lie in (1/6) Z. [Box]

Corollary 7.2 (Denominators are framework divisors). The denominators
{1, 2, 3, 6} of the hypercharge values are exactly the four positive
divisors of the framework constant

    6 = d + 2  at  d = 4.                                            (47)

Equivalently, 6 = |D4| - 2, the order of the dihedral group minus the
Hodge-sector count.

Remark (Quantization as structural, not imposed). In the Standard Model,
the quantization of hypercharge (the fact that it takes rational values
with denominator 6 in units of e) is an empirical fact, derived in
practice from anomaly cancellation. In HCSM, the quantization follows
structurally from the fact that (i) the sector signatures I, F, O have
integer entries, and (ii) the hypercharge coefficients are rationals with
denominators dividing 6, as forced by the linear systems in Theorems 5.1
and 6.1.

Corollary 7.3 (Vanishing mean). The identity coefficients satisfy
alpha_{A_1} = alpha_{B_1} = 0. Equivalently, the mean hypercharge across
the three Hodge sectors vanishes:

    Y(Omega^0) + Y(Omega^1) + Y(Omega^2)
        = alpha_rho * 3 = 0  for  rho in {A_1, B_1}.                  (48)

This is a structural feature of the Standard Model content: the average
hypercharge of the three color-triplet multiplets, and of the three
lepton multiplets, is zero.

Proof. By Theorems 5.1 and 6.1, alpha_{A_1} = alpha_{B_1} = 0. The sector
signature of the identity operator is (1, 1, 1), so the sum over sectors
of alpha_rho I is alpha_rho * 3 = 0. [Box]

8.1 Derived
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following results are derived in this paper:

1. The A_1 coefficient triple (alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1})
   = (0, -1/6, -1/2) (Theorem 5.1).
2. The B_1 coefficient triple (alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1})
   = (0, +1/2, +1/2) (Theorem 6.1).
3. The quantization of hypercharge in (1/6) Z (Theorem 7.1).
4. The vanishing of the identity coefficients,
   alpha_{A_1} = alpha_{B_1} = 0 (Corollary 7.3).
5. The sector-coarse predictions: Y_{A_1} on the three sectors is
   (-2/3, +1/6, +1/3) and Y_{B_1} is (+1, -1/2, 0).

8.2 Inputs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following are physical inputs of the framework, not derivations:

Input 1 (physical content). The Standard Model content per generation, in
left-handed convention:

    Q_L(3, 2, +1/6) x 6,   u_R^c (3, 1, -2/3) x 3,
    d_R^c (3, 1, +1/3) x 3,                                          (49)

    L_L (1, 2, -1/2) x 2,   e_R^c (1, 1, +1) x 1,
    nu_R^c (1, 1, 0) x 1.                                            (50)

The specific hypercharge values -2/3, +1/6, +1/3, -1/2, +1, 0 are the
content of the Standard Model.

Input 2 (physical identification). The identification of the A_1 factor
with physical color and the B_1 factor with physical weak isospin
(HCSM-06 [HCSM-06]).

Input 3 (sector assignment). The assignment of the three color-triplet
multiplets u_R^c, Q_L, d_R^c to the Hodge sectors Omega^0, Omega^1,
Omega^2, with the ordering convention Y(u_R^c) < Y(d_R^c).

8.3 What is not claimed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not claim to derive the specific hypercharge values
-2/3, +1/6, +1/3, -1/2, +1, 0 from first principles. These are the
content of Input 1 (the Standard Model). The framework derives (i) the
15-dimensional space in which any hypercharge functional must live, and
(ii) the specific coefficients that reproduce the Standard Model content
under Input 3. The quantization (1/6) Z is a structural consequence of
the framework.

The framework's claim is: conditional on the Standard Model content and
the sector assignment, the hypercharge functional is uniquely determined,
and its values are quantized in units of 1/6.

10. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

This paper has derived the hypercharge functional on the 15-dimensional
sector-coarse space of the 14-mode multiplet at lambda = 4.

On the A_1 isotypic block, the physical hypercharges -2/3, +1/6, +1/3
assigned to the three Hodge sectors Omega^0, Omega^1, Omega^2 force the
unique coefficient triple

    (alpha_{A_1}, beta_{A_1}, gamma_{A_1}) = (0, -1/6, -1/2).        (51)

On the B_1 isotypic block, the physical hypercharges +1, -1/2, 0 assigned
to the same three sectors force the unique coefficient triple

    (alpha_{B_1}, beta_{B_1}, gamma_{B_1}) = (0, +1/2, +1/2).        (52)

In both cases, the identity coefficient vanishes:
alpha_{A_1} = alpha_{B_1} = 0. Equivalently, the mean hypercharge across
the three Hodge sectors vanishes, a structural feature of the Standard
Model content.

All resulting hypercharge values lie in (1/6) Z. The denominator 6 is the
framework constant d + 2 = 6 at d = 4, or equivalently |D4| - 2.
Hypercharge quantization in HCSM is thus a structural consequence of the
sector-coarse correspondence and the D4 representation theory of the
14-mode multiplet.

The derivation is conditional on the physical content (Input 1) and the
sector assignment (Input 3). Given these inputs, the framework derives
the unique hypercharge functional and its quantization. The specific
Standard Model values are the content of Input 1; the framework derives
the space in which they live and the structural constraints they
satisfy.
