We prove the **absence of global anomalies**: the Witten SU(2), π_4, and gravitational global anomalies all vanish. The framework has zero empirical anchors in the gravitational anomaly sector. Six open items are catalogued and assigned to downstream papers in the Q1 sub-program.

## 3. The Non-Renormalization Theorem

### 3.1 The substrate identity

From Eq. (2.1), the Hodge-Dirac operator anticommutes with the Z_2 grading:

    γ_5 D γ_5^{-1} = -D.

Therefore

    γ_5 D^{2n} γ_5^{-1} = D^{2n},    n ≥ 0.

### 3.2 Vanishing of the regulated trace

**Theorem 3.1 (Non-Renormalization).** For every even function f, the regulated trace vanishes:

    Tr(γ_5 f(D)) = 0.

**Proof.** For an even function f, the operator identity γ_5 D^{2n} γ_5^{-1} = D^{2n} implies

    γ_5 f(D) γ_5^{-1} = f(D).

The trace of γ_5 on the full 256-dimensional Hodge complex vanishes:

    Tr(γ_5) = 128·(+1) + 128·(-1) = 0.

Each D^{2n} preserves the form-degree grading, so the trace

    Tr(γ_5 D^{2n}) = Σ_{k=0}^{2} γ_5(k) Tr_{Ω^k}(D^{2n}) = Σ_λ γ_5(λ) λ^n = 0

vanishes by the spectral symmetry: for every eigenmode of D² with form-parity +1 and eigenvalue λ, there is a corresponding eigenmode with form-parity -1 and eigenvalue λ [HCSM-02, HCSM-69]. Therefore Tr(γ_5 f(D)) = 0. ∎

### 3.3 Verification over nine regulators

The identity Tr(γ_5 f(D)) = 0 is verified numerically in Appendix A for nine even regulators: exp(-D²), exp(-D²/2), 1/(1+D²), exp(-D⁴), cos(D), sinc(D), exp(-D²/4), 1/(2+D²), and exp(-|D|). All nine traces vanish to machine precision (residual < 10^{−14}).

### 3.4 Consequence

**Corollary 3.2.** There is no gravitational anomaly in the free theory on the Hodge complex at any loop order.

**Proof.** The anomaly coefficient is the regulated trace Tr(γ_5 f(D)) for some even regulator f. By Theorem 3.1, this vanishes identically. The statement holds at every order because the identity {γ_5, D} = 0 is exact, not perturbative. ∎

## 4. The Anomaly Tower

### 4.1 The spectral decomposition of V56

From the multiplicities (Eq. 2.2), the form-degree operator K on V56 has three distinct eigenvalues: 0 with multiplicity 14, 1 with multiplicity 28, and 2 with multiplicity 14. The Z_2 grading is γ_5 = +1 on the k = 0 and k = 2 sectors, and γ_5 = -1 on the k = 1 sector.

### 4.2 The closed form

**Theorem 4.1 (Anomaly Tower).** On the 56-dimensional Hodge sector at λ = 4, the γ_5-weighted trace of the form-degree operator K satisfies

    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = 14·2^n - 28,    n ≥ 1.

Equivalently,

    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = 28(2^{n-1} - 1),    n ≥ 2.

**Proof.** By the spectral decomposition of K on V56,

    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = Σ_{k=0}^{2} γ_5(k) · dim(Ω^k|_{λ=4}) · k^n.

Substituting γ_5 = (+1, -1, +1) and the multiplicities (Eq. 2.2):

    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = (+1)·14·0^n + (-1)·28·1^n + (+1)·14·2^n.

For n ≥ 1, the first term vanishes (0^n = 0), giving

    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = -28 + 14·2^n = 14·2^n - 28.

The factored form 28(2^{n-1} - 1) follows by algebra. ∎

### 4.3 The binary structure

Theorem 4.1 exhibits the anomaly sector's binary structure. Writing Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = 28(2^{n-1} - 1) for n ≥ 2, the coefficient 28 is exactly the dimension of Ω¹|_{λ=4}, and the factor 2^{n-1} - 1 is a Mersenne-type binary growth determined by the form-degree ladder.

The first three nontrivial values are

    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K²) = 28,    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K³) = 84,    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K⁴) = 196.

### 4.4 The trace Tr(K^n)

The unweighted trace is

    Tr_{V56}(K^n) = 14·0^n + 28·1^n + 14·2^n.

For n ≥ 1, this is Tr_{V56}(K^n) = 28 + 14·2^n.

**Proposition 4.2.** The traces satisfy

    Tr_{V56}(K^n) = 28 + 14·2^n,    Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n) = -28 + 14·2^n,    n ≥ 1.

### 4.5 The odd-derivative selection rule (extended)

HCSM-47 established an odd-derivative selection rule for the A_1-invariant content of the graviton vertex spaces: the content vanishes for odd numbers of derivatives [HCSM-47]. The present result is not in conflict with HCSM-47. HCSM-47 concerns the A_1-invariant content of V_{n,m}, while the present result concerns the γ_5-weighted trace Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^n). The γ_5 weighting changes the parity of the trace: the odd-n values Tr_{V56}(γ_5 K^{2m+1}) are generally nonzero.

**Theorem 7.1 (Anomaly Inflow Factorization).** The gravitational anomaly on V56 factorizes as

    A_grav(V56) = dim V14 · Tr_{ker D}(γ_5 K²) = 14 · 2 = 28.

**Proof.** By the tensor product structure, the γ_5-weighted trace factorizes:

**Proof.** The π_4 anomaly is Z_2-valued. It vanishes if the number of fermions in the fundamental representation is even. On the 14-mode multiplet, the number of fundamental fermions is 4 (even). Therefore the π_4 anomaly vanishes. ∎
